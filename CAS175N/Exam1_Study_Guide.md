### A. 修辞学与说服的基础  
### A. Foundations of Rhetoric & Persuasion
- **Rhetoric（修辞 / 说服术）**：在特定情境下，通过**符号（语言、图像、行动等）**有目的地影响受众判断与行动的艺术。其本质是**针对受众与场景的有效沟通**（不同于"空话/堆词藻"）。详见 [[Rhetoric_Basics]]。  
  **Rhetoric**: The art of purposefully influencing audience judgment and action through **symbols (language, images, actions, etc.)** in specific situations. Its essence is **effective communication targeted at audience and context** (different from "empty talk/wordiness"). See [[Rhetoric_Basics]] for details.
- **Rhetorical Situation（修辞情境）**：Is a Situation that calls for Rhetoric. *Exigence*（急迫问题/动因）＋*Audience*（可被影响并能行动的受众）＋*Constraints*（制约与资源：规范、时间、媒介、先验观念等）。  
  **Rhetorical Situation**: *Exigence* (urgent problem/motive) + *Audience* (influenceable and action-capable audience) + *Constraints* (limitations and resources: norms, time, medium, prior beliefs, etc.).
- **Ethos / Pathos / Logos**：可信度（身份、品格、专业）/ 情感动员 / 理性论证（证据、推理、数据）。成熟说服往往**三者合奏**。  
  **Ethos / Pathos / Logos**: Credibility (identity, character, expertise) / emotional mobilization / rational argumentation (evidence, reasoning, data). Mature persuasion often involves **all three working together**.
- **Kairos（良机 / 机宜）**：把握**恰当的时机、场合与方式**。同样的表达在不同时点与空间效果天差地别。  
  **Kairos**: Seizing the **appropriate timing, occasion, and manner**. The same expression can have vastly different effects at different times and spaces.
- **Decorum（分寸 / 合宜）**：风格、语气、行为与**受众期待和情境规范**的贴合度。维护 *ethos* 的关键。**有时可策略性破格**（见下）。  
  **Decorum**: The fit between style, tone, behavior and **audience expectations and contextual norms**. Key to maintaining *ethos*. **Sometimes can be strategically violated** (see below).
- **Enthymeme（省略前提的论证）**：把某些"公认前提/常识"留给受众补全的论证，贴近日常传播的实际运作。  
  **Enthymeme**: Arguments that leave certain "accepted premises/common sense" for the audience to complete, reflecting how everyday communication actually works.

### B. 宣传与注意力经济  
### B. Propaganda & Attention Economy
- **Propaganda（宣传）**：在不对等的信息环境中，**持续地塑造议程与认知框架**以推动某种集体立场或行动的传播实践。可分**白/灰/黑**（透明度与真实性不同）。  
  **Propaganda**: Communication practices that **continuously shape agenda and cognitive frameworks** in unequal information environments to promote certain collective positions or actions. Can be categorized as **white/gray/black** (differing in transparency and authenticity).
- **Framing（框架化）**：选择性突出某些角度/因果/责任归属，从而改变意义与判断。  
  **Framing**: Selectively highlighting certain perspectives/causality/responsibility attribution to change meaning and judgment.
- **Agenda Setting（议程设置）**：媒介决定"**谈什么**"比"怎么想"更优先——**显著性**改变公众注意力分配。  
  **Agenda Setting**: Media determining "**what to talk about**" is more important than "how to think"—**salience** changes public attention allocation.
- **Priming（启动效应）**：前置信息改变后续判断的标准或阈值。  
  **Priming**: Prior information changes standards or thresholds for subsequent judgments.
- **Attention Economy（注意力经济）**：注意力是稀缺资源，平台算法**偏好情绪强、极端、离散化的内容**，易导致两极化与"浅速化"。详见 [[Attention_Economy]]。  
  **Attention Economy**: Attention is a scarce resource; platform algorithms **prefer emotionally intense, extreme, fragmented content**, easily leading to polarization and "shallow-speed" trends. See [[Attention_Economy]] for details.

### C. 真假、谎言与"扯淡"  
### C. Truth, Lies & "Bullshit"
- **Lying（说谎）**：**明知为假**而陈述为真，以误导受众为目的。仍以"真值"为参照。  
  **Lying**: **Knowingly false** statements presented as true to mislead audiences. Still references "truth value".
- **Bullshitting（扯淡，哈里·法兰克福语境）**：对真伪**根本不关心**，只求达到某种印象或效果。其危害在于**侵蚀"真"的重要性**。  
  **Bullshitting** (Harry Frankfurt's context): **Completely unconcerned** with truth/falsity, only seeking certain impressions or effects. Its harm lies in **eroding the importance of "truth"**.
- **Misinformation / Disinformation / Malinformation**：无意误导 / 有意误导 / 真实信息的恶意语境化（例如选择性泄露）。  
  **Misinformation / Disinformation / Malinformation**: Unintentional misleading / intentional misleading / malicious contextualization of true information (e.g., selective leaks).
- **Zengotita 的"**Numb**（麻木）"**：托马斯·德·曾戈蒂塔（*Mediated*）提出，**高度媒介化**让世界像"舞台"，一切成为面向自我与他人的表演，导致**感受迟钝、共情耗竭、需要更强刺激才有感觉**。  
  **Zengotita's "Numb"**: Thomas de Zengotita (*Mediated*) proposes that **highly mediated** world makes everything like a "stage," all becomes performance for self and others, leading to **dulled feelings, empathy exhaustion, requiring stronger stimuli for sensation**.

### D. 常见谬误与偏差（高频考点速记）  
### D. Common Fallacies & Biases (High-Frequency Exam Points)
- **Ad Hominem**（人身攻击）、**Straw Man**（稻草人）、**False Dilemma**（二分法）、**Slippery Slope**（滑坡）、**Post hoc**（先后即因果）、**Ad Populum**（诉诸多数）、**Tu quoque**（你也一样）、**Red Herring**（转移话题）、**Equivocation**（一词多义偷换）。  
  **Ad Hominem** (personal attack), **Straw Man** (strawman), **False Dilemma** (false dichotomy), **Slippery Slope**, **Post hoc** (correlation ≠ causation), **Ad Populum** (appeal to majority), **Tu quoque** (you too), **Red Herring** (topic diversion), **Equivocation** (word ambiguity exploitation).
- **确认偏误、可得性启发、锚定效应、内群体偏好**等。  
  **Confirmation bias, availability heuristic, anchoring effect, in-group preference**, etc.

### 1) **"谎言 vs 扯淡（bullshitting）哪个更糟？"并解释原因。再判断注意力经济更助长哪一种？**  
### 1) **"Which is worse: lying vs bullshitting?" Explain why. Then determine which the attention economy more promotes?**

**标准答案要点**  
**Standard Answer Points**
1. **定义对照**  
   **Definition Comparison**
   - *Lying*：以真伪为参照、**明知其假仍声称为真**以误导。  
     *Lying*: References truth/falsity, **knowingly claiming false as true** to mislead.
   - *Bullshitting*（法兰克福）：**无视真伪**，以"效果/印象管理"为唯一目标。  
     *Bullshitting* (Frankfurt): **Ignores truth/falsity**, takes "effect/impression management" as sole goal.
2. **为何扯淡常被认为更糟**（可引法兰克福）  
   **Why bullshitting is often considered worse** (can cite Frankfurt)
   - 说谎者仍承认"真"的规范，只是违反；扯淡者**取消了真值的重要性**，长期看**更破坏公共理性与知识生态**。  
     Liars still acknowledge "truth" norms, just violate them; bullshitters **eliminate the importance of truth value**, long-term **more destructive to public rationality and knowledge ecology**.
   - 使公共讨论从"求真"转为"求爽/求立场/求流量"，提升**犬儒主义与相对主义**。  
     Transforms public discussion from "seeking truth" to "seeking satisfaction/position/traffic," increasing **cynicism and relativism**.
3. **注意力经济的倾向**  
   **Attention Economy Tendencies**
   - 平台优化指标（点击率、停留、转发）偏好**情绪化、夸张、戏剧化**，因此**更容易滋养 bullshitting**而非传统说谎（后者需事实编造成本）。  
     Platform optimization metrics (click rate, dwell time, shares) prefer **emotional, exaggerated, dramatic** content, thus **more likely to foster bullshitting** than traditional lying (which requires fact fabrication costs).
4. **例证**：短视频"情绪爆点"、标题党、断章取义的励志/养生"鸡汤"、梗化政治。  
   **Examples**: Short video "emotional climaxes," clickbait, out-of-context inspirational/health "chicken soup," meme-ified politics.
5. **反思**：并非否认说谎的严重性（法律/伦理后果），而是指出**系统性侵蚀"真"的意义**的危害。  
   **Reflection**: Not denying lying's seriousness (legal/ethical consequences), but pointing out the harm of **systematically eroding the meaning of "truth"**.

**评分点（Rubric）**：给出**清晰定义**（2 分）＋**法兰克福视角**（3 分）＋**平台机制分析**（3 分）＋**例子**（1 分）＋**小结**（1 分）。  
**Scoring Points (Rubric)**: Clear **definition** (2 points) + **Frankfurt perspective** (3 points) + **platform mechanism analysis** (3 points) + **examples** (1 point) + **summary** (1 point).

**词语解析**：*Lying*、*Bullshitting*、*Attention Economy*、*Impression Management*（印象管理）。  
**Term Analysis**: *Lying*, *Bullshitting*, *Attention Economy*, *Impression Management*.

### 2) **什么是 *Decorum*（分寸/合宜）？为何重要？违背会怎样？何时可有策略地"破格"？**  
### 2) **What is *Decorum*? Why important? What happens when violated? When can it be strategically "broken"?**

**标准答案要点**  
**Standard Answer Points**
1. **定义**：表达者在**风格、语气、礼仪、着装、媒介选择**上与**受众期待和场合规范**的贴合度，是 *ethos* 的重要来源。  
   **Definition**: The communicator's fit between **style, tone, etiquette, dress, medium choice** and **audience expectations and contextual norms**, an important source of *ethos*.
2. **重要性**：提升**可信度与可亲性**，降低抵触；保证**注意力与合作意愿**。  
   **Importance**: Enhances **credibility and approachability**, reduces resistance; ensures **attention and cooperation willingness**.
3. **违背后果**：受众反感、**失去面子/权威/信任**，议题被"礼仪失败"盖过。  
   **Consequences of violation**: Audience rejection, **loss of face/authority/trust**, issues overshadowed by "etiquette failure".
4. **何时可策略破格**：当**规范本身阻碍正义或注意力稀缺**时，**适度的越界**可制造"修辞震撼"（kairos）以**重置议程/引发讨论**。  
   **When can strategically break decorum**: When **norms themselves hinder justice or attention is scarce**, **moderate boundary crossing** can create "rhetorical shock" (kairos) to **reset agenda/trigger discussion**.
   - **例**：民权运动静坐、环保行动的"视觉冲击"、企业危机时 CEO 亲自短视频道歉（降格以示真诚）。  
     **Examples**: Civil rights sit-ins, environmental actions' "visual impact," CEO personal video apology during corporate crisis (demeaning oneself to show sincerity).

**评分点**：准确定义（3）＋重要性（3）＋后果（2）＋策略破格＋例子（2）。  
**Scoring Points**: Accurate definition (3) + importance (3) + consequences (2) + strategic violation + examples (2).
**词语解析**：*Decorum*、*Ethos*、*Kairos*、*Norms*（规范）。  
**Term Analysis**: *Decorum*, *Ethos*, *Kairos*, *Norms*.

### 3) **Zengotita 所说的"麻木（numb）"是什么意思？举例。如何说服"麻木"的受众？**  
### 3) **What does Zengotita mean by "numb"? Give examples. How to persuade "numb" audiences?**

**标准答案要点**  
**Standard Answer Points**
1. **核心观点**：在高度媒介化世界，我们**持续作为"观众/表演者"**消费与呈现现实，久而久之需要**更强刺激**才有感觉，**共情阈值提高**。  
   **Core argument**: In highly mediated world, we **continuously act as "audience/performer"** consuming and presenting reality, over time requiring **stronger stimuli** to feel, **empathy threshold increases**.
2. **例子**：灾难轮播让同情心疲劳；刷短视频对"极端标题"逐渐免疫；公益照片不断升级冲击性。  
   **Examples**: Disaster news cycles cause compassion fatigue; scrolling short videos gradually immune to "extreme headlines"; charity photos constantly escalate shock value.
3. **应对策略**：  
   **Response Strategies**:
   - **叙事具象化**：用**具体人物/场景/细节**替代宏大统计（以 *pathos* 直达）。  
     **Narrative concretization**: Use **specific people/scenes/details** instead of grand statistics (direct *pathos* appeal).
   - **参与式与行动线索**：给**可立即执行的小行动**（捐 1 美元、报名志愿、转发给 1 人），把"观看"转为"参与"。  
     **Participatory and action cues**: Provide **immediately executable small actions** (donate $1, volunteer signup, forward to 1 person), transform "viewing" into "participation".
   - **新奇性与*kairos***：形式更新（互动地图、沉浸式体验）、选择**恰当时机**。  
     **Novelty and *kairos***: Format innovation (interactive maps, immersive experience), choose **appropriate timing**.
   - **可信背书（ethos）＋微证据（logos）**：少而精的**可核查数据**与**透明来源**。  
     **Credible endorsement (ethos) + micro evidence (logos)**: Few but refined **verifiable data** and **transparent sources**.

**评分点**：准确转述理论（4）＋有效策略三条（各 2 分）＋例子（2）。  
**Scoring Points**: Accurate theory restatement (4) + three effective strategies (2 each) + examples (2).
**词语解析**：*Mediated*、*Desensitization*（脱敏/麻木） 、*Compassion Fatigue*（同情心疲劳）。  
**Term Analysis**: *Mediated*, *Desensitization*, *Compassion Fatigue*.
