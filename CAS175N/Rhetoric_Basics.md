# 修辞学基础 / Rhetoric Basics

## 修辞情境 / Rhetorical Situation

修辞情境是指需要修辞的情况。  
Is a Situation that calls for Rhetoric.

## 核心概念 / Core Concepts

### 1. Decorum（得体）

在修辞学中，decorum指言语或行为与场合、身份、受众等相适应，表现出恰当、合宜。  
In rhetoric, decorum means that speech or behavior is appropriate and fitting to the context, status, and audience.

### 2. Commonplace（常见论题）

commonplace指的是听众普遍认同的观点，是常识或公众舆论的表达。  
Commonplace is a viewpoint that an audience shares; an expression of common sense or public opinion.

### 3. Inductive（归纳法）

inductive指通过观察具体实例，总结出一般性结论的方法。  
Inductive means drawing general conclusions from specific observations.

### 4. Deductive（演绎法）

deductive是inductive的反义词，指从一般原理推导出具体结论的方法。  
Deductive, the opposite of inductive, means deriving specific conclusions from general principles.
