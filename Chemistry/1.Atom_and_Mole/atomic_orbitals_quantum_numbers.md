# 原子轨道与量子数 / Atomic Orbitals and Quantum Numbers

## 1. 从波尔模型到轨道模型 / From Bohr Model to Orbital Model

在上一讲中，我们看到波尔模型使用行星模型来描述电子，其中电子"围绕"原子核"轨道运行"。我们看到电子存在于量子化轨道中，这些轨道由主量子数"n"顺序编号。我们将在接下来的讲座中看到，这个模型只适用于描述单电子原子（即H、He⁺、Li²⁺等）。这是因为电子实际上并不存在于轨道中；相反，它们存在于由轨道描述的三维空间区域中。

In the last lecture we saw that the Bohr model describes electrons using a planetary model in which they "orbit" the nucleus. We saw that electrons exist in quantized orbits which are sequentially numbered by the principal quantum number "n". We'll see in the upcoming lecture that this model only works for describing single electron atoms (i.e., H, He⁺, Li²⁺, etc.). This is because electrons don't actually exist in orbits; instead, they are found in three-dimensional regions of space described by orbitals.

轨道，就像波尔模型描述的"轨道"一样，具有与之相关的量子化能级。波尔模型中的"轨道"由单一量子数n定义。然而，每个轨道都由一组三个量子数定义：n、ℓ和mₗ。

Orbitals, like the "orbits" described by the Bohr model, have quantized energy levels associated with them. The "orbits" in the Bohr model were defined by a single quantum number, n. Each orbital, however, is defined by a set of three quantum numbers: n, ℓ, and mₗ.

## 2. 主量子数 (n) / Principal Quantum Number (n)

再一次，n是主量子数。就像以前一样，它的值从1开始，以整数步长增加（1、2、3等）。n量子数决定轨道所属的电子壳层。随着壳层数的增加，轨道变得更大，电子离原子核更远。

Once again n is the principal quantum number. Just like before, its values begin with 1 and increase in integer steps (1, 2, 3, etc.). The n quantum number determines the shell to which the orbital belongs. As the shell number increases the orbitals become larger, with electrons residing further from the nucleus.

## 3. 角动量量子数 (ℓ) / Angular Momentum Quantum Number (ℓ)

角动量量子数ℓ决定轨道的形状。ℓ的值范围从0到n-1。例如，在n=3壳层内，可能有ℓ量子数为0、1和2的轨道。ℓ=3在n=3壳层中是不允许的，因为ℓ值3大于n-1（3-1=2）。

The angular momentum quantum number, ℓ, determines the shape of the orbital. The values of ℓ range from 0 to n-1. For example, within the n = 3 shell it is possible to have orbitals with ℓ quantum numbers of 0, 1, and 2. ℓ = 3 would not be allowed in the n = 3 shell since an ℓ value of 3 is larger than n-1 (3-1=2).

### 轨道类型符号 / Orbital Type Symbols

不同的ℓ值有与之相关的特定符号：
- ℓ = 0：s轨道
- ℓ = 1：p轨道  
- ℓ = 2：d轨道
- ℓ = 3：f轨道

Different values of ℓ have specific symbols associated with them:
- ℓ = 0: s orbitals
- ℓ = 1: p orbitals
- ℓ = 2: d orbitals
- ℓ = 3: f orbitals

ℓ量子数决定轨道所属的亚壳层。例如，具有n=2和ℓ=0量子数的轨道将属于"2s亚壳层"，而具有n=3和ℓ=1量子数的轨道将属于"3p亚壳层"。

The ℓ quantum number determines the subshell to which the orbital belongs. For example, an orbital with n = 2 and ℓ = 0 quantum numbers would belong to the "2s subshell", and an orbital with n = 3 and ℓ = 1 quantum numbers would belong to the "3p subshell".

## 4. 磁量子数 (mₗ) / Magnetic Quantum Number (mₗ)

定义轨道所需的最后一个量子数是磁量子数mₗ，它描述轨道的空间取向。mₗ的值范围从-ℓ到+ℓ。例如，在2p亚壳层（n=2，ℓ=1）内，mₗ可能是-1、0或+1。

The final quantum number needed to define an orbital is the magnetic quantum number, mₗ, which describes the spatial orientation of the orbital. The values of mₗ range from -ℓ to +ℓ. For example, within the 2p subshell (n = 2, ℓ = 1), mₗ may be -1, 0, or +1.
### 磁量子数详细解释 / Detailed Explanation of Magnetic Quantum Number

磁量子数$m_\ell$的物理意义在于它决定了同一亚壳层内轨道在空间中的不同取向。对于每一个给定的$\ell$值，$m_\ell$的取值范围为$-ℓ, -(ℓ-1), ..., 0, ..., +(ℓ-1), +ℓ$，共$2\ell+1$个可能值。

The physical meaning of the magnetic quantum number $m_\ell$ is that it determines the different spatial orientations of orbitals within the same subshell. For each given value of $\ell$, $m_\ell$ ranges from $-ℓ, -(ℓ-1), ..., 0, ..., +(ℓ-1), +ℓ$, for a total of $2\ell+1$ possible values.

#### 例子 / Example

- 若$\ell=1$（即p轨道），则$m_\ell$可为$-1, 0, +1$，对应三个不同方向的p轨道（通常记为$p_x, p_y, p_z$）。
- 若$\ell=2$（即d轨道），则$m_\ell$可为$-2, -1, 0, +1, +2$，对应五种不同空间取向的d轨道。

- If $\ell=1$ (i.e., p orbital), then $m_\ell$ can be $-1, 0, +1$, corresponding to three differently oriented p orbitals (commonly denoted as $p_x, p_y, p_z$).
- If $\ell=2$ (i.e., d orbital), then $m_\ell$ can be $-2, -1, 0, +1, +2$, corresponding to five different spatial orientations of d orbitals.

#### d轨道的具体形式（z², x²–y², xy, xz, yz）解释 / Explanation of Specific d Orbitals (z², x²–y², xy, xz, yz)

在$\ell=2$（d轨道）时，$m_\ell$有五个取值，对应五种不同空间取向的d轨道，常用以下符号表示：

- $d_{z^2}$：主要分布在$z$轴方向，形状像“哑铃+甜甜圈”，数学表达式为$z^2$相关。
- $d_{x^2-y^2}$：分布在$x$轴和$y$轴方向，形状像四叶草，数学表达式为$x^2-y^2$相关。
- $d_{xy}$：分布在$x$轴和$y$轴之间的平面上，形状像四叶草，数学表达式为$xy$相关。
- $d_{xz}$：分布在$x$轴和$z$轴之间的平面上，数学表达式为$xz$相关。
- $d_{yz}$：分布在$y$轴和$z$轴之间的平面上，数学表达式为$yz$相关。

For $\ell=2$ (d orbitals), there are five possible $m_\ell$ values, corresponding to five spatial orientations, commonly denoted as:

- $d_{z^2}$: Mainly distributed along the $z$-axis, shaped like a "dumbbell plus donut", mathematically related to $z^2$.
- $d_{x^2-y^2}$: Distributed along the $x$ and $y$ axes, cloverleaf-shaped, related to $x^2-y^2$.
- $d_{xy}$: Distributed in the plane between $x$ and $y$ axes, cloverleaf-shaped, related to $xy$.
- $d_{xz}$: Distributed in the plane between $x$ and $z$ axes, related to $xz$.
- $d_{yz}$: Distributed in the plane between $y$ and $z$ axes, related to $yz$.

这些符号反映了轨道的空间分布和数学表达式，便于区分不同d轨道的方向和形状。

These symbols reflect the spatial distribution and mathematical expressions of the orbitals, making it easier to distinguish the direction and shape of different d orbitals.

### 简并（Degenerate）解释 / Explanation of Degenerate

**简并（degenerate）**是指具有相同能量的不同量子态或轨道。  
**Degenerate** refers to different quantum states or orbitals that have the same energy.

在氢原子中，同一主量子数$n$下，不同的$\ell$和$m_\ell$取值对应的轨道能量完全相同，这些轨道被称为“能级简并”。  
In the hydrogen atom, for the same principal quantum number $n$, orbitals with different $\ell$ and $m_\ell$ values have exactly the same energy. These orbitals are said to be "degenerate in energy."

例如，$n=2$时，2s和2p轨道在氢原子中能量相同，都是简并态。  
For example, when $n=2$, the 2s and 2p orbitals in the hydrogen atom have the same energy and are degenerate states.

但在多电子原子中，由于电子间的相互作用，简并性会被打破，不同$\ell$的轨道能量会有所不同。  
However, in multi-electron atoms, due to electron-electron interactions, degeneracy is lifted and orbitals with different $\ell$ values have different energies.
### 零电子密度区（节点）解释 / Explanation of Regions of Zero Electron Density (Nodes)

**零电子密度区（节点，node）**是指在原子轨道中，电子出现概率为零的空间区域。  
**Regions of zero electron density (nodes)** refer to spatial regions in atomic orbitals where the probability of finding an electron is exactly zero.

节点分为两类：径向节点和角节点。  
There are two types of nodes: radial nodes and angular nodes.

- **径向节点（Radial nodes）**：出现在距离原子核某一特定半径处，电子云密度为零。径向节点数 = $n - \ell - 1$。  
  **Radial nodes** occur at certain distances from the nucleus where the electron density is zero. Number of radial nodes = $n - \ell - 1$.
- **角节点（Angular nodes）**：由轨道的角度分布决定，通常表现为空间中的平面或锥面。角节点数 = $\ell$。  
  **Angular nodes** are determined by the angular distribution of the orbital, usually appearing as planes or cones in space. Number of angular nodes = $\ell$.

**总节点数 = $n-1$，其中$n$为主量子数。**  
**Total number of nodes = $n-1$, where $n$ is the principal quantum number.**

#### 例子 / Example

- 2s轨道：$n=2, \ell=0$，有$1$个径向节点，无角节点。  
  2s orbital: $n=2, \ell=0$, has 1 radial node, no angular nodes.
- 2p轨道：$n=2, \ell=1$，无径向节点，有$1$个角节点（平面）。  
  2p orbital: $n=2, \ell=1$, has no radial nodes, 1 angular node (plane).

节点的存在决定了轨道的形状和电子分布特征，是量子力学描述原子结构的重要内容。  
The existence of nodes determines the shape and electron distribution of orbitals, which is a key concept in the quantum mechanical description of atomic structure.
