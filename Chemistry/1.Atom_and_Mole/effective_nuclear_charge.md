# 有效核电荷 / Effective Nuclear Charge

## 定义 / Definition

有效核电荷（$Z_\mathrm{eff}$）是指原子中最外层电子实际感受到的正电荷，考虑了屏蔽效应。  
Effective nuclear charge ($Z_\mathrm{eff}$) is the net positive charge experienced by valence electrons, taking into account the shielding effect.

## 公式 / Formula

$$
Z_\mathrm{eff} = Z - S
$$

其中$Z$为核电荷数，$S$为屏蔽常数。  
Where $Z$ is the atomic number and $S$ is the shielding constant.

## 1. 随周期表的变化 / Variation Across the Periodic Table

### 1.1 随周期的变化 / Variation Across a Period

- 从左到右，有效核电荷逐渐增加，因为核电荷（质子数）增加，而屏蔽效应变化不大。  
  From left to right across a period, $Z_\mathrm{eff}$ increases because the number of protons increases while shielding does not increase significantly.
- 结果：最外层电子被吸引得更紧，原子半径减小。  
  As a result, valence electrons are held more tightly and atomic radius decreases.

### 1.2 随族的变化 / Variation Down a Group

- 从上到下，有效核电荷增加较慢，因为虽然核电荷增加，但内层电子的屏蔽效应也增强。  
  Down a group, $Z_\mathrm{eff}$ increases slowly because both nuclear charge and shielding increase.
- 结果：最外层电子距离原子核更远，原子半径增大。  
  As a result, valence electrons are farther from the nucleus and atomic radius increases.
