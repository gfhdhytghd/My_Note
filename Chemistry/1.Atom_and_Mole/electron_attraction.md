# 6. 电子间吸引力 / Electron Attraction

> 本笔记整理自 [[Daily Notes/2025-09-03|2025-09-03 日记]]

## 库仑定律 / Coulomb's Law

电子间吸引力遵循库仑定律，描述带电粒子之间的相互作用力。

Electron attraction follows Coulomb's law, which describes the interaction force between charged particles.

$$F = k \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$$

其中 / Where:
- $F$ = 力的大小 / Magnitude of force
- $k$ = 库仑常数 / Coulomb constant
- $q_1, q_2$ = 电荷量 / Electric charges
- $r$ = 距离 / Distance

## 电子-原子核吸引力 / Electron-Nucleus Attraction

### 基本原理 / Basic Principles

原子核带正电，电子带负电，它们之间存在静电吸引力。这种吸引力使电子围绕原子核运动。

The nucleus is positively charged and electrons are negatively charged, creating an electrostatic attraction between them. This attraction keeps electrons orbiting around the nucleus.

### 影响因素 / Influencing Factors

1. **核电荷数 / Nuclear Charge**
   - 质子数越多，吸引力越强 / More protons = stronger attraction
   - 影响电子轨道半径 / Affects electron orbital radius

2. **距离 / Distance**
   - 距离越近，吸引力越强 / Closer distance = stronger attraction
   - 遵循平方反比关系 / Follows inverse square relationship

## 电子-电子排斥力 / Electron-Electron Repulsion

### 同种电荷排斥 / Like Charge Repulsion

电子之间由于都带负电而相互排斥，这种排斥力影响原子的电子排布。

Electrons repel each other due to their negative charges, affecting the electron configuration of atoms.

### 屏蔽效应 / Shielding Effect

内层电子对外层电子的屏蔽作用，减少原子核对外层电子的有效吸引力。

Inner electrons shield outer electrons, reducing the effective nuclear attraction on outer electrons.

$$Z_{eff} = Z - S$$

其中 / Where:
- $Z_{eff}$ = 有效核电荷 / Effective nuclear charge
- $Z$ = 原子序数 / Atomic number
- $S$ = 屏蔽常数 / Shielding constant

## 电子亲和能 / Electron Affinity

### 定义 / Definition

气态原子获得一个电子形成负离子时释放的能量。

The energy released when a gaseous atom gains an electron to form a negative ion.

### 影响因素 / Influencing Factors

1. **原子半径 / Atomic Radius**
   - 半径越小，电子亲和能越大 / Smaller radius = higher electron affinity

2. **核电荷 / Nuclear Charge**
   - 核电荷越大，电子亲和能越大 / Higher nuclear charge = higher electron affinity

3. **电子构型 / Electron Configuration**
   - 半满和全满轨道较稳定 / Half-filled and fully-filled orbitals are more stable

## 电离能 / Ionization Energy

### 定义 / Definition

气态原子失去一个电子形成正离子所需的最小能量。

The minimum energy required to remove an electron from a gaseous atom to form a positive ion.

### 趋势 / Trends

1. **周期趋势 / Periodic Trend**
   - 从左到右增加 / Increases from left to right
   - 核电荷增加，电子束缚更紧 / Increasing nuclear charge holds electrons tighter

2. **族趋势 / Group Trend**
   - 从上到下减少 / Decreases from top to bottom
   - 原子半径增加，电子束缚减弱 / Increasing atomic radius weakens electron binding
