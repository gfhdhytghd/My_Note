# 熵、吉布斯自由能与自发性 / Entropy, Gibbs Free Energy, and Spontaneity

## 1. 反应熵变与反应焓变 / Reaction Entropy and Enthalpy

反应的标准熵变与标准焓变都可用“生成物求和减去反应物求和”的形式表示（$\nu$为化学计量系数）：  
Standard reaction entropy and enthalpy use products-sum minus reactants-sum (with $\nu$ stoichiometric coefficients):

$$
\Delta S^\circ=\sum \nu S^\circ_{prod}-\sum \nu S^\circ_{react}
$$

$$
\Delta H^\circ=\sum \nu\Delta H_f^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum \nu\Delta H_f^\circ(\text{react})
$$

它们都是状态函数，与路径无关。  
Both are state functions (path independent).

## 2. 自发性与\Delta G / Spontaneity and \Delta G

吉布斯自由能：  
Gibbs free energy:

$$
\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S
$$

- 焓驱动：当$\Delta H\ll0$时，即使$\Delta S<0$也可能自发。  
  Enthalpy-driven: large negative $\Delta H$ can overcome negative $\Delta S$.
- 熵驱动：当$\Delta S\gg0$时，高温下$T\Delta S$项变大，吸热反应也可能自发。  
  Entropy-driven: large positive $\Delta S$ can make endothermic reactions spontaneous at sufficiently high $T$.

## 3. 估算与联系 / Estimation and Connections

- $\Delta H$常由量热实验或赫斯定律叠加求得。  
  $\Delta H$ often comes from calorimetry or Hess’s law.
- $\Delta S$可由标准摩尔熵求和，或由可逆过程$\Delta S=\int q_{rev}/T$得到。  
  $\Delta S$ can be computed from tabulated $S^\circ$ values, or via $\Delta S=\int q_{rev}/T$ for reversible paths.

## 4. 相变与温度效应 / Phase Changes and Temperature Effects

相变中熵变与潜热相关：  
During phase changes, entropy change relates to latent heat:

$$
\Delta S_{phase}=\Delta H_{phase}/T_{trans}
$$

温度越高，$T\Delta S$对$\Delta G$的影响越大，因此同一反应在不同温度下自发性可能改变。  
As temperature rises, $T\Delta S$ weighs more in $\Delta G$, so spontaneity can change with temperature.
