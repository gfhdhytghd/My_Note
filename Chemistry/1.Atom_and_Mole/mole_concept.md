# 5. 摩尔概念 / The Concept of Mole

## 摩尔（mol）的概念 / The Concept of Mole (mol)

摩尔（mol）是物质的量的基本单位。1 mol 表示含有 $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ 个微粒（如原子、分子、离子等），这个数叫做阿伏伽德罗常数。

Mole (mol) is the basic unit for the amount of substance. 1 mol means $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ particles (such as atoms, molecules, ions, etc.), which is called Avogadro's number.

### 阿伏伽德罗常数 / Avogadro's Number

$$N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ mol}^{-1}$$

### 实例 / Examples

- 1 mol 水分子 = $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ 个水分子  
  1 mol of water molecules = $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ water molecules

- 1 mol 氢原子 = $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ 个氢原子  
  1 mol of hydrogen atoms = $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ hydrogen atoms

## 摩尔质量 / Molar Mass

### 定义 / Definition

摩尔质量是1 mol 物质的质量，单位：g/mol。数值等于相对分子质量。

Molar mass is the mass of 1 mol of substance, unit: g/mol. Equal to relative molecular mass.

### 计算方法 / Calculation Method

**单原子物质** / Monatomic substances:
- 摩尔质量 = 原子量（g/mol）
- Molar mass = atomic weight (g/mol)

**分子物质** / Molecular substances:
- 摩尔质量 = 分子式中各原子摩尔质量之和
- Molar mass = sum of atomic molar masses in molecular formula

### 例子 / Examples

- **水 H₂O**：摩尔质量 = 2×1.01 + 16.00 = 18.02 g/mol  
  Water H₂O: Molar mass = 2×1.01 + 16.00 = 18.02 g/mol

- **二氧化碳 CO₂**：摩尔质量 = 12.01 + 2×16.00 = 44.01 g/mol  
  Carbon dioxide CO₂: Molar mass = 12.01 + 2×16.00 = 44.01 g/mol

## 摩尔的计算公式 / Mole Calculation Formulas

### 基本关系式 / Basic Relationships

1. **质量与摩尔数** / Mass and Moles:
   $$n = \frac{m}{M}$$
   - n = 摩尔数 (mol) / moles (mol)
   - m = 质量 (g) / mass (g)  
   - M = 摩尔质量 (g/mol) / molar mass (g/mol)

2. **微粒数与摩尔数** / Number of particles and Moles:
   $$N = n \times N_A$$
   - N = 微粒数 / number of particles
   - n = 摩尔数 (mol) / moles (mol)
   - $N_A$ = 阿伏伽德罗常数 / Avogadro's number

3. **质量、摩尔数、微粒数关系** / Mass, Moles, and Particles:
   $$m = n \times M = \frac{N \times M}{N_A}$$

## 摩尔的用途 / Uses of Mole

### 1. 化学计量学 / Stoichiometry
摩尔用于计算化学反应中各物质的量的关系。

Mole is used to calculate the relationship between amounts of substances in chemical reactions.

### 2. 浓度计算 / Concentration Calculations
- **摩尔浓度** / Molarity (M): mol/L
- **质量百分比浓度** / Mass percentage concentration

### 3. 气体计算 / Gas Calculations
- 标准状况下，1 mol 气体体积约为 22.4 L
- At STP, 1 mol of gas occupies approximately 22.4 L

## 实际计算例题 / Practice Problems

### 例题1 / Example 1
计算18 g水中含有多少个水分子？

Calculate how many water molecules are in 18 g of water?

**解答 / Solution:**
1. 水的摩尔质量 = 18.02 g/mol
2. 摩尔数 = 18 g ÷ 18.02 g/mol = 0.999 mol ≈ 1 mol
3. 分子数 = 1 mol × $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ = $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ 个分子

### 例题2 / Example 2
2 mol CO₂的质量是多少？

What is the mass of 2 mol CO₂?

**解答 / Solution:**
1. CO₂的摩尔质量 = 44.01 g/mol
2. 质量 = 2 mol × 44.01 g/mol = 88.02 g
