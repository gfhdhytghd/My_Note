# 反应焓与生成焓 / Reaction Enthalpy and Enthalpy of Formation

## 1. 反应焓 / Reaction Enthalpy

在恒压条件下，反应焓变等于体系在恒压下的热量交换：$\Delta H_{rxn}=q_p$。  
At constant pressure, the reaction enthalpy change equals the heat exchanged: $\Delta H_{rxn}=q_p$.

符号约定：放热$\Delta H_{rxn}<0$，吸热$\Delta H_{rxn}>0$。  
Sign convention: exothermic $\Delta H_{rxn}<0$, endothermic $\Delta H_{rxn}>0$.

## 2. 标准生成焓 / Standard Enthalpy of Formation

标准生成焓$\Delta H_f^\circ$定义为：在标准态下由元素的标准态生成$1mol$该化合物的焓变；元素处于标准态时$\Delta H_f^\circ=0$。  
Standard enthalpy of formation $\Delta H_f^\circ$ is the enthalpy change to form 1 mol of a compound from elements in their standard states; elements in their standard states have $\Delta H_f^\circ=0$.

## 3. 由生成焓计算反应焓 / Compute Reaction Enthalpy from Formation Enthalpies

$$
\Delta H_{rxn}^\circ=\sum \nu\,\Delta H_f^\circ(\text{products})-\sum \nu\,\Delta H_f^\circ(\text{reactants})
$$

其中$\nu$为化学计量系数；注意是“生成物求和减去反应物求和”。  
Here $\nu$ are stoichiometric coefficients; note the products-sum minus reactants-sum structure.

## 4. 方向与倍数 / Direction and Scaling

- 反应式整体乘以$n$，则$\Delta H_{rxn}$也乘以$n$。  
  If the balanced reaction is multiplied by $n$, then $\Delta H_{rxn}$ is multiplied by $n$.
- 反应逆转，$\Delta H_{rxn}$符号翻转。  
  If the reaction is reversed, the sign of $\Delta H_{rxn}$ flips.

这些性质体现焓是状态函数，路径无关。  
These properties reflect enthalpy as a state function (path independent).

## 5. 赫斯定律 / Hess’s Law

当无法直接测得某反应的$\Delta H_{rxn}$时，可将目标反应拆分为若干“已知焓变”的子反应相加，总焓变等于各步焓变之和。  
When direct measurement is unavailable, decompose the target reaction into steps with known enthalpy changes; the total equals the sum of step enthalpies.
