# 光谱基础 / Spectrum Basics

## 1. 光谱的分布（从左到右）/ Spectrum Distribution (From Left to Right)

- 光谱按波长（或频率）从左到右依次为：  
  The spectrum, arranged from left to right by wavelength (or frequency), is as follows:

| 区域         | 波长范围（约）         | 说明 / Description                |
|--------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 伽马射线     | $< 0.01$ nm           | 高能，核反应相关 / High energy, nuclear reactions |
| X射线        | $0.01$ nm – $10$ nm   | 医学成像 / Medical imaging        |
| 紫外线       | $10$ nm – $400$ nm    | 日光、杀菌 / Sunlight, sterilization |
| 可见光       | $400$ nm – $700$ nm   | 人眼可见 / Visible to human eye   |
| 红外线       | $700$ nm – $1$ mm     | 热辐射 / Thermal radiation        |
| 微波         | $1$ mm – $1$ m        | 通信、雷达 / Communication, radar |
| 无线电波     | $> 1$ m               | 广播、电视 / Broadcasting, TV     |

- 从左到右，波长逐渐变长，频率逐渐降低。  
  From left to right, the wavelength increases and the frequency decreases.

## 2. 频率与能量的关系 / Relationship between Frequency and Energy

- 波的能量与频率成正比，公式如下：  
  The energy of a wave is directly proportional to its frequency, as shown below:

$$
E = h \nu
$$

- 其中 $E$ 为能量，$h$ 为普朗克常数，$\nu$ 为频率。  
  Where $E$ is energy, $h$ is Planck's constant, and $\nu$ is frequency.
- 普朗克常数的值为 $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34}\ \mathrm{J \cdot s}$。  
  The value of Planck's constant is $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34}\ \mathrm{J \cdot s}$。

## 3. 吸收光谱与发射光谱 / Absorption and Emission Spectra

- 吸收和发射光谱的谱线位置相同，均对应于电子在不同能级之间跃迁所需或释放的能量（即特定波长）。  
  The positions of the lines in absorption and emission spectra are the same, both corresponding to the energy required or released when electrons transition between different energy levels (i.e., specific wavelengths).

$$
E_n = \frac{-1R_y}{n^2}
$$

- $E_n$ 表示氢原子中第 $n$ 能级的能量，$R_y$ 为里德伯常数，$n$ 为主量子数。  
  $E_n$ represents the energy of the $n$-th level in a hydrogen atom, $Ry$ is the Rydberg constant, and $n$ is the principal quantum number.

## 4. 相关概念 / Related Concepts

**能级跃迁 / Energy Level Transitions：**  
电子在不同能级间跃迁时吸收或释放特定频率的光子。  
Electrons absorb or emit photons of specific frequencies when transitioning between energy levels.

**光谱分析 / Spectral Analysis：**  
通过分析物质的光谱特征，可以确定其组成和结构。  
By analyzing the spectral characteristics of matter, its composition and structure can be determined.
