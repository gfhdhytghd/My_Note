# 体积庞大基团效应 / Bulky Group Effects

**定义 / Definition**  
体积庞大基团（bulky group）指具有较大范德华体积的取代基，其空间占位造成显著位阻效应（steric hindrance）。  
Bulky groups are substituents with large van der Waals volumes whose spatial demand causes significant steric hindrance.

**对键角与几何的影响 / Effects on Bond Angles and Geometry**  
- 大取代基为减小非键相互排斥，常使相邻键角增大或占据更少拥挤的位置（如三角双锥中优先占赤道位）。  
  Bulky substituents often increase adjacent bond angles or occupy less crowded positions (e.g., prefer equatorial sites in trigonal bipyramids) to reduce nonbonded repulsion.
- 在环己烷中，大基团优先取平伏位（equatorial）以降低1,3-轴向相互作用。  
  In cyclohexane, large groups prefer the equatorial position to minimize 1,3-diaxial interactions.

**对反应性的影响 / Effects on Reactivity**  
- SN2 反应对底物拥挤高度敏感：体积庞大基团显著降低进攻路径的可达性，速率下降。  
  SN2 reactions are highly sensitive to steric congestion: bulky groups hinder the backside attack and lower rates.
- 体积庞大碱参与的 E2 消除更易给出霍夫曼产物（较少取代烯烃），因取代基位阻控制。  
  E2 with bulky bases often favors Hofmann products (less substituted alkenes) due to steric control.

**要点 / Key Takeaways**  
- 位阻效应常主导构象与局部几何的选择，优先"少拥挤"排布。  
  Steric effects often dominate conformational and local geometric preferences toward less crowded arrangements.
- 大基团可增大键角、改变位点优先级，并在动力学上抑制靠近反应位点的路径。  
  Bulky groups can enlarge bond angles, alter site preference, and kinetically impede approach to reaction centers.
