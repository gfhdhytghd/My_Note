# 分子极性判断 / Molecular Polarity

## 1. 极性定义 / Definition of Polarity

- 如果一个分子存在净电荷分离（分子一端偏负、另一端偏正），它就是极性分子  
  A molecule is polar if it has net charge separation, with a negative end and a positive end
- 该性质可用分子偶极矩描述：$\vec{\mu}_\text{net} \ne 0$  
  The property is described by the molecular dipole moment: $\vec{\mu}_\text{net} \ne 0$

## 2. 成为极性分子的条件 / Conditions for Polarity

1) 必须含有极性键（电负性差异较大的共价键）  
   Must contain polar bonds (covalent bonds with significant electronegativity difference)  
2) 分子几何不能使所有键偶极完全相互抵消  
   Molecular geometry must not cause complete cancellation of all bond dipoles

## 3. 判断步骤（适用于多原子分子） / Decision Steps (for polyatomic molecules)

1) 画出Lewis结构  
   Draw the Lewis structure → [[lewis_structures]]  
2) 用VSEPR理论预测分子几何  
   Predict molecular geometry using VSEPR → [[VSEPR_theory]]  
3) 在结构上标出每条键偶极的方向（从低电负性指向高电负性）  
   Mark each bond dipole direction (toward the more electronegative atom)  
4) 判断偶极矢量是否相互抵消：能完全抵消→非极性；不能→极性  
   Check vector cancellation: complete cancellation → nonpolar; otherwise → polar

## 4. 示例：水分子 H₂O / Example: Water H₂O

- O–H 键为极性键；水分子为弯曲形（非直线）  
  O–H bonds are polar; water is bent (nonlinear)  
- 两个 O–H 键偶极无法完全抵消，$\vec{\mu}_\text{net}$ 指向氧一侧 → 水为极性分子  
  The two O–H bond dipoles do not cancel; $\vec{\mu}_\text{net}$ points toward oxygen → water is polar

## 5. 提示与常见误区 / Tips and Pitfalls

- 仅有极性键不足以保证分子极性：如 CO₂ 线形对称，两个 C=O 偶极相互抵消 → 非极性  
  Having polar bonds alone is not sufficient: CO₂ is linear and symmetric; two C=O dipoles cancel → nonpolar
- 高对称分子常为非极性：BF₃（三角平面）、CCl₄（四面体对称）偶极可完全抵消  
  Highly symmetric molecules are often nonpolar: BF₃ (trigonal planar), CCl₄ (tetrahedral) have complete cancellation
- 孤对电子常改变几何并产生净偶极：NH₃（三角锥）有 $\vec{\mu}_\text{net} \ne 0$；相比之下 CH₄ 为非极性  
  Lone pairs alter geometry and often yield net dipole: NH₃ (trigonal pyramidal) has $\vec{\mu}_\text{net} \ne 0$; CH₄ is nonpolar
