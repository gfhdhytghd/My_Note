# 轨道杂化与σ/π键 / Orbital Hybridization and σ/π Bonds

轨道杂化是原子轨道线性组合形成更适合分子几何的杂化轨道；与σ/π成键共同决定分子结构与性质。 / Hybridization linearly combines atomic orbitals into hybrids suited to molecular geometry; together with σ/π bonding, it determines structure and properties.

## 常见类型 / Common Types

- sp：直线，180°；例：C₂H₂ / Linear, 180°; ex: C₂H₂
- sp²：三角平面，120°；例：C₂H₄ / Trigonal planar, 120°; ex: C₂H₄
- sp³：四面体，109.5°；例：CH₄ / Tetrahedral, 109.5°; ex: CH₄

## σ键与π键 / Sigma and Pi Bonds

- σ：头碰头重叠，电子密度在键轴上；单键必为σ。 / Head-on overlap; density along bond axis; all single bonds are σ.
- π：侧向重叠，电子密度在键轴上下或两侧；与σ并存于双/三键。 / Side-on overlap; density above/below or lateral to axis; accompanies σ in double/triple bonds.
