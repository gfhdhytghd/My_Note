# 3. 常见官能团与性质 / Common Functional Groups and Properties

- **烷烃 (C–C, C–H) / Alkanes:** 非极性、惰性、自由基取代/加成需强条件。/ Nonpolar, relatively inert; radical substitution/addition under harsh conditions.
- **烯烃 (C=C) / Alkenes:** π键亲电加成、Markovnikov/反Markovnikov可控。/ Electrophilic addition; Markovnikov vs anti-Markovnikov control.
- **炔烃 (C≡C) / Alkynes:** 末端炔酸性pKa≈25，可成碳负离子。/ Terminal alkynes acidic (pKa≈25), form acetylides.
- **芳香 (Ar) / Arenes:** 亲电芳香取代（EAS），定位取代由取代基导向。/ Electrophilic aromatic substitution; directing effects control regiochemistry.
- **卤代烃 (R–X) / Haloalkanes:** SN1/SN2、E1/E2与底物结构、碱/溶剂相关。/ SN1/SN2 and E1/E2 depend on substrate, base, solvent.
- **醇 (R–OH) / Alcohols:** 氢键、酸性弱、可转衍生为卤化物、酯。/ Hydrogen bonding; weakly acidic; derivatized to halides, esters.
- **酚 (Ar–OH) / Phenols:** 共振稳定更酸；易发生EAS。/ More acidic via resonance; undergo EAS readily.
- **醚 (R–O–R) / Ethers:** 惰性、良好溶剂；强酸下可裂解。/ Inert, good solvents; cleave under strong acids.
- **环氧 / Epoxides:** 应力驱动开环，亲核在二级位或更取代位。/ Strain-driven ring-opening; nucleophile attacks more substituted carbon under acid.
- **胺 (R–NH2/R2NH/R3N) / Amines:** 碱性、亲核性强；季铵盐可形成。/ Basic, nucleophilic; form quaternary ammonium salts.
- **硝基 (R–NO2) / Nitro:** 强吸电子，降低相邻位电子密度。/ Strongly electron-withdrawing; deactivates adjacent positions.
- **腈 (R–C≡N) / Nitriles:** 极性强，可水解为酸或还原为胺。/ Polar; hydrolyze to acids or reduce to amines.
- **硫醇/硫醚 (R–SH/R–S–R) / Thiols/Thioethers:** 亲核性强、气味重、易氧化。/ Strong nucleophiles, easily oxidized.
- **醛 (R–CHO) / Aldehydes:** 更易被氧化；亲核加成活性高。/ More easily oxidized; high electrophilicity for nucleophilic addition.
- **酮 (R–CO–R) / Ketones:** 亲核加成；相对醛更稳定。/ Undergo nucleophilic addition; less reactive than aldehydes.
- **羧酸 (R–CO2H) / Carboxylic acids:** 酸性显著，酯化/酰氯化常见。/ Notably acidic; esterification and acyl chloride formation.
- **酯 (R–CO2R) / Esters:** 亲核酰基取代；水解/醇解/胺解可逆可控。/ Nucleophilic acyl substitution; hydrolysis/alcoholysis/aminolysis.
- **酸酐 / Anhydrides:** 活性高，易被亲核体进攻生成酯/酰胺。/ Highly reactive; form esters/amides with nucleophiles.
- **酰氯 / Acyl chlorides:** 活性最高，选择性酰化试剂。/ Most reactive acyl derivatives; selective acylating agents.
- **酰胺 (R–CONH2) / Amides:** 共振稳定，最不活泼的酰基衍生物之一。/ Resonance-stabilized; among least reactive acyl derivatives.
