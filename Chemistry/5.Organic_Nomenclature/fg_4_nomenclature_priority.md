# 4. 命名与优先级（简化）/ Nomenclature Priority (Simplified)

- **优先级从高到低 / Highest to lowest (parent suffix):** 羧酸 > 酸酐 > 酰氯 ≈ 酯 > 酰胺 > 腈 > 醛 > 酮 > 醇 > 硫醇 > 胺 > 烯/炔 > 醚 ≈ 卤代 ≈ 硝基 > 烷。/ Carboxylic acid > Anhydride > Acyl chloride ≈ Ester > Amide > Nitrile > Aldehyde > Ketone > Alcohol > Thiol > Amine > Alkene/Alkyne > Ether ≈ Halo ≈ Nitro > Alkane.
- **作为取代基名称 / As substituents:** 羧酸衍生物→酰氧/酰氨基；羰基→oxo/formyl；醇→hydroxy；胺→amino；卤→fluoro/chloro等。/ Derivatives → acyloxy/amide; carbonyl → oxo/formyl; alcohol → hydroxy; amine → amino; halogens → fluoro/chloro, etc.
