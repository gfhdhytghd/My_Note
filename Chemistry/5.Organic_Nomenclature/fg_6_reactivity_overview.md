# 6. 反应性概要 / Reactivity Overview

- **亲电中心 / Electrophiles:** C=O、R–X、环氧、NO2邻位芳环。/ Carbonyls, alkyl halides, epoxides, nitro-activated aryl rings.
- **亲核中心 / Nucleophiles:** 含孤对（OH−、RO−、CN−、NR2−、S−）。/ Lone-pair-bearing species (OH−, RO−, CN−, NR2−, S−).
- **控制因素 / Controls:** 取代效应、位阻、溶剂、酸碱度、温度。/ Substituent effects, sterics, solvent, acidity/basicity, temperature.
