# 表面张力与粘度 / Surface Tension & Viscosity

## 表面张力的能量图像 / Energetic Picture
- 表面张力$\gamma$可理解为界面自由能密度：$\gamma=F/L=\Delta E/\Delta A$，即在液膜上拉出单位长度或增加单位面积所需的额外能量。  Surface tension $\gamma$ behaves as interfacial free-energy density via $\gamma=F/L=\Delta E/\Delta A$, the extra energy needed to pull out unit length or create unit area on a film.
- 肥皂膜实验显示液体总想最小化表面积；当移动框架或刺破薄膜时，液面会自行收紧，直观说明$\gamma$像一股“收缩力”。  Soap-film experiments show liquids striving to minimize area; when the frame moves or the film is punctured, the surface pulls itself tight, illustrating $\gamma$ as a contracting force.

## 温度与分子结构依赖 / Temperature & Molecular Structure
- $d\gamma/dT<0$：升高温度削弱界面分子排列，使$\gamma$下降并在临界点附近逼近 0。  Because $d\gamma/dT<0$, heating disrupts interfacial ordering, lowering $\gamma$ and driving it toward zero near the critical point.
- 强氢键或多重偶极作用（见 [[intermolecular_forces]]）会抬高$\gamma$，解释水滴近乎球形以及金属汞具有异常大的表面张力。  Strong hydrogen bonding or multiple dipoles (see [[intermolecular_forces]]) elevate $\gamma$, explaining nearly spherical water droplets and the exceptionally large surface tension of mercury.
- 实验提示：必须稳控温度并识别表面活性剂，因为它们占据界面、降低$\gamma$并改写液滴轮廓。  Practical note: control temperature and watch for surfactants, which occupy the interface, lower $\gamma$, and reshape droplets.

## 粘度与流动阻力 / Viscosity & Flow Resistance
- 粘度衡量液体对流动的内摩擦，分子越大或分子间作用力越强（尤其是氢键网络），流动越困难。  Viscosity measures internal friction opposing flow; larger molecules or stronger intermolecular forces (notably hydrogen-bond networks) increase resistance.
- 温度上升提高分子动能，帮助克服作用力从而降低粘度，因此加热常被用来“解黏”或加速流动。  Raising temperature boosts kinetic energy, helping molecules overcome attractions and lowering viscosity, so heating is the go-to tactic for thinning fluids.
