# 生物大分子概览 / Biological Macromolecules Overview

## 定义与特征 / Definition & Features

- 生物大分子是由大量原子构成、分子量可达数十万 g/mol 的巨大分子；它们由重复的结构单元构成。  
  Biological macromolecules are very large molecules with molecular weights up to hundreds of thousands g/mol, built from repeating structural units.
- 大分子通常是聚合物：由称为“单体”的化学单元通过共价键反复连接成长链。  
  Macromolecules are typically polymers: chains of chemical units (monomers) covalently linked into long chains.

## 聚合物与单体 / Polymers and Monomers

- 聚合物（polymer）= 由重复的单体（monomer）通过共价键“拼接”而成；连接反应往往是重复的化学过程。  
  Polymers are formed by repetitive covalent couplings of monomers via recurring chemical reactions.
- 单体不必完全相同；例如蛋白质中的氨基酸种类不同，但都按同一“主链连接规则”串联。  
  Monomers need not be identical; e.g., amino acids differ in side chains but connect using the same backbone chemistry in proteins.

## 例子 / Examples

- 蛋白质 / Proteins：单体为氨基酸；线性不分支；任意序列皆可，序列与侧链化学性质决定结构与功能。  
  Proteins: monomers are amino acids; linear and unbranched; any sequence is possible, and side-chain chemistry plus order determine structure and function.
- 核酸 / Nucleic acids（DNA/RNA）：单体为核苷酸；化学性质相似，信息主要由单体连接顺序编码；通常也为不分支的线性聚合物。  
  Nucleic acids (DNA/RNA): monomers are nucleotides; similar chemistry among monomers, with information encoded by order; generally linear and unbranched.

## 结构—性质联系 / Structure–Property Links

- 重复单元与连接方式定义“大分子类别”，进而决定整体物理化学性质。  
  The repeating unit and linkage chemistry define the macromolecule class and its bulk properties.
- 生物大分子的稳定性与功能不仅依赖共价主链，也依赖多种分子间作用力的综合（见 [[Chemistry/6.Intermolecular_Forces/intermolecular_forces]]）。  
  Stability and function depend on both covalent backbones and the ensemble of intermolecular interactions (see [[Chemistry/6.Intermolecular_Forces/intermolecular_forces]]).
