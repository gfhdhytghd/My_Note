# 溶液与溶解度 / Solutions & Solubility

- 溶解与析晶的动态平衡 / Dynamic equilibrium between dissolution and crystallization: 当固体溶质与溶剂接触时，溶解与结晶同时发生；随溶质浓度升高，结晶速率提高，直到两者速率相等达到动态平衡，此时溶液中溶质浓度达到该条件下的最大值，即“溶解度”。 / When a solid solute contacts a solvent, dissolution and crystallization both occur; as solute concentration rises, crystallization rate increases until it equals the dissolution rate, establishing dynamic equilibrium. The solute concentration at this point is the solubility under those conditions.

- 溶解度的定义 / Definition of solubility: 在特定条件下，每一定量溶剂中所能溶解的溶质最大量（以克或摩尔计）。 / The maximum amount of solute (in grams or moles) that can dissolve in a given amount of solvent under specified conditions.

- 影响因素 / Factors affecting solubility: 由溶质-溶剂与溶质-溶质间的分子间作用力决定，并受温度影响（随溶盐与溶剂而增减）；气体溶解度还受压强影响。 / Determined by intermolecular forces between solute–solvent and solute–solute; affected by temperature (may increase or decrease depending on the system); gas solubility is also affected by pressure.

- 饱和度术语 / Saturation terminology: 
  - 不饱和溶液 / Unsaturated: 实际溶解量低于该条件下的溶解度。例：室温下 NaCl 在水中的溶解度约为 36 g/100 g 水，则 30 g NaCl 全部溶解为不饱和。 / Contains less solute than the solubility at those conditions. Example: At room temperature, NaCl solubility in water ≈ 36 g per 100 g water; 30 g dissolves completely, giving an unsaturated solution.
  - 饱和溶液 / Saturated: 已达该条件的最大溶解量。例：向 100 g 水中加 40 g NaCl，仅约 36 g 溶解，剩余 4 g 以固体存在，所得溶液为饱和。 / Contains the maximum amount soluble at those conditions. Example: Add 40 g NaCl to 100 g water at room temperature; ≈36 g dissolves and 4 g remains solid, so the solution is saturated.
  - 过饱和溶液 / Supersaturated: 溶解量暂时超过溶解度，状态不稳定，易析出多余溶质。常由改变温度或压强制得。例：100°C 时 39 g NaCl/100 g 水可溶，若在 100°C 配制饱和溶液并冷却至室温，短暂可得过饱和溶液，随后多余溶质会析出。 / Temporarily contains more solute than the solubility; unstable and excess solute tends to precipitate. Often created by changing temperature or pressure. Example: 39 g NaCl can dissolve in 100 g water at 100°C; preparing a saturated solution at 100°C and cooling to room temperature yields a supersaturated solution until excess solute precipitates.
