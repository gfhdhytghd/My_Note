## 课程概述与目标 / Course Overview and Objectives

本课程以数学严谨性与工程近似为基础，训练学生分析静止机械或结构构件任一点的应力状态，并同时考察强度、刚度与内载荷变形。

The course uses mathematical rigor and engineering approximations to analyze stress at points in stationary mechanical or structural members, including strength, rigidity, and deformation under internal loads.

学习内容包括力系向量与力矩、自由体图、干摩擦、形心与面积矩、平衡、桁架和截面内力，以及应力—应变、轴向载荷、扭转、梁弯曲与剪应力、压力容器、组合载荷、挠度、Mohr 圆、柱屈曲和安全系数。

Topics include force systems and moments, free-body diagrams, dry friction, centroids and area moments, equilibrium, trusses and internal loads, stress–strain, axial loading, torsion, beam bending and shear, pressure vessels, combined loading, deflection, Mohr’s circle, column buckling, and factors of safety.
