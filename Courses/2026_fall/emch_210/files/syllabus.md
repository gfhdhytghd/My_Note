## Course and Assessment

- Instructor: Andrew J. Miller
- Meetings: Monday/Friday 4:40–5:30 PM; Tuesday/Thursday 4:35–5:50 PM, 110 Wartik Lab.
- Four midterms (15% each), comprehensive final (25%), homework (15%; two lowest dropped).
- Midterms: September 23, October 7, November 4, December 2, 2026, 8:00–9:15 PM; location TBA.
- Grade scale: A ≥93; A− 90–92.99; B+ 87–89.99; B 83–86.99; B− 80–82.99; C+ 77–79.99; C 70–76.99; D 60–69.99; F ≤59.99.

## Requirements and Policies

- Textbook: R. C. Hibbeler, *Statics and Mechanics of Materials*, 6th ed.
- Homework is due by 11:59 PM Eastern as one clear PDF and must be completed independently.
- Cite websites, other people, solution manuals, and generative AI when used; uncited use may receive zero credit and trigger academic-integrity procedures.
- Exams permit only a scientific or graphing calculator; phones may be used to scan/upload after the exam.
- Conflict exams require approval at least one week in advance. Cheating, plagiarism, fabrication, unauthorized exam possession, undisclosed resubmission, and tampering are prohibited.
- Accessibility requests go through Student Disability Resources each semester. CAPS, crisis support, disability services, and Report Bias resources are provided.
