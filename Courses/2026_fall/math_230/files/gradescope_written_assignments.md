> Canvas 来源 / Source：<https://psu.instructure.com/courses/2487353/pages/important-all-written-assignments-must-be-submitted-through-gradescope?module_item_id=49060178>
>
> 类型 / Type：Course Information 网页；采集日期 / captured 2026-08-25。

## 平台与账户 / Platform and account

书面作业由 GradeScope 评分；所有学生须先从左侧菜单链接，以 PSU 凭据登录 GradeScope。Written Homework is graded in GradeScope, and every student must first sign in there using PSU credentials.

必须使用与 GradeScope roster 和 Canvas roster 匹配的 PSU 邮箱。Use the PSU email address that matches both the GradeScope and Canvas rosters.

## 提交格式要求 / Submission requirements

1. 所有提交必须为 PDF。Submissions must be PDF.
2. 每道题必须从单独的一页开始；一道题可占多页。Every problem must be on its own page or pages.
3. 同一道题的多个小问可以同页或跨页，但每个小问之间必须空两行。Parts of one problem may share pages, but leave two lines between parts.

不遵守上述要求，作业可能不予评分或丢分。Failure to follow the requirements risks an ungraded submission or loss of points.
