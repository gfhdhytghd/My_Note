# 补考政策 / Makeup Exam Policies

> Canvas 来源 / Source：<https://psu.instructure.com/courses/2487353/pages/important-makeup-exam-policies?module_item_id=49060175>
>
> 类型 / Type：Course Information 网页；采集日期 / captured 2026-08-25。

## 核心规则 / Core rules

- 数学系为无法参加正常期中考试的学生提供补考；补考通常在与正常考试不同的晚上举行。The Mathematics Department offers makeup exams for students unable to take a regularly scheduled midterm, typically on a different evening.
- 每场补考只提供一次，不会再次改期。A makeup examination is offered once and will not be rescheduled.
- 已参加正常考试的学生不得再参加补考；否则记零分，并可能违反学术诚信政策。Students who completed the regular exam may not take the makeup; doing so earns zero and may implicate Academic Integrity.
- 未获批准且正常考试和补考均缺席者，该次考试记零分。Missing both without an approved excuse results in zero.

## 资格与申请 / Eligibility and request

1. 先联系任课教师，说明需要补考及原因。Contact the instructor with the reason for the request.
2. 在课程大纲所列截止日期前提交数学系在线补考申请。Submit the department’s online request by the deadline in the syllabus.
3. 按要求提供支持性证明。Provide supporting documentation when required.

可被考虑的校方认可情形包括课程冲突、其他课程晚间考试、宗教活动冲突及学校活动所需出行；提交证明不保证获批。University-approved examples include official class conflicts, other evening exams, religious observances, and required University-sponsored travel; documentation does not guarantee approval.

对于非校方认可缺席，学期内可有一次无扣分补考；此后即使获准，考试分数可扣 20%。For non-approved absences, one makeup without penalty may be allowed in the semester; additional approved requests may receive a 20% score penalty.

## 截止与结果 / Deadlines and decisions

- 正常考试前一天 23:59 前的申请，会在正常考试开始前审阅。Requests by 11:59 p.m. the day before are reviewed before the regular exam.
- 考试日 00:00 至 18:15 的申请未必能在正常考试开始前获审。Requests from midnight to 6:15 p.m. on exam day may not be reviewed before the exam.
- 审核结果会邮件通知学生和教师：批准（含日期、时间、地点）、待补材料或拒绝。Both student and instructor receive an email: approved, pending documentation, or denied.
- 被拒绝仍可能允许补考，但分数扣 20%。A denied request may still permit the makeup with a 20% penalty.
