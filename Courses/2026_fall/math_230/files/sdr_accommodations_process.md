## 必做事项 / Required actions

获得 Penn State SDR 正式批准安排的学生，须将 accommodation letter 交给教师（纸本或电子版）、与教师讨论安排，并将电子版发送给数学系考试协调员 `mathexams@psu.edu`。Students with approved SDR accommodations must give the instructor the letter, discuss accommodations, and email an electronic copy to `mathexams@psu.edu`.

若考试协调员没有电子版证明，学生不会得到相应安排。Without an electronic copy held by the exam coordinator, appropriate accommodations will not be provided.

## 小测与考试 / Quizzes and exams

- 小测：学生须联系本班教师并商定日期、时间与地点。For quizzes, student and instructor must agree on day, time, and location.
- 考试：学生须提交数学系 Course Information 页面 SDR Student Information 下的 `Extended Time Exam Sign` 申请表。For exams, complete the Extended Time Exam Sign request form from the Math Department Course Information page.
- 同一学期修读多门数学课时，每门课均需单独提交一份表格。One form is required per math course each semester.
