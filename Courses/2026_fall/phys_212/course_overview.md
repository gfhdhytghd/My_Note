## 课程说明与目标 / Description and Objectives

本课是以微积分为基础的经典电磁学导论，涵盖电荷与电场、高斯定律、电势、电容、电流、电阻与电路、磁场与电流产生的场、电磁感应与电感、物质磁性、麦克斯韦方程和电磁振荡。

This is a calculus-based introduction to classical electromagnetism, covering electric charge and fields, Gauss's law, potential, capacitance, circuits, magnetism, induction, Maxwell's equations, and electromagnetic oscillations.

- 通识能力 / General-education skills：有效沟通、批判与分析思维，以及物理素养。 / Effective communication, critical and analytical thinking, and physics literacies.
- 学科掌握 / Physics mastery：电荷、场和力；电势与势能；直流/交流电路；磁场；电磁学与电磁波。 / Charges, fields, and forces; potential and potential energy; DC/AC circuits; magnetic fields; electromagnetism and waves.
