# PHYS 212 Syllabus and Administrative Policies

Source: [Course Syllabus](https://psu.instructure.com/courses/2474831/assignments/syllabus). Markdown organization of the Canvas syllabus.

## Course and Assessment

- Instructor: Prof. Doug Cowen. Course administration: Dr. Hafsa Khurshid.
- Components: two lectures, one recitation, and one laboratory weekly.
- Weights: Homework 5%; Recitations 13%; Laboratories 13%; Quizzes 12%; Exam 1 15%; Exam 2 15%; Final 25%; in-class questions 2%.
- Exam 1: October 1, 2026. Exam 2: November 5, 2026. Both 6:15–7:30 PM; final set by the Registrar.

## Requirements and Policies

- Prerequisites: PHYS 211 and MATH 140; co-requisite: MATH 141.
- Textbook: *University Physics Volume 2* (OpenStax).
- Expert TA is required for homework; Top Hat is used for lecture participation.
- Homework is usually due Tuesday at 11:59 PM Eastern; late work receives no credit.
- Quizzes and exams are closed-book/closed-notes; only the supplied equation sheet, testing-center scratch paper, and approved non-networked calculator are permitted.
- Use PSU email for administration. Do not switch recitation/lab groups without authorization.
- Individually assessed work must be original; prewritten recitation answers and discussion of active exams are prohibited.
- Accessibility and support follow Penn State Student Disability Resources and related university policies.
