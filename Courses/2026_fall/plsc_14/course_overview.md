## 课程说明 / Course Description

课程介绍世界政治与全球经济，涵盖国际关系的主要行为者、概念和理论，以及国家间战争、内战、恐怖主义、国际贸易、金融、外国直接投资与发展。

The course introduces world politics and the global economy, covering major actors, concepts, and theories in international relations as well as interstate war, civil war, terrorism, trade, finance, foreign direct investment, and development.

## 学习目标 / Learning Objectives

- 识别并解释国际关系中的主要行为者、制度、概念与理论。 / Identify and explain the principal actors, institutions, concepts, and theories in international relations.
- 运用“利益—互动—制度”框架与谈判、信息、国内政治及国际制度理论解释冲突与合作。 / Apply the interests–interactions–institutions framework and related theories to conflict and cooperation.
- 分析政治经济力量如何影响贸易、金融、投资、发展与经济胁迫。 / Analyze how political-economic forces shape trade, finance, investment, development, and economic coercion.
- 用课程概念和证据解释当代国际事件，并比较不同解释。 / Use course concepts and evidence to interpret current events and compare competing explanations.
