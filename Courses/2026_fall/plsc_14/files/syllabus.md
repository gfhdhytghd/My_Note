# PLSC 14 Syllabus and Administrative Policies

Source: [Course Syllabus](https://psu.instructure.com/courses/2478621/assignments/syllabus). Markdown organization of the embedded Canvas syllabus.

## Course and Assessment

- Instructor: Prof. Boliang Zhu. Meetings: Tuesday/Thursday 10:35–11:50 AM, 105 Forum Building.
- Midterm I: 22.5%, October 1, 2026. Midterm II: 22.5%, November 5, 2026. Final: 25%, finals week.
- Seven quizzes: 15%, best five counted. Event Report: 15%, due October 30; optional revision due December 4.
- Grade scale: A 93–100; A− 90–92; B+ 87–89; B 83–86; B− 80–82; C+ 77–79; C 70–76; D 60–69; F <60.

## Requirements and Policies

- Complete assigned readings before class and participate in discussion.
- Event Report: maximum three double-spaced numbered pages, font no larger than 12 pt, margins at least one inch; submit through Canvas.
- Textbook: Frieden et al., *World Politics: Interests, Interactions, and Institutions*, 6th ed. (2026).
- No makeup exams or extensions except where permitted by University policy. Late work loses 20% per day and is zero after five days.
- Cheating, plagiarism, fabrication, facilitating misconduct, unauthorized exam possession, unauthorized resubmission, and tampering are prohibited.
- Generative AI is prohibited for exam questions; for papers it is limited to brainstorming, outlines/headings, and grammar improvement and must be listed as “Sourced Tool.”
- Accessibility, extended absences, counseling, and bias reporting follow Penn State policies.
