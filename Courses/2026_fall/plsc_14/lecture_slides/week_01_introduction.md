## 研究对象 / Subject Matter

国际关系也称世界政治，是政治学的一个分支，研究世界各国与人民如何相处。典型问题包括国家为何作战、为何开放或限制贸易，以及为何加入国际组织。 / International relations, also called world politics, is a subfield of political science studying relations among countries and peoples. Typical questions concern why states fight, open or restrict trade, and join international organizations.（Slides 2–3）

研究领域包括安全与冲突、国际政治经济、国际组织以及跨国行为体和全球议题。战争、内战、恐怖主义、贸易、金融、投资、移民、援助、人权和环境都属于研究范围。 / Its domains include security and conflict, international political economy, international organizations, and transnational actors and global issues. Topics include war, civil war, terrorism, trade, finance, investment, migration, aid, human rights, and the environment.（Slides 4–5）
