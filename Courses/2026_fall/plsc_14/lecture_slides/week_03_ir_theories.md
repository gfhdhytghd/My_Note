## 理论的作用 / What a Theory Does

理论是一组逻辑一致的命题，解释某种现象的因果因素以及因素间的联系。它帮助我们描述哪些因素重要，预测因素变化如何改变行为和结果，并据此提出政策建议。三大理论提供不同的解释起点。 / A theory is a logically consistent set of statements explaining causal factors and their connections. It helps describe which factors matter, predict how changes affect behavior and outcomes, and prescribe policy responses. The three grand theories offer different starting points.（Slides 1–3）

## 现实主义 / Realism

### 无政府状态、安全困境与均势 / Anarchy, the Security Dilemma, and Balancing

国家是主要行为体；无政府状态塑造其利益和互动。在没有上级保护者、又不确定他国意图的体系中，国家依赖自助来追求生存、安全与权力。 / States are the dominant actors; anarchy shapes their interests and interactions. Without a superior protector and with uncertain intentions, states rely on self-help to pursue survival, security, and power.（Slide 4）

安全困境：A 国为增强自身安全而扩军，却使其他国家更不安全。课件的因果链为“无政府状态＋不确定性 → 自助 → 权力最大化 → 安全困境”。现实主义因而对长期合作与国际组织的独立作用持怀疑态度；共同敌人可促成合作，但共同威胁消失后，合作基础可能削弱。 / In a security dilemma, A's military improvements for its own security make others less secure. The deck's causal chain is “anarchy + uncertainty → self-help → power maximization → security dilemmas.” Realism is consequently skeptical of lasting cooperation and independent institutional effects; a common enemy can enable cooperation, whose basis may weaken when that threat disappears.（Slide 5）

均势可能抑制战争：如果双方都不预期进攻能取胜，战争的吸引力会下降。内部均势通过建设本国能力实现，外部均势通过联盟聚合力量实现；冷战用来说明“和平但未必和谐”。 / Balancing may deter war when neither side expects victory from attacking. Internal balancing builds a state's own capabilities; external balancing aggregates capabilities through alliances. The Cold War illustrates peace without harmony.（Slide 6）

### 四种现实主义路径 / Four Realist Approaches

| 路径 / Approach | 核心解释 / Central explanation | 课件依据 / Slides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 古典现实主义 / Classical realism | 人性的自利、支配欲与领导者的缺陷推动争权与冲突；政体和领导质量会影响外交。 / Self-interest, domination, and leaders' flaws drive power struggles; regimes and leadership affect foreign policy. | 7–8, 10 |
| 新现实主义 / Neorealism | 国际体系结构与权力分布塑造国家行为，国内差异退居次位；权力是生存工具。 / System structure and power distribution shape state behavior; domestic differences are secondary, and power serves survival. | 7, 9–10 |
| 防御性现实主义 / Defensive realism | 攻防平衡、地理与技术决定扩张的收益；当防御占优、远距离投射力量困难时，维持现状与制衡更合理。 / The offense–defense balance, geography, and technology affect expansion; defense advantages and difficult power projection favor the status quo and balancing. | 7, 11 |
| 进攻性现实主义 / Offensive realism | 意图不确定和进攻能力使大国追求更高相对实力及地区霸权；水域限制力量投射。 / Uncertain intentions and offensive capability push great powers toward relative power and regional hegemony; water constrains power projection. | 7, 12 |

第 9 页以 Waltz（1979）说明两极相较多极更稳定的论点：两大对手较容易互相监测，多极中的危险、责任和核心利益更不清晰。该页也列出“霸权稳定论”，但未进一步展开，不能把它等同于两极稳定论。 / Slide 9 uses Waltz (1979) to present the argument that bipolarity is more stable than multipolarity: two rivals can monitor each other, while dangers, responsibilities, and vital interests are less clear in multipolarity. “Hegemonic Stability Theory” is also listed without elaboration and should not be equated with bipolar stability.

第 11 页引用 Van Evera（1999），第 12 页引用 Mearsheimer（2001）。比较防御与进攻现实主义时，应说明为何同样的无政府状态可能分别导向谨慎制衡或扩张竞争。 / Slide 11 cites Van Evera (1999), and slide 12 cites Mearsheimer (2001). In comparing defensive and offensive realism, explain why the same anarchic condition can lead to cautious balancing or expansionist competition.

## 自由主义 / Liberalism

自由主义把无政府状态视为可应对的困难，重视相互依赖、多种行为体和国内政治。政府利益来自国内群体在政治制度中的互动，而非简单假设国家有统一固定的利益。财富与共同收益使合作成为可能，例如贸易、避免冲突和环境保护。 / Liberalism treats anarchy as a manageable difficulty and emphasizes interdependence, diverse actors, and domestic politics. Government interests emerge from domestic groups interacting within institutions rather than a unitary, fixed national interest. Wealth and shared gains create opportunities for trade, conflict avoidance, and environmental cooperation.（Slides 13–15）

### 新自由制度主义 / Neoliberal Institutionalism

以 Keohane 的 *After Hegemony*（1984）为代表：没有霸权国也可能维持合作。国际制度降低交易成本、提供信息、联结议题、支持互惠、延长“未来的阴影”（重复互动），并解决争端。 / Represented by Keohane's *After Hegemony* (1984), this approach allows cooperation without a hegemon. Institutions reduce transaction costs, provide information, link issues, support reciprocity, extend the shadow of the future through repeated interaction, and resolve disputes.（Slide 16；课件将姓氏拼作 “Keohance”，此处统一为 Keohane / the deck spells the surname “Keohance,” normalized here to Keohane.）

与 [[Courses/2026_fall/plsc_14/lecture_slides/week_01_interests_interactions_institutions/week_01_interests_interactions_institutions]] 对照：制度即使没有世界政府式的执行能力，也能改变合作的激励与信息条件。 / Compare the Week 1 notes: institutions can change incentives and information even without enforcement resembling a world government.

### 商业和平论 / Commercial Peace Theory

贸易、开放市场和资本流动创造财富；与经济伙伴开战会破坏这些收益，从而增加战争的机会成本。受损的国内群体有动力反对战争，跨国企业与金融联系也可能降低经济征服的收益。这里的机制是“相互依赖提高开战代价”，不是“有贸易就绝不战争”。 / Trade, open markets, and capital flows create wealth; war with an economic partner destroys those gains and raises war's opportunity cost. Affected domestic groups have incentives to oppose war, and multinational firms and financial links may reduce gains from conquest. The mechanism is a higher cost of war, not a guarantee that trade prevents all war.（Slide 17）

### 民主和平论 / Democratic Peace Theory

必须区分单体命题与双边命题：前者称民主国家总体更和平，后者称民主国家彼此不交战。二者不是同一句话。课件列出的解释包括领导人问责、更透明的决策程序、危机中的可信信号，以及相互尊重和非暴力规范。 / Distinguish the monadic claim that democracies are generally more peaceful from the dyadic claim that democracies do not fight each other. They are different propositions. The deck lists leader accountability, transparent procedures, credible crisis signals, and norms of mutual respect and nonviolence as explanations.（Slide 18）

## 建构主义 / Constructivism

Wendt 的 *Social Theory of International Politics*（1999）强调观念、文化、规范与身份。行为体想要什么取决于“自己是谁”及其社会环境，利益并非预先固定；不能只用财富等物质因素解释。 / Wendt's *Social Theory of International Politics* (1999) emphasizes ideas, culture, norms, and identity. What actors want depends on who they understand themselves to be and their social context; interests are not predetermined and cannot be explained only by material factors such as wealth.（Slide 19）

制度承载“合适行为”的规则，互动可使行为体社会化，进而改变身份与利益。规范和价值变化意味着国际政治可能发生深层转型，而不完全由无政府状态预先决定。 / Institutions embody rules of appropriate behavior, and interaction can socialize actors and change identities and interests. Changing norms and values make fundamental transformation possible rather than leaving world politics predetermined by anarchy.（Slide 20）

课件例子：如果许多国家把某种武器视为野蛮，国家可能放弃使用；活动者也可以通过推广新规范改变国家行为。分析时应明确“规范 → 合适性判断／身份 → 偏好或行为变化”的过程。 / The deck's example is that states may forgo a weapon when many countries regard its use as barbaric; activists may also change state behavior by promoting norms. Trace the mechanism from norms to judgments of appropriateness or identity, then to changed preferences or behavior.（Slides 19–20）

## 易混淆点 / Common Confusions

- **无政府状态与无合作：** 缺乏中央执法者，不等于没有规则，也不自动排除合作；三种理论对后果解释不同。 / **Anarchy and cooperation:** No central enforcer does not mean no rules or no cooperation; the theories interpret its consequences differently.
- **国家能力与国家意图：** 能力可以观察或估计，但未来意图存在不确定性，这是安全困境的重要环节。 / **Capabilities and intentions:** Capabilities can be observed or estimated, but future intentions remain uncertain, a key part of the security dilemma.
- **制度主义与建构主义：** 前者重点是制度如何帮助实现利益，后者还问制度和规范如何塑造利益本身。 / **Institutionalism and constructivism:** The former emphasizes institutions helping actors achieve interests; the latter also asks how institutions and norms shape interests themselves.
- **课件简化与理论全貌：** 课件对各流派作入门概括，例如“合作不可能”“战争可预期”等应联系相应假设理解。 / **Introductory simplification:** Statements about unlikely cooperation or expected war should be interpreted within the relevant assumptions rather than as complete accounts of each tradition.
