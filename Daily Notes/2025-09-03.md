## 2. 比较优势 / Comparative Advantage

比较优势是指一个经济体在生产某种商品时，相对于其他经济体具有更低机会成本的能力。
Comparative advantage refers to the ability of an economic entity to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost than other entities.

- 比较优势强调的是机会成本而非绝对成本。
- Comparative advantage emphasizes opportunity cost rather than absolute cost.

### 数学表达 / Mathematical Expression

若国家A生产商品X的机会成本低于国家B，则A在X上具有比较优势。
If country A has a lower opportunity cost of producing good X than country B, then A has a comparative advantage in X.

$$
\text{比较优势条件：} \quad OC_A^X < OC_B^X
$$

其中$OC$表示机会成本。
Where $OC$ denotes opportunity cost.

### 例子 / Example

- 国家A生产1单位X需放弃2单位Y，国家B需放弃3单位Y，则A在X上有比较优势。
- If country A gives up 2 units of Y to produce 1 unit of X, and country B gives up 3 units of Y, then A has a comparative advantage in X.
