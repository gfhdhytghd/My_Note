# Chapter3 Demand and Supply
## Law of demand
存在商品价格与买方愿意购买数量之间的反向关系。
There exists an inverse relationship between the price of a good and the amount of it that buyers are willing to purchase.
### 解释 / Explanation

当商品价格上升时，其他条件不变，买方通常会减少购买数量；反之，价格下降时，买方会增加购买数量。这种现象称为需求定律。

When the price of a good rises, ceteris paribus, buyers usually reduce the quantity they purchase; conversely, when the price falls, buyers increase the quantity they purchase. This phenomenon is called the law of demand.

## Demand Curve / 需求曲线

需求曲线表示在不同价格水平下，消费者愿意购买某种商品的数量。  
The demand curve shows the quantity of a good that consumers are willing to buy at different price levels.

- 需求曲线通常向右下方倾斜，反映价格与需求量的反向关系。  
  The demand curve typically slopes downward to the right, reflecting the inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded.

$$
Q_d = f(P)
$$

其中 $Q_d$ 为需求量，$P$ 为价格。  
Where $Q_d$ is quantity demanded and $P$ is price.

---

## Demand Curve Shift / 需求曲线的移动（需求的变化）

需求曲线的移动表示在所有价格水平下，需求量整体发生变化，称为“需求的变化”（change in demand），而不是“需求量的变化”（change in quantity demanded）。

A shift of the demand curve means that the quantity demanded changes at every price level, called a "change in demand," not just a "change in quantity demanded."

### 影响需求曲线移动的因素 / Factors Causing Demand Curve Shifts

- 收入变化 / Change in income
- 消费者偏好变化 / Change in consumer preferences
- 相关商品价格变化（替代品、互补品）/ Change in prices of related goods (substitutes, complements)
- 预期 / Expectations
- 人口数量变化 / Change in population

**右移：** 需求增加，曲线整体向右上方移动。  
**Leftward shift:** Demand increases, the curve shifts to the right.

**左移：** 需求减少，曲线整体向左下方移动。  
**Rightward shift:** Demand decreases, the curve shifts to the left.

---

### 需求量的变化 vs. 需求的变化 / Change in Quantity Demanded vs. Change in Demand

- 价格变化引起的是“需求量的变化”，表现为曲线上的点移动。  
  Change in price causes a "change in quantity demanded," shown as movement along the curve.
- 非价格因素变化引起的是“需求的变化”，表现为曲线整体移动。  
  Change in non-price factors causes a "change in demand," shown as a shift of the entire curve.
