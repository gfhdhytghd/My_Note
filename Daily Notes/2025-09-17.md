# 2025-09-17
## 1. 电负性 / Electronegativity

- 电负性是指原子在化学键中吸引电子的能力。  
  Electronegativity refers to the ability of an atom to attract electrons in a chemical bond.

- 常用的电负性标度是保罗·灵（Pauling）标度。  
  The most commonly used scale is the Pauling scale.

- 一般规律：  
  General trends:  
  - 从左到右，电负性增加；从上到下，电负性减小。  
    Electronegativity increases from left to right across a period and decreases from top to bottom in a group.

---

## 2. 极性共价键 / Polar Covalent Bond

- 当两个原子的电负性差异较小但不为零时，形成极性共价键。  
  When two atoms have a small but nonzero difference in electronegativity, a polar covalent bond is formed.

- 电子云偏向电负性较大的原子，形成电偶极。  
  The electron cloud is drawn closer to the more electronegative atom, creating a dipole.

- 例子：$H_2O$中的O-H键。  
  Example: The O-H bond in $H_2O$.

---

## 3. 离子键 / Ionic Bond

- 当两个原子的电负性差异很大时，电子几乎完全转移，形成离子键。  
  When the difference in electronegativity between two atoms is large, electrons are almost completely transferred, forming an ionic bond.

- 形成阳离子和阴离子，通过静电力结合。  
  Cations and anions are formed and held together by electrostatic forces.

- 例子：$NaCl$中的Na$^+$和Cl$^-$.  
  Example: Na$^+$ and Cl$^-$ in $NaCl$.

---

## 4. Lewis结构 / Lewis Structures

- Lewis结构是用点和线表示分子中化学键和孤对电子的图示方法  
  Lewis structures are diagrams showing bonding and lone pairs in molecules

- 绘制步骤：计算价电子 → 确定中心原子 → 连接原子 → 分配电子 → 检查八隅体  
  Drawing steps: count valence electrons → determine central atom → connect atoms → distribute electrons → check octet rule

- 重要概念：共振结构、形式电荷、VSEPR理论预测  
  Key concepts: resonance structures, formal charge, VSEPR theory prediction
