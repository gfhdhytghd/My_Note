### 课堂笔记总结 / Class Notes Summary

- 若价格上限高于均衡价格（P*），则不具约束力；市场结果不变。  
- If a price ceiling is above equilibrium (P*), it is non-binding; market outcome unchanged.  
- 案例：斯德哥尔摩租金管制，进入受控房源平均等待约 9 年。  
- Case: Stockholm rent control; about 9-year average waiting time to access controlled units.

**补充 / Additional**  
- 有效价格上限（低于 P*）会导致短缺、排队与黑市，并可能降低维护与新建激励。  
- An effective ceiling (below P*) causes shortages, queues, black markets, and may reduce maintenance/new construction incentives.

## 3. 价格欺诈 / Price Gouging

card![[price_gouging]]

**课堂要点 / Class highlights**  
- 定义：突发需求冲击下显著提价的行为（多见于灾害、疫情）。  
- Definition: Significant price increases during sudden demand shocks (disasters, pandemics).  
- 争议：效率（资源调配）与公平（消费者保护）间的权衡。  
- Debate: Trade-off between efficiency (allocation) and fairness (consumer protection).
