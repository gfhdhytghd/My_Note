## 宣传的特征 / Character of Propaganda

- 定义——“宣传是有意、系统地尝试塑造感知、改变认知并引导行为，以实现有利于宣传者目标的回应。” / Definition — “Propaganda is the deliberate, systematic attempt to shape perceptions, manipulate cognitions, and direct behavior to achieve a response that furthers the desired intent of the propagandist.”
- 1. 深思熟虑——这是故意为之；没人会“误打误撞”地做宣传 / Deliberate — it’s intentional; no one engages in propaganda by mistake.
- 2. 系统方法——有条不紊；有明确计划 / Systematic — it’s methodical; there is a plan.
- 3. “尝试”——定向沟通；有明确目标 / An “attempt” — it’s directed communication; there is a goal.
- 4. 塑造感知——目标是改变我们的看法 / Shaping perceptions — the goal is to change how we see.
- 5. 改变认知——想要改变我们的理解方式 / Altering cognitions — it wants to change how we understand.
- 6. 瞄准行为——通过改变看法与理解来改变我们的行为 / Aimed at behavior — it wants to change how we see and understand something to alter how we act.
- 7. 追求回应——宣传追求的是让受众作出符合其目标的回应 / The response a propagandist wants — propaganda is about achieving a goal.
- 煽动型与整合型——煽动型促使人们去做事（如鼓励投票）；整合型旨在阻止行动（如虚假警告投票会威胁自由，使人们留在沙发上） / Agitative vs. integrative — agitative aims to make people act (e.g., encouraging voting); integrative aims to prevent action (e.g., falsely warning that voting threatens liberty to keep people on the couch).
- 另外：可将宣传置于“真实性/透明度”的光谱上…… / Additionally: they describe propaganda on a spectrum of truth and transparency…

## 宣传技巧 / Propaganda Techniques

- 1. 背书与推荐——使用知名人士来支持产品或观点 / Endorsements and testimonials — using a well-known person to support a product or point of view.
- 2. 随大流（从众）——“大家都在做，所以你也该做”的论点 / Bandwagon — a version of the “everybody’s doing it so you should too” argument.
- 3. 个人诉求——如果你做/买了这件东西，你会感觉更好，或他人会更尊重/更喜欢你 / Personal appeals — the argument that if you do or buy this thing you will feel better about yourself or others will respect/like you more.
- 4. 华丽空泛（笼统辞藻）——关于某个行动为何是好的模糊主张 / Glittering generalities — vague claims about why a course of action is good.
- 5. 人身攻击/污名化——与“华丽空泛”相反，模糊声称某个行动为何是坏的 / Name calling — the opposite of glittering generalities — vague claims about why a course of action is bad.
- 6. 诱导性用词（带情绪词）——用充满情绪的语言引发反应而非理性回应 / Loaded words — language filled with emotion designed to get people to react instead of respond to argumentative claims.
- 7. 选择性陈述（堆牌）——有选择地使用事实，使某事看起来比实际更好 / Card stacking — selective use of available facts designed to make something look better than it is.
- 8. 误导性数字——以不完全错误但也不准确的方式使用统计和证据 / Misleading numbers — statistics and other evidence used in ways that aren’t exactly wrong, but also aren’t accurate.
- 9. 刻板印象——依赖对群体特征不准确且常带贬义的定义 / Stereotyping — relying on inaccurate and often demeaning definitions of group characteristics.
- 10. 平民化诉求——把人包装成“普通人”，从而显得更可信 / Plain folks appeals — making a person look like they are a “regular” person and therefore trustworthy.

## 化学 / Chemistry

### 轨道杂化 / Orbital Hybridization

轨道杂化是指原子轨道通过线性组合形成新的杂化轨道的过程。这些杂化轨道具有不同的形状和方向性，以适应分子几何结构。 / Orbital hybridization is the linear combination of atomic orbitals to form new hybrid orbitals whose shapes and orientations fit molecular geometry.
例如： / For example:
- **sp杂化**：一个s轨道和一个p轨道杂化，形成两个线性杂化轨道，键角为180°。 / **sp hybridization**: one s and one p orbital mix to form two linear hybrids with a 180° bond angle.
- **sp²杂化**：一个s轨道和两个p轨道杂化，形成三个平面三角形杂化轨道，键角为120°。 / **sp² hybridization**: one s and two p orbitals mix to form three trigonal planar hybrids with 120° bond angles.
- **sp³杂化**：一个s轨道和三个p轨道杂化，形成四个四面体杂化轨道，键角为109.5°。 / **sp³ hybridization**: one s and three p orbitals mix to form four tetrahedral hybrids with 109.5° bond angles.

### Sigma键和Pi键 / Sigma and Pi Bonds

- **Sigma键 (σ键)**：通过轨道的头碰头重叠形成，电子密度集中在键轴上。它是最强的共价键类型，通常是单键的组成部分。 / **Sigma bond (σ)**: formed by head-on overlap with electron density along the bond axis; the strongest covalent bond, constituting single bonds.
- **Pi键 (π键)**：通过轨道的侧向重叠形成，电子密度分布在键轴的上下方或两侧。它通常出现在双键和三键中，与σ键共同存在。 / **Pi bond (π)**: formed by side-on overlap with electron density above/below or on sides of the bond axis; occurs in double and triple bonds alongside a σ bond.

#### 示例 / Example
- 乙烯 (C₂H₄) 中，碳原子采用sp²杂化，每个碳原子形成三个σ键和一个π键。 / In ethene (C₂H₄), each carbon is sp²-hybridized, forming three σ bonds and one π bond.
- 乙炔 (C₂H₂) 中，碳原子采用sp杂化，每个碳原子形成两个σ键和两个π键。 / In ethyne (C₂H₂), each carbon is sp-hybridized, forming two σ bonds and two π bonds.

## 经济学 / Economics

### 价格下限 / Price Floor

价格下限是政府设定的最低价格，通常用于保护生产者的利益。 / A price floor is a government-set minimum price, typically intended to protect producers.
例如： / For example:
- **最低工资**：政府规定的雇员工资最低标准，防止工资过低。 / **Minimum wage**: a mandated wage floor to prevent very low pay.
- **农产品支持价格**：政府为保护农民收入而设定的最低收购价格。 / **Agricultural support price**: a minimum procurement price to stabilize farmers’ income.

#### 影响 / Impacts
- **供需失衡**：价格下限可能导致供过于求，例如最低工资可能导致失业增加（过剩）。 / **Supply-demand imbalance**: price floors can create surpluses; e.g., a higher minimum wage can reduce labor demanded and raise unemployment.
- **市场扭曲**：价格下限可能抑制市场的自由调节功能，导致资源分配效率降低。 / **Market distortion**: interferes with price signals, reducing allocative efficiency.

#### 示例 / Example
- 在某些国家，政府规定牛奶的最低价格，以确保奶农的收入不受市场波动的影响。 / Some countries set a minimum milk price to stabilize dairy farmers’ incomes.
- 最低工资政策可能导致企业减少雇佣，特别是对低技能工人的需求下降。 / Minimum wage policies can reduce hiring, especially for low-skill workers.
