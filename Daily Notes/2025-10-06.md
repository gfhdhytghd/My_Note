# Economic

### Diminishing Marginal Product / 边际产量递减

- 随着某一生产要素投入增加，其边际产量最终会递减。  
  As more of one input is added, its marginal product will eventually decrease.

- 公式：$\frac{\partial^2 Q}{\partial L^2} < 0$  
  Formula: $\frac{\partial^2 Q}{\partial L^2} < 0$
