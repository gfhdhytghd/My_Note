## 浓度指标总览 / Concentration Metrics Overview

  Collected mass fraction $w=\frac{m_\mathrm{solute}}{m_\mathrm{solution}}$, weight percent $\text{mass\%}=w\times100\%$, mole fraction $x=\frac{n_\mathrm{solute}}{n_\mathrm{solution}}$, molarity $M=\frac{n_\mathrm{solute}}{V_\mathrm{solution}}$, and the $10^6/10^9/10^{12}$ scaling behind ppm/ppb/ppt to keep a clear path for unit conversions.

## 强电解质离子核算 / Strong Electrolyte Ion Accounting
- 通过$\ce{CaCl2(s) -> Ca^{2+}(aq) + 2Cl^{-}(aq)}$示例确认溶解化学计量直接给出离子浓度：1 M $\ce{CaCl2}$对应1 M $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$和2 M $\ce{Cl^{-}}$，总离子数浓度为3 M。  
  Used $\ce{CaCl2(s) -> Ca^{2+}(aq) + 2Cl^{-}(aq)}$ to reinforce that dissolution stoichiometry maps straight onto ion molarities: 1 M $\ce{CaCl2}$ yields 1 M $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$ plus 2 M $\ce{Cl^{-}}$, giving 3 M total ions.
- 记录遇到多价或多元电解质时应平衡电荷并跟踪每种离子的化学计量系数，避免漏算高倍数阳离子或阴离子。  
  Noted the need to balance charge and track stoichiometric coefficients when handling multivalent or polyatomic electrolytes so that high-multiplicity ions are not overlooked.

## 溶液操作区分 / Handling Solution Operations
- 转移操作仅移动部分溶液，溶质摩尔数与浓度都保持不变；稀释则通过补加溶剂改变总体积，使摩尔数不变但浓度下降。  
  Distinguished transfer, which preserves both solute moles and concentration, from dilution, which adds solvent so moles stay fixed while concentration drops.
- 复习课堂强调的「先判定何者保持恒定」策略：转移题锁定$M$不变，稀释题锁定$n$不变，为设立方程提供首要条件。  
  Echoed the lecture strategy of spotting which quantity stays constant first: hold $M$ steady for transfers and hold $n$ steady for dilutions to anchor the algebra.

## 稀释算例记录 / Dilution Worked Example
- 快速稀释法直接应用$M_1V_1=M_2V_2$，并可扩展到混合多组分溶液时的逐项物料平衡。  
  Logged the shortcut $M_1V_1=M_2V_2$ for one-step dilutions and its extension to term-by-term balances when multiple solutions are combined.
- 课堂例题：10.0 mL、2.25 M的$\ce{Na3PO4}$稀释至40.0 mL，得到$M_2=2.25\times\frac{10.0}{40.0}=0.5625$ M，因此$\ce{Na+}$浓度为$3\times0.5625=1.6875$ M。  
  Lecture example: diluting 10.0 mL of 2.25 M $\ce{Na3PO4}$ to 40.0 mL gives $M_2=2.25\times\frac{10.0}{40.0}=0.5625$ M, so the $\ce{Na+}$ concentration becomes $3\times0.5625=1.6875$ M.
