## 亨利定律与气体溶解度 / Henry's Law & Gas Solubility
- 仅气体溶解度随外部压力显著变化，而液体与固体在常见范围内几乎不受影响。  Only dissolved gases respond strongly to pressure changes; liquids and solids are nearly pressure-independent.
- 亨利定律表述为 $C_g=kP_g$，其中 $C_g$ 为气体溶解度、$P_g$ 为气体分压、$k$ 为与溶剂间分子间作用力相关的常数。  Henry's law uses $C_g=kP_g$ where $k$ captures IMF strength between the gas and solvent.
- 强氢键或偶极作用的气体拥有更大的 $k$ 值，因此在相同分压下溶解更多；图像上体现为更陡的斜率。  Gases forming stronger IMFs with water have larger $k$ and steeper solubility–pressure slopes, so more dissolves at the same partial pressure.
- 碳酸饮料装瓶时在 1.5–2.0 atm 的 $\text{CO}_2$ 分压下达到高溶解度；开盖后分压骤降至近 0 atm，溶解度降低导致气泡逸出。  Soda stays saturated under 1.5–2.0 atm $\text{CO}_2$ headspace, but once opened the drop to ~0 atm drives supersaturation and bubbling.

## 正外部性 / Positive Externalities
- 例：疫苗、城市公园。  Ex: Vaccines, parks.
- 市场与社会最优：正外部性下市场产量低于社会最优（$Q_{\text{market}}<Q_{\text{social}}$）。  Positive externality yields less market Q than socially desirable.
- 政策工具：政府可对供给或需求给予补贴，使曲线右移并达到社会最优（supply subsidy → right shift）。  The government can subsidize supply or demand to reach the social optimum.
- 判据：社会最优处满足 $MSB = MSC$。  At the social optimum, $MSB = MSC$.
- 图示要点：供给曲线 $S$，原需求 $D$，社会最优需求 $D'\,(MSB)$ 更靠右；市场均衡 $Q^*$ 与社会最优数量 $Q_{\text{social}}$ 对比。  Diagram: $S$, $D$, and $D'$ (MSB) with subsidy shifting right; compare $Q^*$ vs. $Q_{\text{social}}$.

## 四种商品类型 / Four Types of Goods
- 可竞争（Rival）：需要竞争消费，通常同一单位只能被一人有效消费。  Rival: have to compete for consumption.
- 可排他（Excludable）：可阻止他人获取该商品。  Excludable: can prevent people from accessing the good.

分类 / Classification：
- 可排他、可竞争 → 私人品（Private Good）：冰淇淋、披萨、住房。  Ex: Ice cream, pizza, housing.
- 不可排他、可竞争 → 公共资源/公地（Common Resource）：鱼类、树木。  Ex: Fish, trees.
- 可排他、不可竞争 → 俱乐部品（Club Good）：俱乐部、卫星电视、未拥挤的演唱会。  Ex: Club, satellite TV, concert (not crowded).
- 不可排他、不可竞争 → 公共品（Public Good）：公园、海洋。  Ex: Park, ocean.

注 / Note：非竞争品在空间极度稀缺时会变得“拥挤”而具竞争性（泰勒·斯威夫特 vs 本地乐队）。  Nonrival goods can become rival if space is very scarce (Taylor Swift vs a local band).

## 公共品困境 / Public Goods Problem
- 公共品困境指公共物品因搭便车行为而供给不足，社会最优被系统性低估。  The public goods problem captures chronic under-provision caused by free riders.
- 在标准公共品博弈中，纳什均衡是所有玩家都“留牌”，即保留自己的资源不投入公共池。  In the canonical public-goods game, the Nash equilibrium is for everyone to keep both cards.
- 囚徒困境式逻辑支配公共品：个体理性→集体低效率，需协调或制度来突破。  Public goods share prisoner’s-dilemma logic: individual rationality creates collective inefficiency.
- 未捐献者享受他人贡献属于搭便车，例如街头艺人旁观者不打赏却享受表演。  Non-donors free ride on others’ contributions, e.g., people enjoying a performer without tipping.
- 详见 [[Economics/16.Externalities_Public_Goods/social_costs_and_externalities]] 对公共品均衡的图示。  See [[Economics/16.Externalities_Public_Goods/social_costs_and_externalities]] for diagrams.

## 公地悲剧 / Tragedy of the Commons
- 公共资源人人可用但不可排他，导致过度使用与维护不足，被称为公地悲剧。  Shared, non-excludable resources are overused and under-maintained, creating the tragedy of the commons.
- 空气污染：排放者分摊成本给所有人，空气质量恶化。  Air pollution spreads the cost to everyone, reducing quality.
- 海洋污染：渔民与航运者若不内化成本，将破坏海洋生态。  Ocean pollution arises when fishers/shippers ignore external costs.
- 电影院爆米花浪费：共享容器或无限续杯促成过量消费与浪费。  Movie popcorn buckets illustrate overconsumption when access is unrestricted.
- 回收（recycling）等制度可把外部损害内部化，缓解搭便车与公地悲剧。  Recycling programs internalize damage and curb free riding.

## 政策与激励案例 / Policy & Incentives Examples
- 1971 年俄勒冈州对啤酒与汽水瓶设定 \$0.05 回收押金，提供清洁正向激励。  Oregon’s 1971 five-cent bottle refund created a positive cleanup incentive.
- 新墨西哥州圣菲市采用分级水价，最高用水者每加仑支付至少 4 倍于低用水者。  Santa Fe, NM uses tiered water pricing so heavy users pay ~4× more per gallon than modest users.

## TPS 课堂公共品游戏 / TPS Classroom Public Goods Game
- 每队持有两张可保留或捐赠的卡片，决策写在纸上防止信息泄露。  Each team starts with two cards to keep or donate, written privately.
- 每保留一张卡片可得 \$30 私人收益；每捐赠一张卡片记入公共池。  Keeping a card yields \$30 privately; donating moves it to the public pot.
- 公共池每张卡价值 \$60，最终总额在所有队伍之间平均分配。  Each donated card adds \$60 to the pot, which is split equally among all teams.
- 策略选项：全留、全捐或部分捐赠，考察个人理性与集体利益冲突。  Options—keep both, donate both, mix—highlight the tension between individual incentives and group welfare.
