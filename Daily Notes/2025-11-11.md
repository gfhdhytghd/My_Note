- 摘录 / Extract:
In the upcoming lecture we’re going to begin to explore chemical reactions. During a chemical reaction bonds and lone pairs are reapportioned among the substances involved in the reaction. In order words, bonds are broken and formed during reactions, and electrons are rearranged among the structures of the reactants.

## 完整/净离子方程式 / Complete and Net Ionic Reactions
- 写法步骤 / How to write:
  - 将强电解质写成离子形式；分子物质、弱电解质、气体和沉淀保持分子形式。 Write strong electrolytes as ions; keep molecular species, weak electrolytes, gases, and precipitates intact.
  - 约去旁观离子，得到净离子方程式。 Cancel spectator ions to obtain the net ionic equation.

- 示例（沉淀反应）/ Example (precipitation):
  - 分子方程 / Molecular: BaCl2(aq) + Na2SO4(aq) → BaSO4(s) + 2 NaCl(aq)
  - 完整离子 / Complete ionic: Ba^{2+}(aq) + 2 Cl^{-}(aq) + 2 Na^{+}(aq) + SO4^{2-}(aq) → BaSO4(s) + 2 Na^{+}(aq) + 2 Cl^{-}(aq)
  - 净离子 / Net ionic: Ba^{2+}(aq) + SO4^{2-}(aq) → BaSO4(s)
