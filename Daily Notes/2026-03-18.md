## Math 220 / 基与维数 / Basis and Dimension

### 为什么向量空间有用 / Why Vector Spaces Are Useful
向量空间之所以重要，是因为一旦选定一组基，空间中的任意向量都能唯一写成这组基向量的线性组合；于是线性变换就可以用矩阵来描述。  
Vector spaces are useful because once a basis is chosen, every vector in the space can be written uniquely as a linear combination of basis vectors; this is why linear transformations can be described by matrices.

并不是只有$\mathbb{R}^n$有基；矩阵空间、多项式空间等也都可以有自己的基。  
Basis is not limited to $\mathbb{R}^n$; matrix spaces and polynomial spaces also have their own bases.

### 基的定义 / Definition of a Basis
向量空间的一组基（basis）是一个既线性无关、又能张成整个空间的向量集合。  
A basis of a vector space is a set of vectors that is both linearly independent and spanning.

换句话说，空间中的每个向量都可以表示成基向量的线性组合，而且这种表示方式是唯一的。  
In other words, every vector in the space can be written as a linear combination of basis vectors, and that representation is unique.

### 维数的定义 / Definition of Dimension
向量空间的维数（dimension）是一组基中向量的个数，也可以理解为这个空间的“自由度”。  
The dimension of a vector space is the number of vectors in a basis, which can also be interpreted as the “degrees of freedom” of the space.

### 典型例子 / Standard Examples

#### 1. $\mathbb{R}^3$ / The Space $\mathbb{R}^3$
$\mathbb{R}^3$ 的标准基是
The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is
$$
\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}
\right\}.
$$

因此$\dim(\mathbb{R}^3)=3$。例如，
So $\dim(\mathbb{R}^3)=3$. For example,
$$
\begin{bmatrix}3\\4\\5\end{bmatrix}
=
3\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix}
+
4\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{bmatrix}
+
5\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

这说明标准基给出了向量在各坐标方向上的唯一分解。  
This shows that the standard basis gives the unique decomposition of a vector into coordinate directions.

#### 补充：$\mathbb{R}^2$ 不是 $\mathbb{R}^3$ 的子空间 / Why $\mathbb{R}^2$ Is Not a Subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$
课堂上常会特别强调：$\mathbb{R}^2$ is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$。  
In class, it is often emphasized that $\mathbb{R}^2$ is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$.

原因不是“二维比较小、三维比较大”这么简单，而是子空间必须首先是母空间的一个子集。  
The reason is not merely that two dimensions are “smaller” than three; a subspace must first be a subset of the ambient space.

$\mathbb{R}^2$ 的元素是形如$(x,y)$的有序对，而$\mathbb{R}^3$ 的元素是形如$(x,y,z)$的有序三元组；因此$\mathbb{R}^2$ 里的向量并不是$\mathbb{R}^3$ 里的向量，所以$\mathbb{R}^2\not\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3$。  
Elements of $\mathbb{R}^2$ are ordered pairs $(x,y)$, while elements of $\mathbb{R}^3$ are ordered triples $(x,y,z)$; therefore vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ are not literally vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$, so $\mathbb{R}^2\not\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3$.

不过，$\mathbb{R}^3$ 里面确实有“长得像”$\mathbb{R}^2$ 的子空间，例如
However, $\mathbb{R}^3$ does contain subspaces that “look like” $\mathbb{R}^2$, for example
$$
W=\{(x,y,0)\mid x,y\in\mathbb{R}\}.
$$

这个集合是$\mathbb{R}^3$ 的一个子空间，因为它包含零向量，并且对加法与数乘都封闭；几何上，它就是$xy$平面。  
This set is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ because it contains the zero vector and is closed under addition and scalar multiplication; geometrically, it is the $xy$-plane.

所以更准确的说法是：$\mathbb{R}^2$ 本身不是$\mathbb{R}^3$ 的子空间，但$\mathbb{R}^3$ 中某些平面子空间与$\mathbb{R}^2$ 同构（isomorphic）。  
So the more accurate statement is: $\mathbb{R}^2$ itself is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$, but some plane subspaces inside $\mathbb{R}^3$ are isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^2$.

#### 2. 矩阵空间 / Matrix Space
矩阵空间$M_{2\times2}(\mathbb{R})$由所有$2\times2$实矩阵组成。  
The matrix space $M_{2\times2}(\mathbb{R})$ consists of all $2\times2$ real matrices.

它的一组自然基可以取为四个“单位矩阵块”：
A natural basis is given by the four matrix units:
$$
\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
\right\}.
$$

因此$\dim(M_{2\times2}(\mathbb{R}))=4$。任意一个$2\times2$矩阵都能唯一写成这四个基矩阵的线性组合。  
Therefore $\dim(M_{2\times2}(\mathbb{R}))=4$. Any $2\times2$ matrix can be written uniquely as a linear combination of these four basis matrices.

#### 3. 多项式空间 / Polynomial Space
二次及以下多项式空间记为
The space of polynomials of degree at most $2$ is
$$
P_2(\mathbb{R})=\{ax^2+bx+c\mid a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}\}.
$$

它的一组标准基是
A standard basis for this space is
$$
\{1,x,x^2\}.
$$

因此$\dim(P_2(\mathbb{R}))=3$。任意$p(x)\in P_2(\mathbb{R})$都能唯一写成
Thus $\dim(P_2(\mathbb{R}))=3$. Any $p(x)\in P_2(\mathbb{R})$ can be written uniquely as
$$
p(x)=c\cdot1+b\cdot x+a\cdot x^2.
$$

也就是说，系数本身就是该多项式在这组基下的坐标。  
That means the coefficients themselves are the coordinates of the polynomial in this basis.

#### 4. 对称矩阵空间 / Symmetric Matrix Space
对称矩阵空间记为$\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$，它由所有满足$A^T=A$的$n\times n$实矩阵组成：  
The symmetric matrix space is denoted by $\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$, and it consists of all $n\times n$ real matrices satisfying $A^T=A$:
$$
\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})
=
\{A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})\mid A^T=A\}.
$$

也就是说，一个矩阵是对称矩阵，当且仅当它关于主对角线对称，也就是$a_{ij}=a_{ji}$。  
In other words, a matrix is symmetric exactly when it is mirrored across the main diagonal, meaning $a_{ij}=a_{ji}$.

它是$M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$的一个子空间，因为零矩阵是对称的，而且对加法与数乘都封闭：若$A,B$对称，则$(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T=A+B$；若$c\in\mathbb{R}$，则$(cA)^T=cA^T=cA$。  
It is a subspace of $M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$ because the zero matrix is symmetric and the set is closed under both addition and scalar multiplication: if $A,B$ are symmetric, then $(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T=A+B$; if $c\in\mathbb{R}$, then $(cA)^T=cA^T=cA$.

若记$E_{ij}$为在第$(i,j)$个位置是$1$、其余位置全为$0$的矩阵，那么$\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$的一组自然基可以写成  
If $E_{ij}$ denotes the matrix with a $1$ in position $(i,j)$ and $0$ elsewhere, then a natural basis for $\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$ can be written as
$$
\{E_{11},E_{22},\dots,E_{nn}\}
\cup
\{E_{ij}+E_{ji}\mid 1\le i<j\le n\}.
$$

前一部分给出$n$个对角方向，后一部分给出所有成对出现的非对角方向；任意对称矩阵都能唯一写成这些矩阵的线性组合。  
The first part gives the $n$ diagonal directions, and the second part gives all paired off-diagonal directions; every symmetric matrix can be written uniquely as a linear combination of these matrices.

因此它的维数是
Therefore its dimension is
$$
\dim(\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R}))
=
n+\binom{n}{2}
=
n+\frac{n(n-1)}{2}
=
\frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
$$

这个例子说明，“基”和“维数”并不只用于列向量空间，也同样适用于满足某种结构条件的矩阵空间。  
This example shows that basis and dimension are not limited to column-vector spaces; they also apply to matrix spaces defined by structural conditions.

### 课堂小结 / Quick Takeaway
- 基 = 线性无关 + 张成整个空间。  
  Basis = linear independence + spanning the whole space.
- 维数 = 基向量个数 = 自由度。  
  Dimension = number of basis vectors = degrees of freedom.
- 不同类型的向量空间都可以讨论基与维数，不只限于$\mathbb{R}^n$。  
  Many kinds of vector spaces have bases and dimensions, not just $\mathbb{R}^n$.
- $\mathbb{R}^2$ 本身不是$\mathbb{R}^3$ 的子空间；真正的子空间例子是$\{(x,y,0)\}$这样的平面。  
  $\mathbb{R}^2$ itself is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$; a genuine subspace example is a plane such as $\{(x,y,0)\}$.
- 对称矩阵空间$\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$是一个典型例子，其维数为$\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$。  
  The symmetric matrix space $\operatorname{Sym}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$ is a standard example, and its dimension is $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
