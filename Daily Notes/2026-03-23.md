## Change of Basis / 换基
今天记的核心问题是：同一个向量在不同 basis 下怎么改写坐标。向量本身不变，变的是它相对于不同基的坐标表示。  
The key question today is how to rewrite the coordinates of the same vector in a different basis. The vector itself stays the same; what changes is its coordinate representation relative to different bases.

课堂上的两种做法都很直接：可以把“新基向量”排成矩阵后做增广矩阵求解，也可以直接用逆矩阵来把坐标换过去。  
The two classroom methods are both direct: form a matrix from the new basis vectors and solve with an augmented matrix, or use the inverse matrix to convert the coordinates directly.

若设基矩阵为$P=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_n]$，那么标准坐标$x$与基坐标$c$之间满足$x=Pc$，因此$c=P^{-1}x$。  
If the basis matrix is $P=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_n]$, then the standard coordinate vector $x$ and the basis coordinate vector $c$ satisfy $x=Pc$, so $c=P^{-1}x$.
