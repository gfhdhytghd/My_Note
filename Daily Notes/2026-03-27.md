## Math 220 / 特征值、特征向量与特征子空间 / Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, and Eigenspaces

### 定义与直观 / Definition and Intuition
如果非零向量$\vec{v}$满足$A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$，那么$\vec{v}$是矩阵$A$的特征向量（eigenvector），而$\lambda$是对应的特征值（eigenvalue）。  
If a nonzero vector $\vec{v}$ satisfies $A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$, then $\vec{v}$ is an eigenvector of $A$, and $\lambda$ is the corresponding eigenvalue.

几何上，特征向量在矩阵变换后不会“转向”，只会被拉长、压缩，或者在$\lambda<0$时翻到相反方向。  
Geometrically, an eigenvector does not change direction under the matrix transformation; it is only stretched, compressed, or flipped when $\lambda<0$.

判断一个向量是不是特征向量的最快方法，就是先算$A\vec{v}$，再检查结果是否为原向量$\vec{v}$的标量倍数。  
The quickest way to test whether a vector is an eigenvector is to compute $A\vec{v}$ and check whether the result is a scalar multiple of $\vec{v}$.

严格地说，与某个特征值$\lambda$对应的特征子空间写作$E_\lambda(A)=Nul(A-\lambda I)$；它包含该方向上的所有特征向量以及零向量。  
Strictly speaking, the eigenspace corresponding to an eigenvalue $\lambda$ is written as $E_\lambda(A)=Nul(A-\lambda I)$; it contains all eigenvectors in that direction together with the zero vector.

### 为什么特征子空间是子空间 / Why an Eigenspace Is a Subspace
如果$\vec{x},\vec{y}\in E_\lambda(A)$，那么
If $\vec{x},\vec{y}\in E_\lambda(A)$, then
$$
A(\vec{x}+\vec{y})=A\vec{x}+A\vec{y}=\lambda\vec{x}+\lambda\vec{y}=\lambda(\vec{x}+\vec{y}).
$$
如果$c\in\mathbb{R}$，那么
If $c\in\mathbb{R}$, then
$$
A(c\vec{x})=cA\vec{x}=c\lambda\vec{x}=\lambda(c\vec{x}).
$$
因此它对加法与数乘封闭；再加上$E_\lambda(A)=Nul(A-\lambda I)$，所以它确实是一个子空间。  
Therefore it is closed under addition and scalar multiplication; together with $E_\lambda(A)=Nul(A-\lambda I)$, this shows that it is a subspace.

### 特征子空间维数与重数 / Dimension of an Eigenspace and Multiplicity
课堂上补充的结论是：如果$\lambda$是矩阵$A$的一个特征值，那么对应特征子空间$E_\lambda(A)$的维数至少是$1$，但不会超过$\lambda$的代数重数。  
The additional class conclusion is: if $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of a matrix $A$, then the dimension of the corresponding eigenspace $E_\lambda(A)$ is at least $1$, but no greater than the algebraic multiplicity of $\lambda$.

写成公式就是
In symbols,
$$
1\leq \dim(E_\lambda(A))\leq \operatorname{mult}(\lambda).
$$

这里$\dim(E_\lambda(A))$也叫几何重数（geometric multiplicity），而$\operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$是代数重数（algebraic multiplicity）。几何重数表示这个特征值对应多少个线性无关的特征向量方向。  
Here $\dim(E_\lambda(A))$ is also called the geometric multiplicity, while $\operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$ is the algebraic multiplicity. The geometric multiplicity tells us how many linearly independent eigendirections correspond to that eigenvalue.

这句话可以拆成上下两个部分理解。首先，既然$\lambda$已经被称为一个特征值，就说明至少存在一个非零向量$\vec{v}$满足$A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$；于是$E_\lambda(A)$里至少有这一个方向，所以维数不可能小于$1$。  
This statement can be understood in two parts. First, if $\lambda$ is called an eigenvalue, then there must exist at least one nonzero vector $\vec{v}$ satisfying $A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$; therefore $E_\lambda(A)$ contains at least that one direction, so its dimension cannot be smaller than $1$.

其次，代数重数$\operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$表示$\lambda$在特征方程里重复出现了多少次；它可以理解为这个特征值“最多能提供多少个独立方向的名额”。几何重数$\dim(E_\lambda(A))$是真正解出来以后，能找到多少个线性无关的特征向量方向，所以它绝不会超过代数重数。  
Second, the algebraic multiplicity $\operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$ counts how many times $\lambda$ appears as a root of the characteristic equation; you can think of it as the maximum number of independent eigendirections that eigenvalue is allowed to supply. The geometric multiplicity $\dim(E_\lambda(A))$ is how many linearly independent eigendirections actually appear when you solve the system, so it can never exceed the algebraic multiplicity.

如果一个特征值的代数重数是$2$，那么它的 eigenspace 维数只能是$1$或$2$，不可能是$3$。若维数正好等于重数，说明这个特征值对应的方向“足够多”；若维数比重数小，说明有一部分重数没有转化成新的独立特征方向。  
If an eigenvalue has algebraic multiplicity $2$, then its eigenspace dimension can only be $1$ or $2$, but never $3$. If the dimension equals the multiplicity, then that eigenvalue has “as many directions as possible”; if the dimension is smaller, then part of the multiplicity did not turn into a new independent eigendirection.

在上面的$3\times3$例子里，$\lambda=-2$的代数重数是$2$，而$\dim(E_{-2}(B))=2$，所以这个特征值“占满了”它允许的最大维数；这就是为什么它对应的是一个 eigenplane。  
In the $3\times3$ example above, the algebraic multiplicity of $\lambda=-2$ is $2$, and $\dim(E_{-2}(B))=2$, so this eigenvalue reaches the largest possible eigenspace dimension; that is why it corresponds to an eigenplane.

同一个例子里，$\lambda=2$的代数重数是$1$，而$\dim(E_2(B))=1$，所以它只对应一条 eigenline。把这两个特征值放在一起看，就能清楚比较“线”和“平面”这两种 eigenspace。  
In the same example, the algebraic multiplicity of $\lambda=2$ is $1$, and $\dim(E_2(B))=1$, so it corresponds to a single eigenline. Looking at these two eigenvalues together makes the contrast between an eigenline and an eigenplane very clear.

### 补充备注 / Additional Remarks
对于上三角矩阵或下三角矩阵，特征值就是主对角线上的元素。  
For an upper triangular or lower triangular matrix, the eigenvalues are exactly the entries on the main diagonal.

原因是此时$A-\lambda I$仍然是三角矩阵，而三角矩阵的行列式等于对角线元素之积，所以
The reason is that $A-\lambda I$ is still triangular, and the determinant of a triangular matrix equals the product of its diagonal entries, so
$$
\det(A-\lambda I)=
(a_{11}-\lambda)(a_{22}-\lambda)\cdots(a_{nn}-\lambda).
$$
令这个乘积等于$0$时，解出来的$\lambda$正好就是各个对角线元素。  
Setting this product equal to $0$ shows that the eigenvalues are precisely the diagonal entries.

另外，零向量$\vec{0}$不能被当作特征向量，但特征值本身可以等于$0$。  
Also, the zero vector $\vec{0}$ is not allowed to be an eigenvector, but an eigenvalue itself is allowed to be $0$.

如果存在某个非零向量$\vec{v}\neq\vec{0}$满足$A\vec{v}=\vec{0}$，那么这其实就是
If there exists a nonzero vector $\vec{v}\neq\vec{0}$ such that $A\vec{v}=\vec{0}$, then this is exactly
$$
A\vec{v}=0\vec{v},
$$
所以$\vec{v}$是特征向量，而对应特征值是$\lambda=0$。真正被排除的是“用$\vec{0}$自己当特征向量”，不是“特征值取$0$”。  
so $\vec{v}$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda=0$. What is excluded is using $\vec{0}$ itself as an eigenvector, not letting the eigenvalue be $0$.

### 补充性质：行列式、矩阵幂与逆矩阵 / Additional Properties: Determinant, Powers, and Inverse
矩阵$A$的行列式等于它所有特征值的乘积，并且要把代数重数也算进去。对前面的$2\times2$矩阵来说，$\det(A)=6\cdot(-9)=-54$；对矩阵$B$来说，$\det(B)=2\cdot(-2)\cdot(-2)=8$。  
The determinant of a matrix $A$ equals the product of all its eigenvalues, counting algebraic multiplicity. For the earlier $2\times2$ matrix, $\det(A)=6\cdot(-9)=-54$; for matrix $B$, $\det(B)=2\cdot(-2)\cdot(-2)=8$.

如果$A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$，那么反复作用矩阵不会改变这条特征方向，只会把缩放因子不断相乘，因此  
If $A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$, then repeated application of the matrix does not change the eigendirection; it only keeps multiplying the scaling factor, so
$$
A^n\vec{v}=\lambda^n\vec{v}\qquad(n\geq1).
$$
这也是为什么特征值特别适合描述离散系统的长期行为：绝对值最大的特征值往往主导“长期会往哪里走”。  
This is also why eigenvalues are useful for describing the long-term behavior of discrete systems: the eigenvalue with the largest absolute value often dominates what happens in the long run.

如果$A$可逆并且$\lambda\neq0$，那么同一个特征向量对$A^{-1}$仍然有效，只是对应特征值变成倒数：  
If $A$ is invertible and $\lambda\neq0$, then the same eigenvector also works for $A^{-1}$, but the corresponding eigenvalue becomes its reciprocal:
$$
A^{-1}\vec{v}=\frac{1}{\lambda}\vec{v}.
$$

### 为什么这些结论有用 / Why These Results Matter
从计算角度看，如果一个矩阵有足够多的线性无关特征向量，那么求$A^n$会比直接连乘容易得多；从建模角度看，特征值常用来判断系统是否稳定，以及状态在长期会增长、衰减还是振荡。  
From a computational point of view, if a matrix has enough linearly independent eigenvectors, then computing $A^n$ can be much easier than multiplying $A$ repeatedly. From a modeling point of view, eigenvalues are often used to judge stability and to predict whether a state will grow, decay, or oscillate in the long run.

### 应用：应力张量与主应力 / Application: Stress Tensor and Principal Stresses
在线性代数的应用里，三维物体内部的应力常写成一个$3\times3$矩阵  
In applications of linear algebra, the internal stress of a three-dimensional object is often written as a $3\times3$ matrix
$$
\Sigma=
\begin{bmatrix}
\sigma_{xx}&\sigma_{xy}&\sigma_{xz}\\
\sigma_{yx}&\sigma_{yy}&\sigma_{yz}\\
\sigma_{zx}&\sigma_{zy}&\sigma_{zz}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
在静力平衡的常见情形下，这个矩阵通常是对称的，也就是$\sigma_{xy}=\sigma_{yx}$、$\sigma_{xz}=\sigma_{zx}$、$\sigma_{yz}=\sigma_{zy}$。  
In common static-equilibrium settings, this matrix is usually symmetric, meaning $\sigma_{xy}=\sigma_{yx}$, $\sigma_{xz}=\sigma_{zx}$, and $\sigma_{yz}=\sigma_{zy}$.

这时，$\Sigma$的特征值叫做主应力（principal stresses），对应的特征向量给出主方向（principal directions）。课堂上的例子使用了  
In that setting, the eigenvalues of $\Sigma$ are called the principal stresses, and the corresponding eigenvectors give the principal directions. The class example used
$$
\Sigma=
\begin{bmatrix}
1.2&1.5&1.3\\
1.5&2.1&0.7\\
1.3&0.7&2.0
\end{bmatrix},
$$
并得到近似特征值$\lambda\approx4.11,\ 1.35,\ -0.16$；其中最大的特征值对应最主要的应力量级，约为$4.11\text{ Pa}$。  
and obtained approximate eigenvalues $\lambda\approx4.11,\ 1.35,\ -0.16$; the largest eigenvalue corresponds to the dominant stress scale, about $4.11\text{ Pa}$.

与最大特征值对应的特征向量大致沿着$\begin{bmatrix}1.17\\1.11\\1.00\end{bmatrix}$的方向，所以它告诉我们“主要应力沿哪个方向作用”。  
The eigenvector associated with the largest eigenvalue is approximately in the direction of $\begin{bmatrix}1.17\\1.11\\1.00\end{bmatrix}$, so it tells us the direction in which the dominant stress acts.

### 课堂小结 / Quick Takeaway
- 特征向量满足$A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$，方向不变，只改长度或翻转。  
  Eigenvectors satisfy $A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$, so their direction is preserved while their length changes or flips.
- 判断是否为特征向量，要看$A\vec{v}$是否为$\vec{v}$的倍数。  
  To test whether a vector is an eigenvector, check whether $A\vec{v}$ is a multiple of $\vec{v}$.
- 特征子空间本质上就是$Nul(A-\lambda I)$。  
  An eigenspace is fundamentally the null space $Nul(A-\lambda I)$.
- 特征子空间的维数满足$1\leq\dim(E_\lambda(A))\leq \operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$。  
  The eigenspace dimension satisfies $1\leq\dim(E_\lambda(A))\leq \operatorname{mult}(\lambda)$.
- 行列式等于全部特征值之积，并且要计入代数重数。  
  The determinant equals the product of all eigenvalues, counting algebraic multiplicity.
- 若$A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$，则$A^n\vec{v}=\lambda^n\vec{v}$；若$A$可逆且$\lambda\neq0$，则$A^{-1}\vec{v}=\frac{1}{\lambda}\vec{v}$。  
  If $A\vec{v}=\lambda\vec{v}$, then $A^n\vec{v}=\lambda^n\vec{v}$; if $A$ is invertible and $\lambda\neq0$, then $A^{-1}\vec{v}=\frac{1}{\lambda}\vec{v}$.
- 求特征值先解$\det(A-\lambda I)=0$，再回代找对应的特征向量。  
  To find eigenvalues, solve $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$, then substitute back to find the corresponding eigenvectors.
- 在 stress tensor 这类应用里，特征值给出主应力，特征向量给出主方向。  
  In applications such as the stress tensor, eigenvalues give the principal stresses and eigenvectors give the principal directions.
