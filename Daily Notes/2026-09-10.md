### 国际关系与分析框架 / International Relations and the Analytical Framework

- **研究对象：** 国际关系研究世界各国与人民如何相处，涵盖战争、安全、贸易、金融、国际组织及跨国议题。关键是解释政治结果，而非仅记事件。 / **Scope:** IR studies relations among countries and peoples, including war, security, trade, finance, international organizations, and transnational issues; the aim is to explain outcomes rather than merely recall events.（导论 / Introduction, slides 2–6）
- **利益—互动—制度：** 先识别行为体及其偏好，再分析选择如何相互影响，最后考察规则如何限制或促进互动。国家、企业、政治人物、官僚与 NGO 未必具有相同利益。 / **Interests–interactions–institutions:** Identify actors and preferences, examine how their choices affect one another, and assess how rules constrain or enable interaction. States, firms, politicians, bureaucracies, and NGOs may have different interests.（Week 1, slides 2–10, 39）
- **无政府状态不等于混乱：** 国际体系缺乏能对所有国家强制执法的中央权威；主权意味着国家在其领土内具有最高法律与政治权威。 / **Anarchy does not mean chaos:** The international system lacks a central authority able to enforce rules on all states; sovereignty means supreme legal and political authority within a state's territory.（Week 1, slides 4–6）

### 合作、谈判与制度 / Cooperation, Bargaining, and Institutions

- **合作与谈判：** 合作创造额外价值，谈判决定如何分配价值，二者经常同时存在。对参与者有利的合作仍可能损害外部行为体。 / **Cooperation and bargaining:** Cooperation creates additional value, while bargaining distributes it; both often occur together. Cooperation among participants may still harm outsiders.（Week 1, slides 11–16）
- **协调与协作：** 协调一旦达成共同选择，就没有偏离激励；协作虽能带来共同收益，却仍有违约或搭便车的诱因。 / **Coordination and collaboration:** Once coordinated on a common choice, actors have no incentive to deviate; collaboration offers joint gains but retains incentives to defect or free ride.（Week 1, slide 17）
- **囚徒困境：** 刑期越短越好。双方沉默为 $(1,1)$，单方告密为 $(0,5)$ 或 $(5,0)$，双方告密为 $(3,3)$。告密是各自的占优策略，$(3,3)$ 是纳什均衡，却被双方沉默的结果帕累托支配。 / **Prisoner's dilemma:** Lower prison terms are preferable. Mutual silence gives $(1,1)$, unilateral informing $(0,5)$ or $(5,0)$, and mutual informing $(3,3)$. Informing is each player's dominant strategy; $(3,3)$ is a Nash equilibrium but is Pareto-dominated by mutual silence.（Week 1, slides 18–21）
- **公共品与集体行动：** 公共品非排他、非竞争；人人希望别人承担成本会造成搭便车。重复互动、议题联结、互惠与信息有助于合作。 / **Public goods and collective action:** Public goods are non-excludable and non-rival. Hoping others bear the cost creates free riding; iteration, issue linkage, reciprocity, and information can help cooperation.（Week 1, slides 25–28）
- **谈判能力：** 协议失败的回归结果、外部选项、强制与议程设置会影响让步。对“不成交”越能接受的一方，通常越不急于让步。 / **Bargaining power:** Reversion outcomes, outside options, coercion, and agenda setting affect concessions. An actor more comfortable with no agreement generally has less incentive to concede.（Week 1, slides 29–31）
- **制度的作用与偏向：** 制度设定标准、核查履约、降低共同决策成本并解决争端；即使缺乏独立强制执行能力，也能促进合作。但规则可能反映强国的谈判优势。 / **Institutional effects and bias:** Institutions set standards, verify compliance, lower joint decision-making costs, and resolve disputes even without independent enforcement. Rules may nevertheless reflect powerful states' bargaining advantages.（Week 1, slides 32–39）

### 三种国际关系理论 / Three IR Theories

- **理论是什么：** 逻辑一致、解释因果因素及其联系的一组命题，可用来描述、预测并提出政策建议。 / **Theory:** A logically consistent set of statements explaining causal factors and their connections, used to describe, predict, and prescribe.（Week 3, slides 2–3）
- **现实主义：** 国家在无政府状态与意图不确定性下追求安全和权力。一国加强军备可能降低他国安全，形成安全困境。内部均势依靠自身能力，外部均势依靠联盟。 / **Realism:** States seek security and power under anarchy and uncertainty about intentions. One state's military buildup can reduce another's security, creating a security dilemma. Internal balancing builds capabilities; external balancing forms alliances.（Week 3, slides 4–6）
- **现实主义的分支：** 古典现实主义强调人性与领导者；新现实主义强调国际体系结构及权力分布；防御性现实主义强调攻防平衡与维持现状；进攻性现实主义强调相对实力最大化与地区霸权竞争。 / **Realist variants:** Classical realism emphasizes human nature and leaders; neorealism emphasizes system structure and the distribution of power; defensive realism emphasizes the offense–defense balance and the status quo; offensive realism emphasizes relative power maximization and regional hegemony.（Week 3, slides 7–12）
- **自由主义：** 国内群体和制度塑造国家利益；财富、相互依赖与共同利益为合作提供空间。新自由制度主义强调规则和信息，商业和平论强调战争的经济机会成本，民主和平论区分“民主国家总体较和平”与“民主国家彼此不交战”两个命题。 / **Liberalism:** Domestic groups and institutions shape state interests; wealth, interdependence, and common interests create scope for cooperation. Neoliberal institutionalism stresses rules and information; commercial peace stresses war's economic opportunity costs; democratic peace distinguishes democracies being generally peaceful from peace between democracies.（Week 3, slides 13–18）
- **建构主义——今日课表重点：** 利益不是固定给定的，而受到身份、文化、观念和规范塑造。制度不只约束行为，也定义“什么行为合适”；互动与社会化能改变利益，规范变化因此可能改变国际政治。 / **Constructivism—today's scheduled focus:** Interests are shaped by identity, culture, ideas, and norms rather than fixed in advance. Institutions define appropriate behavior as well as constrain action; interaction and socialization can change interests, allowing norm change to transform world politics.（Week 3, slides 19–21）

**复习时的区分：** 现实主义首先问“权力与安全如何分布”，自由主义问“哪些国内利益与共同收益推动合作”，建构主义问“身份与规范如何塑造行为体想要的东西”。这些是不同理论的解释重点，不应当作对所有事件都成立的定律。 / **Review distinction:** Realism asks how power and security are distributed; liberalism asks which domestic interests and shared gains support cooperation; constructivism asks how identities and norms shape what actors want. These are theoretical emphases, not universal laws.

### Math 230：平面的点法式 / Point-Normal Form of a Plane

课程：[[Courses/2026_fall/math_230/course_overview]]。相关复习：[[Daily Notes/2026-09-02]] 中的空间曲线与参数化。 / Course: [[Courses/2026_fall/math_230/course_overview]]. Related review: space curves and parametrization in [[Daily Notes/2026-09-02]].

一个平面可以由平面上一点 $P_0=(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ 和一个非零法向量 $\vec{n}=\langle A,B,C\rangle$ 确定。平面内任意点 $P=(x,y,z)$ 与 $P_0$ 的连线向量都垂直于 $\vec{n}$，因此点法式（point-normal form）为： / A plane is determined by a point $P_0=(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ on it and a nonzero normal vector $\vec{n}=\langle A,B,C\rangle$. For every point $P=(x,y,z)$ in the plane, the displacement from $P_0$ to $P$ is perpendicular to $\vec{n}$, giving the point-normal form:

$$\vec{n}\cdot(\vec{r}-\vec{r}_0)=0$$

$$A(x-x_0)+B(y-y_0)+C(z-z_0)=0$$

展开后得到一般式 $Ax+By+Cz=D$，其中 $D=Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0$；法向量不必是单位向量。若已知三个不共线点 $P_0,P_1,P_2$，可取 $\vec{n}=\overrightarrow{P_0P_1}\times\overrightarrow{P_0P_2}$。 / Expanding gives the general form $Ax+By+Cz=D$, where $D=Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0$; the normal need not be a unit vector. Given three noncollinear points $P_0,P_1,P_2$, use $\vec{n}=\overrightarrow{P_0P_1}\times\overrightarrow{P_0P_2}$.

补充例题：过 $P_0=(1,-2,3)$、法向量为 $\langle2,1,-4\rangle$ 的平面满足 $2(x-1)+(y+2)-4(z-3)=0$，即 $2x+y-4z=-12$。 / Supplementary example: the plane through $P_0=(1,-2,3)$ with normal $\langle2,1,-4\rangle$ satisfies $2(x-1)+(y+2)-4(z-3)=0$, or $2x+y-4z=-12$.

### Math 230：三维位置、速度与加速度 / Position, Velocity, and Acceleration in 3D

当 $t$ 表示时间时，对位置向量逐分量求导得到速度，再求导得到加速度： / When $t$ represents time, differentiate the position vector componentwise to obtain velocity, then differentiate again to obtain acceleration:

$$\vec{r}(t)=\langle x(t),y(t),z(t)\rangle\xrightarrow{d/dt}\vec{v}(t)=\vec{r}\,'(t)\xrightarrow{d/dt}\vec{a}(t)=\vec{v}\,'(t)=\vec{r}\,''(t)$$

速度是向量，速率是其大小。以下用 $q(t)$ 表示速率，以免与速度向量混淆；若 $s(t)$ 为沿运动方向累积的弧长，则： / Velocity is a vector, while speed is its magnitude. Below, $q(t)$ denotes speed to distinguish it from velocity; if $s(t)$ is accumulated arc length along the motion, then:

$$q(t)=\|\vec{v}(t)\|=\sqrt{[x'(t)]^2+[y'(t)]^2+[z'(t)]^2}=\frac{ds}{dt}$$

### Math 230：加速度的切向与法向分解 / Tangential and Normal Components of Acceleration

这里的“顺向”对应沿运动轨迹的切向（tangential）。当 $q>0$ 时，单位切向量 $\vec{T}$ 沿速度方向；当 $\vec{T}\,'\ne\vec{0}$ 时，主单位法向量 $\vec{N}$ 指向曲线局部弯曲的方向，且与 $\vec{T}$ 垂直： / Here, “along the motion” refers to the tangential direction. When $q>0$, the unit tangent $\vec{T}$ points along velocity; when $\vec{T}\,'\ne\vec{0}$, the principal unit normal $\vec{N}$ points toward the local bending of the curve and is perpendicular to $\vec{T}$:

$$\vec{T}=\frac{\vec{v}}{q},\qquad\vec{N}=\frac{\vec{T}\,'}{\|\vec{T}\,'\|}$$

由 $\vec{v}=q\vec{T}$ 使用乘积法则，并用曲率 $\kappa=\|\vec{T}\,'\|/q$，得到： / Apply the product rule to $\vec{v}=q\vec{T}$ and use curvature $\kappa=\|\vec{T}\,'\|/q$ to obtain:

$$\vec{a}=\frac{dq}{dt}\vec{T}+q\vec{T}\,'=a_T\vec{T}+a_N\vec{N}$$

$$a_T=\frac{dq}{dt}=\frac{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{a}}{\|\vec{v}\|},\qquad a_N=\kappa q^2=\frac{\|\vec{v}\times\vec{a}\|}{\|\vec{v}\|}=\sqrt{\|\vec{a}\|^2-a_T^2}$$

- **切向分量改变速率：**$a_T>0$ 表示加速，$a_T<0$ 表示减速，$a_T=0$ 表示该时刻速率的导数为零；切向加速度向量为 $\vec{a}_T=a_T\vec{T}$。 / **The tangential component changes speed:** $a_T>0$ means speeding up, $a_T<0$ means slowing down, and $a_T=0$ means the instantaneous derivative of speed is zero; the tangential acceleration vector is $\vec{a}_T=a_T\vec{T}$.
- **法向分量改变方向：**$a_N\geq0$，法向加速度向量为 $\vec{a}_N=a_N\vec{N}=\vec{a}-a_T\vec{T}$。匀速转弯仍有加速度；圆周运动中 $\kappa=1/R$，故 $a_N=q^2/R$。 / **The normal component changes direction:** $a_N\geq0$, and the normal acceleration vector is $\vec{a}_N=a_N\vec{N}=\vec{a}-a_T\vec{T}$. Turning at constant speed still involves acceleration; for circular motion, $\kappa=1/R$, so $a_N=q^2/R$.
- **适用条件：**上述含速度分母的公式要求 $\vec{v}\ne\vec{0}$；若 $a_N=0$，法向加速度为零，但不能通过归一化零向量来定义 $\vec{N}$。平面的法向量 $\vec{n}$ 与曲线的主单位法向量 $\vec{N}$ 是不同概念。 / **Conditions:** formulas dividing by speed require $\vec{v}\ne\vec{0}$. If $a_N=0$, normal acceleration is zero, but $\vec{N}$ cannot be defined by normalizing a zero vector. A plane normal $\vec{n}$ and a curve's principal unit normal $\vec{N}$ are distinct concepts.

补充三维例题：取螺旋线 $\vec{r}(t)=\langle3\cos t,3\sin t,4t\rangle$，则 $\vec{v}=\langle-3\sin t,3\cos t,4\rangle$、$\vec{a}=\langle-3\cos t,-3\sin t,0\rangle$。速率 $q=5$ 为常数，所以 $a_T=0$；由于 $\|\vec{a}\|=3$，有 $a_N=3$、$\vec{N}=\langle-\cos t,-\sin t,0\rangle$、$\kappa=3/25$。这说明三维运动即使速率不变，速度方向仍可改变。 / Supplementary 3D example: for the helix $\vec{r}(t)=\langle3\cos t,3\sin t,4t\rangle$, we have $\vec{v}=\langle-3\sin t,3\cos t,4\rangle$ and $\vec{a}=\langle-3\cos t,-3\sin t,0\rangle$. Speed is constant at $q=5$, so $a_T=0$. Since $\|\vec{a}\|=3$, we get $a_N=3$, $\vec{N}=\langle-\cos t,-\sin t,0\rangle$, and $\kappa=3/25$. Thus, velocity can change direction in 3D even at constant speed.
