## 多元函数偏导数简略笔记 / Brief Notes on Partial Derivatives of Multivariable Functions

### 1. 偏导数 / Partial Derivative

对于多元函数 $f(x,y,z,w)$，对某一个变量求偏导时，**只把这个变量看成变量，其他变量全部当作常数**。 / For a multivariable function $f(x,y,z,w)$, when taking a partial derivative with respect to one variable, **treat only that variable as a variable and hold all others constant**.

例如： / For example:

$$
f_z=\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}
$$

这表示只对 $z$ 求导，而把 $x,y,w$ 当作常数。 / This means differentiating only with respect to $z$ while treating $x,y,w$ as constants.

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}e^{xz+y}=xe^{xz+y}
$$

因为根据链式法则： / By the chain rule:

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}(xz+y)=x
$$

### 2. 原有的求导法则全部适用 / Ordinary Differentiation Rules Still Apply

**乘法法则 / Product rule:**

$$
(uv)'=u'v+uv'
$$

**商法法则 / Quotient rule:**

$$
\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)'=\frac{vu'-uv'}{v^2}
$$

**链式法则 / Chain rule:**

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{g(x)}=e^{g(x)}g'(x)
$$

唯一需要额外注意的是：**先确认现在对谁求导。** / The only extra consideration is to **first identify the variable with respect to which you are differentiating**.

### 3. 二阶偏导 / Second-Order Partial Derivatives

二阶偏导就是连续求两次偏导。 / A second-order partial derivative is obtained by taking partial derivatives twice in succession.

$$
f_{xx}=(f_x)_x
$$

即： / That is:

$$
f\xrightarrow{x}f_x\xrightarrow{x}f_{xx}
$$

例如，若 $f=x^2y^3$，则： / For example, if $f=x^2y^3$, then:

$$
f_x=2xy^3
$$

$$
\boxed{f_{xx}=2y^3}
$$

### 4. 混合偏导 $f_{xy}$ / Mixed Partial Derivative $f_{xy}$

下标记法可以理解为： / Subscript notation can be understood as:

$$
\boxed{f_{xy}=(f_x)_y}
$$

所以，**下标从左往右做**。 / Therefore, **evaluate subscripts from left to right**.

$$
f\xrightarrow{x}f_x\xrightarrow{y}f_{xy}
$$

例如，若 $f=x^2y^3$，先对 $x$ 求偏导： / For example, if $f=x^2y^3$, first differentiate with respect to $x$:

$$
f_x=2xy^3
$$

再对 $y$ 求偏导： / Then differentiate with respect to $y$:

$$
\boxed{f_{xy}=6xy^2}
$$

### 5. $\partial$ 分数记法的顺序 / Order in Fractional $\partial$ Notation

这一点最容易混淆： / This is the easiest point to confuse:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)}
$$

因此，**分母从右往左做**。 / Therefore, **evaluate the variables in the denominator from right to left**.

$$
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}:\qquad y\rightarrow x
$$

所以对应关系是： / Thus, the corresponding relationship is:

$$
\boxed{f_{xy}=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}}
$$

记忆： / Memory aid:

$$
\boxed{\text{下标从左往右，分母从右往左 / Subscripts: left to right; denominator: right to left}}
$$

### 6. 混合偏导通常相等 / Mixed Partial Derivatives Are Usually Equal

对于课程中常见的、足够光滑的函数： / For sufficiently smooth functions commonly encountered in this course:

$$
\boxed{f_{xy}=f_{yx}}
$$

因此： / Therefore:

$$
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}
$$

但做题时仍然要会正确读懂记号。 / Even so, you still need to interpret the notation correctly when solving problems.

### 一句话总结 / One-Sentence Summary

**偏导：选一个变量，其他当常数；二阶偏导：连续做两次；$f_{xy}$ 的下标从左往右，$\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}$ 的分母从右往左。** / **For partial derivatives, choose one variable and treat the others as constants; for second-order partial derivatives, differentiate twice; read the subscripts of $f_{xy}$ from left to right and the denominator of $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}$ from right to left.**

### 7. Clairaut 定理：混合偏导相等 / Clairaut's Theorem: Equality of Mixed Partials

如果 $f_{xy}$ 和 $f_{yx}$ 都存在，并且在圆盘区域 $D$ 上连续，那么对所有 $(a,b)\in D$： / If $f_{xy}$ and $f_{yx}$ both exist and are continuous on a disk $D$, then for every $(a,b)\in D$:

$$
f_{xy}(a,b)=f_{yx}(a,b)
$$

因此在 $D$ 上： / Therefore, on $D$:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}}
$$

更一般地，只要相关偏导数连续，就可以交换偏微分的顺序。 / More generally, the order of partial differentiation may be exchanged provided that the derivatives involved are continuous.

### 8. 聪明选择求导顺序 / Choosing the Order Wisely

设 / Let

$$
g(x,y,z,w)=x^3w^2z^2+\sin\left(\frac{xy}{z^2}\right),\qquad z\ne0.
$$

对于同时包含 $w$ 和 $y$ 的高阶混合偏导，不必按照题目给出的顺序硬算。由于在 $z\ne0$ 的区域内该函数光滑，Clairaut 定理允许我们交换求导顺序。 / For a higher-order mixed partial involving both $w$ and $y$, we do not have to differentiate in the order displayed. Since the function is smooth wherever $z\ne0$, Clairaut's theorem allows us to rearrange the order.

先对 $w$ 求偏导时，正弦项消失；再对 $y$ 求偏导时，剩余的多项式项也消失： / Differentiating first with respect to $w$ eliminates the sine term; differentiating next with respect to $y$ eliminates the remaining polynomial term:

$$
g_w=2x^3wz^2
$$

$$
(g_w)_y=0.
$$

后面无论再对哪些变量求偏导，结果仍为零。因此，图中所求的混合偏导为： / Any further partial derivatives remain zero. Hence the mixed partial requested in the example is:

$$
\boxed{g_{zzwwxy}=0}
$$

关键思路：先寻找能让某一项直接变成零的变量，再利用 Clairaut 定理调整求导顺序，可以避免不必要的链式法则计算。 / Key idea: first differentiate with respect to variables that make terms vanish, then use Clairaut's theorem to rearrange the order and avoid unnecessary chain-rule calculations.

### 9. 偏微分方程 / Partial Differential Equations

偏微分方程（PDE）是包含多元函数及其偏导数的微分方程。PDE 的解是满足该方程的函数。 / A partial differential equation (PDE) is a differential equation involving functions of several variables and their partial derivatives. A solution to a PDE is a function that satisfies the equation.

#### 热方程 / The Heat Equation

热方程是一种描述热量如何在物体中传播的 PDE。它有无穷多个解；具体解取决于物体的初始温度分布以及边界条件。 / The heat equation is a PDE that models how heat spreads through an object. It has infinitely many solutions; the particular solution depends on the object's initial temperature distribution and boundary conditions.

典型的一维热方程为： / A typical one-dimensional heat equation is:

$$
\boxed{u_t=ku_{xx}},\qquad k>0
$$

其中 $u(x,t)$ 表示金属棒在位置 $x$、时间 $t$ 的温度，$u_t$ 表示温度随时间的变化率，$u_{xx}$ 描述温度分布在空间中的弯曲程度，而常数 $k$ 是材料的热扩散率。 / Here, $u(x,t)$ is the temperature of the metal rod at position $x$ and time $t$; $u_t$ is the rate of change of temperature over time, $u_{xx}$ describes the spatial curvature of the temperature distribution, and the constant $k$ is the material's thermal diffusivity.

图中的情形是在 $t=0$ 时给金属棒的中心点一个瞬时热脉冲。之后热量从中心向外扩散，温度峰值逐渐降低，温度分布也随时间变得更加平缓。 / In the illustrated situation, the center of a metal rod receives an instantaneous burst of heat at $t=0$. The heat then diffuses outward, causing the temperature peak to decrease and the temperature profile to become smoother over time.
