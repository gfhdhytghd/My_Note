## 绘制梯度向量 / Drawing a Gradient Vector

对于二维标量函数 $f(x,y)$，梯度为： / For a two-dimensional scalar function $f(x,y)$, the gradient is:

$$
\nabla f(x,y)=\left\langle f_x(x,y),f_y(x,y)\right\rangle.
$$

在点 $P=(a,b)$ 处绘制梯度向量时，把向量的起点放在 $P$，然后按照分量 $\langle f_x(a,b),f_y(a,b)\rangle$ 决定水平方向和竖直方向。 / To draw the gradient vector at $P=(a,b)$, place its tail at $P$ and use the components $\langle f_x(a,b),f_y(a,b)\rangle$ to determine its horizontal and vertical directions.

### 从函数公式绘制 / Drawing from a Formula

1. 求偏导 $f_x$ 和 $f_y$。 / Find the partial derivatives $f_x$ and $f_y$.
2. 把点 $(a,b)$ 代入，得到 $\nabla f(a,b)$。 / Substitute $(a,b)$ to obtain $\nabla f(a,b)$.
3. 以 $(a,b)$ 为起点，按梯度的两个分量画箭头；可以按比例缩短箭头，但不能改变方向。 / Start the arrow at $(a,b)$ and draw it according to the two components; the arrow may be scaled for readability, but its direction must not change.

例如，设 $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$，在点 $P=(1,2)$ 处： / For example, let $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$. At $P=(1,2)$:

$$
\nabla f(x,y)=\langle2x,2y\rangle,
\qquad
\nabla f(1,2)=\langle2,4\rangle.
$$

因此从 $(1,2)$ 出发，向右 $2$、向上 $4$ 画箭头。这个向量与经过该点的等高线 $x^2+y^2=5$ 垂直，并指向函数值增大的方向。 / Therefore, draw an arrow from $(1,2)$ that goes $2$ units right and $4$ units up. It is perpendicular to the level curve $x^2+y^2=5$ through the point and points toward increasing function values.

### 从等高线图绘制 / Drawing from a Contour Map

- 梯度向量与经过该点的等高线垂直。 / The gradient vector is perpendicular to the contour through the point.
- 箭头指向等高线数值增大的方向。 / The arrow points toward contours with larger values.
- 等高线越密，函数变化越快，梯度通常越长；等高线越疏，梯度通常越短。 / Closer contours indicate faster change and usually a longer gradient; wider spacing indicates a smaller gradient.
- 如果只要求方向，可以画单位梯度向量 $\displaystyle\mathbf u=\frac{\nabla f}{\|\nabla f\|}$。 / If only the direction is required, draw the unit gradient vector $\displaystyle\mathbf u=\frac{\nabla f}{\|\nabla f\|}$.

### 关键理解 / Key Interpretation

$$
\boxed{\nabla f=\text{最陡上升方向 / direction of steepest increase}}
$$

其长度 $\|\nabla f\|$ 是该点的最大方向导数，也就是最快增长率。负梯度 $-\nabla f$ 指向最陡下降方向。若 $\nabla f=\mathbf0$，则没有唯一的最陡上升方向。 / Its magnitude $\|\nabla f\|$ is the maximum directional derivative, or the greatest rate of increase at that point. The negative gradient $-\nabla f$ points in the direction of steepest decrease. If $\nabla f=\mathbf0$, there is no unique direction of steepest increase.

常见错误：梯度不是沿着等高线画，而是与等高线垂直；也不要把箭头起点错误地放在原点，除非所求点本身就是原点。 / Common mistakes: the gradient is not tangent to a contour but perpendicular to it; also, do not place the tail at the origin unless the given point is the origin.

### 三变量函数的例子 / Example with a Three-Variable Function

设 / Let

$$
f(x,y,z)=x^2y+ye^{xz}+z^3.
$$

求函数在点 $P=(1,2,1)$ 处的梯度。 / Find the gradient of the function at $P=(1,2,1)$.

先分别对 $x$、$y$ 和 $z$ 求偏导；每次求导时把另外两个变量视为常数。 / First take the partial derivatives with respect to $x$, $y$, and $z$, treating the other two variables as constants each time.

$$
f_x=2xy+yz e^{xz},
$$

$$
f_y=x^2+e^{xz},
$$

$$
f_z=xy e^{xz}+3z^2.
$$

因此三维梯度为： / Therefore, the three-dimensional gradient is:

$$
\nabla f(x,y,z)
=\left\langle
2xy+yz e^{xz},
x^2+e^{xz},
xy e^{xz}+3z^2
\right\rangle.
$$

把 $P=(1,2,1)$ 代入，其中 $e^{xz}=e^{1\cdot1}=e$： / Substitute $P=(1,2,1)$, where $e^{xz}=e^{1\cdot1}=e$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla f(1,2,1)
&=\left\langle
2(1)(2)+2(1)e,
1^2+e,
(1)(2)e+3(1)^2
\right\rangle\\
&=\boxed{\left\langle4+2e,1+e,2e+3\right\rangle}.
\end{aligned}
$$

绘图时，把箭头的起点放在 $(1,2,1)$，并使箭头沿 $\langle4+2e,1+e,2e+3\rangle$ 的方向延伸。这个梯度垂直于经过 $P$ 的等值面 $f(x,y,z)=f(1,2,1)$，并指向函数值增长最快的方向。 / When drawing it, place the tail at $(1,2,1)$ and extend the arrow in the direction $\langle4+2e,1+e,2e+3\rangle$. This gradient is normal to the level surface $f(x,y,z)=f(1,2,1)$ through $P$ and points in the direction of steepest increase.

### 嵌套幂函数的梯度通式 / Gradient of a Nested Power Function

设 / Let

$$
g(x,y,z)=\left(ax^d+by^e+cz^f\right)^n,
$$

其中 $a,b,c,d,e,f,n$ 都是常数。令内层函数 / where $a,b,c,d,e,f,n$ are constants. Define the inner function

$$
u(x,y,z)=ax^d+by^e+cz^f,
$$

则 $g=u^n$。对每个变量求偏导时，都要先对外层求导，再乘以内层对该变量的偏导： / Then $g=u^n$. For each partial derivative, differentiate the outer function first and multiply by the derivative of the inner function with respect to that variable:

$$
\begin{aligned}
g_x
&=nu^{n-1}u_x\\
&=nadx^{d-1}\left(ax^d+by^e+cz^f\right)^{n-1},
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
g_y
&=nu^{n-1}u_y\\
&=nbey^{e-1}\left(ax^d+by^e+cz^f\right)^{n-1},
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
g_z
&=nu^{n-1}u_z\\
&=ncfz^{f-1}\left(ax^d+by^e+cz^f\right)^{n-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此，可以把共同因子提出来，得到梯度通式： / Therefore, factoring out the common term gives the general gradient formula:

$$
\boxed{
\nabla g
=n\left(ax^d+by^e+cz^f\right)^{n-1}
\left\langle
ad x^{d-1},
be y^{e-1},
cf z^{f-1}
\right\rangle
}.
$$

记忆方式：外层的 $n$ 降下来，整体次数变成 $n-1$，再分别乘以内层对 $x$、$y$、$z$ 的偏导。 / Memory aid: bring down the outer exponent $n$, reduce its power to $n-1$, and then multiply by the inner partial derivatives with respect to $x$, $y$, and $z$.

## 路径的链式法则 / Chain Rule for Paths

当函数 $f$ 沿参数路径 $\mathbf r(t)$ 变化时，会得到复合函数 $f(\mathbf r(t))$。在二维中，路径可写成 $\mathbf r(t)=\langle x(t),y(t)\rangle$；在三维中，可写成 $\mathbf r(t)=\langle x(t),y(t),z(t)\rangle$。 / When a function $f$ is evaluated along a parametric path $\mathbf r(t)$, it produces the composite function $f(\mathbf r(t))$. In two dimensions, write $\mathbf r(t)=\langle x(t),y(t)\rangle$; in three dimensions, write $\mathbf r(t)=\langle x(t),y(t),z(t)\rangle$.

如果 $f$ 和 $\mathbf r(t)$ 都可微，则路径链式法则为： / If $f$ and $\mathbf r(t)$ are differentiable, then the Chain Rule for Paths is:

$$
\boxed{
\frac{d}{dt}f(\mathbf r(t))
=\nabla f(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)
}.
$$

二维分量形式为： / In two-dimensional component form:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}f(x(t),y(t))
=f_x(x(t),y(t))x'(t)
+f_y(x(t),y(t))y'(t).
$$

三维分量形式为： / In three-dimensional component form:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}f(x(t),y(t),z(t))
=f_x\,x'(t)+f_y\,y'(t)+f_z\,z'(t),
$$

其中 $f_x$、$f_y$、$f_z$ 都在路径上的点 $(x(t),y(t),z(t))$ 处计算。这个公式表示：函数沿路径的瞬时变化率，等于该点的梯度与路径速度向量的点积。 / Here, $f_x$, $f_y$, and $f_z$ are evaluated at the point $(x(t),y(t),z(t))$ on the path. The formula says that the instantaneous rate of change of the function along the path equals the dot product of the gradient and the path's velocity vector.

### 例：昆虫沿路径运动时感受到的温度变化 / Example: Temperature Change Along a Bug's Path

位置 $(x,y)$ 处的温度为： / The temperature at location $(x,y)$ is:

$$
T(x,y)=20+10e^{-0.3(x^2+y^2)}\;{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$

一只昆虫沿路径 / A bug follows the path

$$
\mathbf r(t)=\langle\cos(t-2),\sin(2t)\rangle,
$$

其中 $t$ 的单位为秒。求它在 $t=0.6$ 秒时感受到的温度变化率。 / where $t$ is measured in seconds. Find the rate of change of temperature experienced by the bug at $t=0.6$ seconds.

首先求温度梯度： / First find the temperature gradient:

$$
\nabla T(x,y)
=\left\langle
-6xe^{-0.3(x^2+y^2)},
-6ye^{-0.3(x^2+y^2)}
\right\rangle.
$$

路径的速度向量为： / The velocity vector of the path is:

$$
\mathbf r'(t)=\langle-\sin(t-2),2\cos(2t)\rangle.
$$

由路径链式法则： / By the Chain Rule for Paths:

$$
\frac{dT}{dt}
=\nabla T(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t).
$$

先不要立刻代入 $t=0.6$。从路径中分别读出： / Do not substitute $t=0.6$ immediately. First read the coordinate functions from the path:

$$
x(t)=\cos(t-2),
\qquad
y(t)=\sin(2t).
$$

把这两个坐标代入 $\nabla T(x,y)$，得到路径上任意时刻的温度梯度： / Substitute these coordinates into $\nabla T(x,y)$ to obtain the temperature gradient along the path at any time:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla T(\mathbf r(t))
=\Bigl\langle
&-6\cos(t-2)e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]},\\
&-6\sin(2t)e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]}
\Bigr\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

现在将梯度和速度向量逐分量相乘。若 $\langle A,B\rangle\cdot\langle C,D\rangle=AC+BD$，则： / Now multiply the gradient and velocity vectors component by component. Since $\langle A,B\rangle\cdot\langle C,D\rangle=AC+BD$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dT}{dt}
={}&\Bigl[-6\cos(t-2)e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]}\Bigr]
\Bigl[-\sin(t-2)\Bigr]\\
&+\Bigl[-6\sin(2t)e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]}\Bigr]
\Bigl[2\cos(2t)\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
$$

整理正负号和常数后，才得到： / Only after simplifying the signs and constants do we obtain:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dT}{dt}
={}&6e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]}\cos(t-2)\sin(t-2)\\
&-12e^{-0.3[\cos^2(t-2)+\sin^2(2t)]}\sin(2t)\cos(2t).
\end{aligned}
$$

在 $t=0.6$ 时： / At $t=0.6$:

$$
\mathbf r(0.6)
=\langle\cos(-1.4),\sin(1.2)\rangle
\approx\langle0.16997,0.93204\rangle.
$$

最后把 $t=0.6$ 代入上式： / Finally, substitute $t=0.6$ into the expression above:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left.\frac{dT}{dt}\right|_{t=0.6}
={}&6e^{-0.3[\cos^2(-1.4)+\sin^2(1.2)]}\cos(-1.4)\sin(-1.4)\\
&-12e^{-0.3[\cos^2(-1.4)+\sin^2(1.2)]}\sin(1.2)\cos(1.2)\\
\approx{}&\boxed{-3.864\;{}^\circ\mathrm C/\mathrm s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

负号表示昆虫在 $t=0.6$ 秒时正移动到温度较低的位置，即它感受到的温度正在下降。 / The negative sign means that at $t=0.6$ seconds, the bug is moving toward a cooler location, so the temperature it experiences is decreasing.
