# 经济学研究方法 / Economic Research Methods

## 1. 科学方法在经济学中的应用 / Application of Scientific Method in Economics

经济学作为一门社会科学，采用科学方法来检验理论和模型。  
Economics, as a social science, applies the scientific method to test theories and models.

### 科学方法的四个步骤 / Four Steps of Scientific Method

1. **观察现象 / Observe a Phenomenon**  
   观察经济现象和行为模式  
   Observe economic phenomena and behavioral patterns

2. **提出假设 / Develop Hypothesis**  
   基于观察，研究并提出假设，即对该现象的解释  
   Based on observations, research and develop a hypothesis, which is an explanation for that phenomenon

3. **构建模型 / Construct Model**  
   构建数学或理论模型以检验假设  
   Construct mathematical or theoretical models to test the hypothesis

4. **设计实验 / Design Experiments**  
   设计实验来测试模型的有效性。收集数据后，可以验证、修正或否定假设  
   Design experiments to test how well the model works. After collecting data, one can verify, revise, or refute the hypothesis

---
## 5. 经济学变量类型 / Types of Economic Variables

- **内生变量 / Endogenous Variables**  
  在模型中被控制或由模型内部决定的变量  
  Variables that are controlled for or determined within the model

- **外生变量 / Exogenous Variables**  
  在模型之外，由外部因素决定的变量  
  Variables that are determined outside the model by external factors
