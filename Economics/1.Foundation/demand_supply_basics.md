# 供需基础 / Demand and Supply Basics

## 1. 需求 / Demand

### 1.1 需求定义 / Definition of Demand

**需求**是在特定价格水平下，消费者愿意且能够购买的商品或服务数量。  
**Demand** is the quantity of goods or services that consumers are willing and able to purchase at specific price levels.

### 1.2 需求定律 / Law of Demand

在其他条件不变的情况下，商品价格与需求量呈反向关系。  
Holding other factors constant, there is an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded.

$$P \uparrow \Rightarrow Q_d \downarrow$$
$$P \downarrow \Rightarrow Q_d \uparrow$$

### 1.3 需求函数 / Demand Function

$$Q_d = f(P, \text{Income}, \text{Prices of related goods}, \text{Tastes}, \text{Expectations}, \text{Number of buyers})$$

**简化形式** / **Simplified form:**
$$Q_d = a - bP$$

其中：
- $a$ = 截距（价格为零时的需求量）/ Intercept (quantity when price is zero)
- $b$ = 斜率（需求曲线斜率）/ Slope (demand curve slope)

---

## 2. 供给 / Supply

### 2.1 供给定义 / Definition of Supply

**供给**是在特定价格水平下，生产者愿意且能够提供的商品或服务数量。  
**Supply** is the quantity of goods or services that producers are willing and able to provide at specific price levels.

### 2.2 供给定律 / Law of Supply

在其他条件不变的情况下，商品价格与供给量呈正向关系。  
Holding other factors constant, there is a direct relationship between price and quantity supplied.

$$P \uparrow \Rightarrow Q_s \uparrow$$
$$P \downarrow \Rightarrow Q_s \downarrow$$

### 2.3 供给函数 / Supply Function

$$Q_s = f(P, \text{Input prices}, \text{Technology}, \text{Expectations}, \text{Number of sellers})$$

**简化形式** / **Simplified form:**
$$Q_s = c + dP$$

其中：
- $c$ = 截距 / Intercept
- $d$ = 斜率（供给曲线斜率）/ Slope (supply curve slope)

---

## 3. 市场均衡 / Market Equilibrium

### 3.1 均衡定义 / Equilibrium Definition

**均衡**是需求量和供给量相等时的状态。  
**Equilibrium** occurs when quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

$$Q_d = Q_s$$

### 3.2 均衡价格与数量 / Equilibrium Price and Quantity

**均衡价格** / **Equilibrium Price ($P^*$):**
$$P^* = \frac{a - c}{b + d}$$

**均衡数量** / **Equilibrium Quantity ($Q^*$):**
$$Q^* = a - bP^* = c + dP^*$$

### 3.3 均衡条件 / Equilibrium Conditions

- **均衡时**：$Q_d = Q_s$ / **At equilibrium**: $Q_d = Q_s$
- **过剩**：$Q_s > Q_d$ / **Surplus**: $Q_s > Q_d$  
- **短缺**：$Q_d > Q_s$ / **Shortage**: $Q_d > Q_s$

---

## 4. 需求与供给的变动 / Shifts in Demand and Supply

### 4.1 需求变动 / Demand Shifts

**需求增加**（曲线右移）/ **Demand increases** (curve shifts right):
- 收入增加（正常品）/ Income increase (normal goods)
- 替代品价格上升 / Substitute price rises
- 互补品价格下降 / Complement price falls
- 偏好变化 / Taste changes
- 预期价格上升 / Expected price rises

**需求减少**（曲线左移）/ **Demand decreases** (curve shifts left):
- 收入减少（正常品）/ Income decrease (normal goods)
- 替代品价格下降 / Substitute price falls
- 互补品价格上升 / Complement price rises

### 4.2 供给变动 / Supply Shifts

**供给增加**（曲线右移）/ **Supply increases** (curve shifts right):
- 投入品价格下降 / Input price falls
- 技术进步 / Technology improvement
- 预期价格下降 / Expected price falls
- 生产者数量增加 / Number of producers increases

**供给减少**（曲线左移）/ **Supply decreases** (curve shifts left):
- 投入品价格上升 / Input price rises
- 技术退步 / Technology deterioration
- 预期价格上升 / Expected price rises

## 5. 供需分析应用 / Applications of Demand-Supply Analysis

### 5.1 价格管制 / Price Controls

#### 价格上限 / Price Ceiling
- 设定低于均衡价格的价格上限 / Set below equilibrium price
- 导致短缺 / Creates shortage
- 如租金管制 / Examples: rent control

#### 价格下限 / Price Floor  
- 设定高于均衡价格的价格下限 / Set above equilibrium price
- 导致过剩 / Creates surplus
- 如最低工资 / Examples: minimum wage

### 5.2 税收影响 / Tax Effects

**税收对均衡的影响** / **Tax effects on equilibrium:**

**从量税** / **Specific tax:**
$$P_{\text{after tax}} = P_{\text{before tax}} + t$$

**税收负担分配** / **Tax burden distribution:**
- 需求弹性越小，消费者负担越重 / Less elastic demand → consumers bear more burden
- 供给弹性越小，生产者负担越重 / Less elastic supply → producers bear more burden

## 6. 弹性与供需 / Elasticity and Demand-Supply

### 6.1 需求弹性 / Demand Elasticity

$$E_d = \frac{\%\Delta Q_d}{\%\Delta P}$$

**弹性类型** / **Elasticity types:**
- $|E_d| > 1$：富有弹性 / Elastic
- $|E_d| = 1$：单位弹性 / Unit elastic  
- $|E_d| < 1$：缺乏弹性 / Inelastic

### 6.2 供给弹性 / Supply Elasticity

$$E_s = \frac{\%\Delta Q_s}{\%\Delta P}$$

**影响因素** / **Determining factors:**
- 时间跨度 / Time horizon
- 生产能力 / Production capacity
- 投入品可获得性 / Input availability

---

## 7. 消费者剩余与生产者剩余 / Consumer and Producer Surplus

### 7.1 消费者剩余 / Consumer Surplus

**定义**：消费者愿意支付的价格与实际支付价格之间的差额。  
**Definition**: The difference between what consumers are willing to pay and what they actually pay.

$$CS = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times \text{Height} = \frac{1}{2} \times Q^* \times (P_{\max} - P^*)$$

### 7.2 生产者剩余 / Producer Surplus

**定义**：生产者实际收到的价格与愿意接受的最低价格之间的差额。  
**Definition**: The difference between what producers receive and the minimum price they would accept.

$$PS = \frac{1}{2} \times Q^* \times (P^* - P_{\min})$$

### 7.3 总剩余 / Total Surplus

$$TS = CS + PS$$

---

## 8. 市场效率 / Market Efficiency

### 8.1 帕累托效率 / Pareto Efficiency

**定义**：在均衡状态下，无法在不损害他人利益的情况下改善某人的福利。  
**Definition**: At equilibrium, it's impossible to make someone better off without making someone else worse off.

### 8.2 市场失灵 / Market Failure

**原因** / **Causes:**
- 外部性 / Externalities
- 公共物品 / Public goods
- 信息不对称 / Information asymmetry
- 市场势力 / Market power
