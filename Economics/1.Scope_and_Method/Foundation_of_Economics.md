# 经济学基础 / Foundation of Economics


## F1. 激励很重要 / Incentives Matter

激励机制是经济活动的重要驱动力，人们会根据激励做出相应的行为调整。  
Incentive mechanisms are important driving forces of economic activities, and people will adjust their behavior accordingly based on incentives.

### 激励的作用 / Role of Incentives

激励影响人们的选择和行为，是理解经济现象的关键因素。  
Incentives influence people's choices and behaviors, and are key factors in understanding economic phenomena.

---

## F2. 生活就是权衡取舍 / Life is About Trade-offs

在资源稀缺的情况下，任何选择都意味着放弃其他选项。经济学的核心就是研究如何在有限资源下做出最优选择。  
In situations of scarce resources, any choice means giving up other options. The core of economics is studying how to make optimal choices with limited resources.

### 历史案例：艾森豪威尔总统的例子 / Historical Example: President Eisenhower's Example

艾森豪威尔总统曾经指出，一个重型轰炸机的成本相当于：  
President Eisenhower once pointed out that the cost of a heavy bomber could equal:

- 30 个城市各建一所学校  
  A school in each of 30 cities
- 2 家设备齐全的医院  
  2 fully equipped hospitals  
- 50 英里的水泥公路  
  50 miles of concrete highway

这个例子说明了资源配置的机会成本问题。  
This example illustrates the opportunity cost problem in resource allocation.

## F3. 机会成本 / Opportunity Cost

### 定义 / Definition

机会成本是指为了进行某项活动而必须放弃的价值最高的其他选择。  
Opportunity cost is the highest-valued alternative that must be given up to engage in an activity.

### 关键概念 / Key Concepts

- 机会成本不是所有被放弃的选择，而是其中价值最高的那一个  
  Opportunity cost is not all the alternatives given up, but the highest-valued one among them

- 机会成本总是存在的，因为资源是稀缺的  
  Opportunity cost always exists because resources are scarce

### 实例分析 / Example Analysis

**个人时间分配 / Personal Time Allocation**

如果你用2小时去看电影，你的机会成本可能是：  
If you spend 2 hours watching a movie, your opportunity cost might be:

- 用这段时间学习获得的知识提升  
  The knowledge improvement from studying during this time
- 兼职工作获得的收入  
  The income from part-time work
- 与朋友聚会获得的社交价值  
  The social value from gathering with friends

其中价值最高的那个选择就是你的机会成本。  
The highest-valued option among these is your opportunity cost.

## F4. 边际思维 / Marginal Thinking

### 定义 / Definition

边际思维是指评估再获得一单位某物时，其带来的收益是否大于所付出的成本。  
Marginal thinking is evaluating whether the benefit of acquiring one more unit of something is greater than the cost.

### 核心原理 / Core Principle

理性决策者只有在边际收益大于边际成本时才会采取行动。  
Rational decision-makers take action only when marginal benefit exceeds marginal cost.

$$\text{边际收益} > \text{边际成本} \Rightarrow \text{采取行动}$$
$$\text{Marginal Benefit} > \text{Marginal Cost} \Rightarrow \text{Take Action}$$

### 应用实例 / Application Examples

1. **购买教材 / Purchase a Textbook**  
   评估购买一本额外教材的边际收益与边际成本  
   Evaluate the marginal benefit versus marginal cost of purchasing an additional textbook

2. **企业操纵 / Corporate Manipulation**  
   公司操纵用户心智和边际思维  
   Companies manipulate consumer's marginal thinking
