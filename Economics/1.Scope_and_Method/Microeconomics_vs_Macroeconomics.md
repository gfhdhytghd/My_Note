# 微观经济学与宏观经济学 / Microeconomics vs Macroeconomics

## 定义 / Definitions

### 微观经济学 / Microeconomics

微观经济学研究单个经济主体（如个人、家庭、企业）的行为及其在市场中的相互作用。  
Microeconomics studies the behavior of individual economic agents (such as individuals, households, and firms) and their interactions in markets.

### 宏观经济学 / Macroeconomics

宏观经济学关注整个经济体的总体现象，如国民收入、失业、通货膨胀和经济增长。  
Macroeconomics focuses on aggregate phenomena of the whole economy, such as national income, unemployment, inflation, and economic growth.

## 比较 / Comparison

|  | 微观经济学 / Microeconomics | 宏观经济学 / Macroeconomics |
|---|---|---|
| 研究对象 / Object | 个体、企业、市场 / Individuals, firms, markets | 整体经济 / The whole economy |
| 关注点 / Focus | 价格、供求、资源配置 / Prices, supply and demand, resource allocation | 总产出、失业、通胀 / Aggregate output, unemployment, inflation |
