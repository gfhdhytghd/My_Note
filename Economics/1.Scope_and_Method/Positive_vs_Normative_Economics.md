# 实证经济学与规范经济学 / Positive vs Normative Economics

## 定义 / Definitions

### 实证经济学 / Positive Economics

实证经济研究描述和解释经济现象，关注"是什么"，基于事实和数据。  
Positive economics describes and explains economic phenomena, focusing on "what is" and is based on facts and data.

### 规范经济学 / Normative Economics

规范性经济研究涉及价值判断，关注"应该是什么"，提出政策建议或主张。  
Normative economics involves value judgments, focusing on "what ought to be" and makes policy recommendations or proposals.

## 比较 / Comparison

|  | 实证经济学 / Positive Economics | 规范性经济学 / Normative Economics |
|---|---|---|
| 关注点 / Focus | 事实、因果关系 / Facts, causality | 价值判断、政策建议 / Value judgments, policy recommendations |
| 例子 / Example | "提高最低工资会影响就业。" / "Raising minimum wage affects employment." | "应该提高最低工资以减少贫困。" / "Minimum wage should be raised to reduce poverty." |
