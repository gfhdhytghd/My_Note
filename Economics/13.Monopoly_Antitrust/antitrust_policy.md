# 反垄断政策 / Antitrust Policy

- 定义：政府通过法律与监管来防止企业获取或滥用市场力量（market power）。/ Definition: Government laws and regulation to prevent firms from acquiring or abusing market power.
- 目的：维护有效竞争、提高配置效率、保护消费者福利并促进创新。/ Purpose: Preserve effective competition, improve allocative efficiency, protect consumer welfare, and foster innovation.

## 政策工具 / Policy Instruments

- 拆分与重组 / Breakups and restructuring：对占支配地位的企业实施结构性补救（如历史上的 AT&T）。/ Structural remedies for dominant firms (e.g., AT&T breakup).
- 并购审查 / Merger review：阻止会“显著减少竞争”的并购。/ Block mergers that would substantially lessen competition.
- 行为补救 / Conduct remedies：禁止搭售、排他协议、掠夺性定价等排他性行为。/ Prohibit tying, exclusivity, predatory pricing, and other exclusionary conduct.
- 价格与接入监管 / Price and access regulation：对自然垄断实施价格上限、强制开放接入。/ For natural monopolies, impose price caps or mandate access.
- 开放市场 / Open markets：降低进入与贸易壁垒，引入外部竞争。/ Lower entry and trade barriers to invite outside competition.

## 注意事项 / Notes

- 自然垄断监管权衡：价格过低会削弱维护与投资激励，影响长期供给；参见：[[Economics/13.Monopoly_Antitrust/monopoly_characteristics]]. / For natural monopolies, overly low caps may undercut maintenance and investment; see [[Economics/13.Monopoly_Antitrust/monopoly_characteristics]].
- 不作为选项 / Do-nothing option：在信息不足或创新快速的行业，谨慎干预以避免抑制动态效率。/ In fast‑moving sectors or under high uncertainty, light‑touch or no intervention can avoid harming dynamic efficiency.
