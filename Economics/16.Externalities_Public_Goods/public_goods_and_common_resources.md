# 公共品与公地 / Public Goods & Commons

## 商品特性与分类 / Characteristics & Classification
- 可竞争（Rival）：消费一单位会削弱他人可得数量或效用，多人需竞争使用权。  Rival goods force consumers to compete for the same unit of consumption.
- 可排他（Excludable）：供应方能以价格、会员或许可阻止未付费者进入。  Excludable goods let providers block access via price, membership, or licensing.
- 分类速览 / Quick matrix：
  - 可竞争且可排他 → 私人品（Private goods）：冰淇淋、披萨、住房。  Ex: Ice cream, pizza, housing.
  - 可竞争但不可排他 → 公共资源/公地（Common resources）：海洋鱼类、森林树木。  Ex: Fish stocks, timber in commons.
  - 不可竞争但可排他 → 俱乐部品（Club goods）：俱乐部会员、卫星电视、未拥挤演唱会。  Ex: Clubs, satellite TV, uncrowded concerts.
  - 不可竞争且不可排他 → 公共品（Public goods）：公园、灯塔、开阔海洋。  Ex: Parks, lighthouses, open oceans.
- 拥挤效应 / Congestion note：理论上不可竞争的商品在空间紧缺时会转为“拥挤”而具竞争性，例如超火演唱会 vs. 小型本地乐队表演。  Even nonrival goods become rival once space is scarce (Taylor Swift tour vs. local gig).

## 公共品困境 / Public Goods Problem
- 公共品因不可排他、不可竞争而存在系统性的搭便车，供给量低于社会最优。  Non-excludable, nonrival goods induce chronic free riding so supply falls short of the social optimum.
- 公共品博弈的纳什均衡通常是所有参与者“留牌”不贡献，复制了囚徒困境结构。  In standard public-goods games the Nash equilibrium is to keep both cards, mirroring a prisoner’s dilemma.
- 未捐献者共享公共品却不付费即为搭便车，如街头艺人旁观者享受演出却不打赏。  People who refuse to donate while still enjoying the benefit—audiences for buskers not tipping—are free riders.
- 更多外部性图景参见 [[Economics/16.Externalities_Public_Goods/social_costs_and_externalities]]。  See [[Economics/16.Externalities_Public_Goods/social_costs_and_externalities]] for the broader externality framework.

## 公地悲剧 / Tragedy of the Commons
- 公共资源人人可用但难以排他，单个决策者忽视对资源总量的边际损耗，导致过度使用与维护不足。  Shared yet non-excludable resources invite overuse and underinvestment because individuals ignore marginal depletion.
- 案例：空气污染、海洋污染、电影院无限续杯爆米花等都展示了“人人的便宜”引发的浪费与退化。  Examples include air pollution, ocean dumping, and unlimited popcorn refills that encourage waste.
- 政策可通过产权界定、限额、定价或社区规范来让使用者内部化成本。  Solutions assign property rights, set caps, charge access fees, or enforce community norms to internalize costs.
- 回收与押金返还制度把外部损害货币化，是缓解公地悲剧与搭便车的常见工具。  Recycling programs and deposit-refund schemes monetize external damage and reduce overuse.

## 政策与激励案例 / Policy & Incentive Examples
- 俄勒冈州 1971 年对啤酒与汽水瓶设 \$0.05 押金，形成“退瓶得钱”的正向激励，显著提升清洁率。  Oregon’s 1971 bottle bill added a \$0.05 refund, giving people a cash incentive to return containers.
- 新墨西哥州圣菲市实施分级水价，最高用水者每加仑支付约 4× 于低用水者，以价格机制节约稀缺资源。  Santa Fe, NM uses tiered pricing so heavy water users pay roughly four times as much per gallon, rationing scarcity through price.

## TPS 公共品仿真游戏 / TPS Public Goods Simulation
- 每队持有两张卡，可选择保留或捐赠，并需私下写下策略防止信息外泄。  Each team gets two cards to keep or donate and records the choice privately.
- 留下一张卡可得 \$30 私人收益；每捐出一张卡会投入公共池，按 \$60 计值。  Keeping a card yields \$30 privately, while donating adds \$60 to the public pot.
- 公共池总额在所有队伍之间平均分配，即使未贡献者也可分享，使得搭便车成为占优策略。  The pot is split equally across teams, so even non-contributors share the payout—free riding becomes dominant.
- 策略集合包括“全留”“全捐”“各一”，教学上突出个人理性与集体福利冲突。  Strategies span keep-both, donate-both, or split choices, highlighting the clash between individual incentives and group welfare.
