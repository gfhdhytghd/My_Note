# 基尼系数与洛伦兹曲线 / Gini Coefficient and Lorenz Curve

## 1. 直观含义 / Intuition

基尼系数用于衡量收入（或财富）分配的不平等程度：越接近0越平等，越接近1越集中。  
The Gini coefficient measures inequality in income (or wealth): closer to 0 means more equal, closer to 1 means more concentrated.

## 2. 定义与公式 / Definition and Formulas

以洛伦兹曲线$L(p)$与完全平等线之间的面积表示：  
Defined using the Lorenz curve $L(p)$ relative to perfect equality:

$$
G=1-2\int_0^1 L(p)\,dp
$$

也可用离散形式计算（$x_i$为按从低到高排序的收入，$\mu$为平均收入）：  
Discrete forms (with $x_i$ sorted ascending and $\mu$ mean income):

$$
G = \frac{1}{n\mu} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (2i-n-1)x_i
$$

$$
G = \frac{1}{2n^2\mu} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} |x_i-x_j|
$$

## 3. 度量注意事项 / Measurement Notes

- 口径一致：税前/税后、个人/家庭、等价收入调整必须明确，否则跨国或跨期比较会失真。  
  Consistency matters: pre/post-tax, individual/household, and equivalence adjustments affect comparisons.
- 解读要结合分位点：基尼下降可能来自底层上升或顶层受冲击，应配合分位点收入与政策背景。  
  Interpret with quantiles: a falling Gini can reflect bottom gains or top losses; use quantile data and context.
