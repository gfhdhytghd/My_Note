# 实物转移 / In-Kind Transfers

## 1. 定义与例子 / Definition and Examples

实物转移是政府以“指定用途”的物品或凭证形式提供福利（如食品券、住房券、医保、托育名额），限制用于特定消费。  
In-kind transfers are government benefits provided as goods or earmarked vouchers (food stamps, housing vouchers, health insurance, childcare slots), restricting spending to specific categories.

## 2. 福利与效率 / Welfare and Efficiency

- 优点：保障最低消费，减少对现金“滥用”的担忧。  
  Pros: ensures minimum consumption and reduces concerns about misuse of cash.
- 代价：当受益者更偏好现金时，会产生“效用损失”（限制带来的不便）。  
  Cost: generates utility loss if recipients would prefer cash.

## 3. 外部性与政治可行性 / Externalities and Political Economy

对教育、疫苗等正外部性品类，实物转移可确保资源投向并提高公众支持度。  
For goods with positive externalities (education, vaccines), in-kind provision helps ensure spending and often increases political support.

## 4. 激励与扭曲 / Incentives and Distortions

相较等额现金，实物转移往往更少削弱劳动供给，但资格线仍可能形成“收入悬崖”。  
Compared with cash, in-kind benefits often distort labor supply less, though eligibility cliffs can still occur.

## 5. 供给形式 / Delivery Forms

- 凭证型：给予家庭一定选择权（如住房券、学券）。  
  Voucher-style: grants some choice (housing/school vouchers).
- 直接供给型：政府直接供给更易控成本，但可能排队、质量不均。  
  Direct provision: controls cost but may create queues and uneven quality.
