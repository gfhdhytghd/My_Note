# 完全竞争市场中的税收 / Taxation in Perfect Competition

## 1. 完全竞争市场的基本特征 / Basic Features of Perfect Competition

- 买卖双方数量众多  
  Large number of buyers and sellers
- 自由进入与退出市场  
  Free entry and exit from the market
- 商品同质、相似  
  Homogeneous and similar products
- 本章重点：分析税收在完全竞争市场中的影响  
  Focus: analyzing the impact of taxation in perfectly competitive markets

## 2. 税收类型与基本概念 / Tax Types and Basic Concepts

**只考虑"单位税"（Per-unit tax）**：对每售出1单位商品征税。  
Only consider "per-unit tax": tax levied on each unit of goods sold.

### 关键术语 / Key Terms

**1. Levy（法定义务）**  
- 谁在法律上需要缴税（政府可以规定由供给方或需求方承担）。  
- Who is legally required to pay the tax (government can specify suppliers or demanders).

**2. Incidence（实际负担）**  
- 谁实际上承担税收。  
- Who actually bears the tax burden.
- 不论税收加在供给还是需求方，买卖双方都会分担，部分负担会被转嫁。  
- Regardless of whether tax is imposed on supply or demand, both buyers and sellers share the burden, with some burden being shifted.
- 税负分担比例取决于供需弹性：需求越缺乏弹性，消费者承担的税负越多；供给越缺乏弹性，生产者承担的税负越多。  
- The division of tax burden depends on elasticity: the less elastic the demand, the more tax is borne by consumers; the less elastic the supply, the more tax is borne by producers.

**3. Deadweight Loss (DWL，无谓损失)**  
- 税收减少了交易量，导致经济活动下降，总剩余减少。  
- Taxation reduces transaction volume, leading to decreased economic activity and reduced total surplus.

## 3. 税收在竞争市场的效果 / Effects of Taxation in Competitive Markets

- 在竞争市场里，税收几乎总会造成**无谓损失**。  
- In competitive markets, taxation almost always creates **deadweight loss**.
- 政府征税的动机：  
- Government motives for taxation:
  1. **财政收入**（税收收入会计入社会总剩余）  
     **Fiscal revenue** (tax revenue is included in total social surplus)
  2. **改变消费行为**：如香烟的消费税，用来抑制不良消费  
     **Modify consumption behavior**: e.g., cigarette excise tax to discourage harmful consumption

### 总剩余公式 / Total Surplus Formula

$$
TS = CS + PS + Tax\ Revenue
$$

## 4. 数值与图形例子（$5单位税）/ Numerical and Graphical Example ($5 Per-unit Tax)

税收加在供给方，供给曲线向上平移。  
Tax imposed on suppliers, supply curve shifts upward.

### 税前 / Before Tax

- 交易量：60  
  Transaction quantity: 60
- 均衡价格：20  
  Equilibrium price: 20
- **消费者剩余（CS）**：$(30-20) \times 60 / 2 = 360$  
  **Consumer Surplus (CS)**: $(30-20) \times 60 / 2 = 360$
- **生产者剩余（PS）**：$(20-0) \times 60 / 2 = 540$  
  **Producer Surplus (PS)**: $(20-0) \times 60 / 2 = 540$
- **总剩余（TS）**：360 + 540 = 900  
  **Total Surplus (TS)**: 360 + 540 = 900

### 税后 / After Tax

- 交易量：50  
  Transaction quantity: 50
- 买方支付价格：20（含税前价格）  
  Price paid by buyers: 20 (including pre-tax price)
- 卖方获得价格：15（税后价格）  
  Price received by sellers: 15 (after-tax price)
- **消费者剩余（CS）**：$(30-20) \times 50 / 2 = 250$  
  **Consumer Surplus (CS)**: $(30-20) \times 50 / 2 = 250$
- **生产者剩余（PS）**：$(15-0) \times 50 / 2 = 375$  
  **Producer Surplus (PS)**: $(15-0) \times 50 / 2 = 375$
- **税收收入（TR）**：5 × 50 = 250  
  **Tax Revenue (TR)**: 5 × 50 = 250
- **总剩余（TS）**：250 + 375 + 250 = 875  
  **Total Surplus (TS)**: 250 + 375 + 250 = 875

### 无谓损失 / Deadweight Loss

$$
DWL = TS_{before} - TS_{after} = 900 - 875 = 25
$$

## 5. 税负分担（Tax Incidence）/ Tax Burden Sharing

- 消费者承担：价格从20涨到19（多付1元），占税收的40%  
- Consumer burden: price rises from 20 to 19 (pay 1 more), accounting for 40% of tax
- 生产者承担：价格从20降到15（少得4元），占税收的60%  
- Producer burden: price falls from 20 to 15 (receive 4 less), accounting for 60% of tax

## 6. 总结 / Summary

1. 单位税会减少交易量，带来无谓损失。  
   Per-unit tax reduces transaction volume and creates deadweight loss.
2. 法律上的纳税责任（levy）≠ 实际的税负承担（incidence）。  
   Legal tax liability (levy) ≠ actual tax burden (incidence).
3. 税收负担由买卖双方共同承担，比例取决于供需弹性。  
   Tax burden is shared by both buyers and sellers, with proportions depending on supply and demand elasticity.
4. 政府征税的主要动机是筹集财政收入和调节消费。  
   Main government motives for taxation are raising fiscal revenue and regulating consumption.
5. 总剩余计算公式：  
   Total surplus calculation formula:

$$
TS = CS + PS + TR
$$
