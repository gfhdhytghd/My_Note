# 税收对供需曲线的影响 / The Impact of Taxes on Supply and Demand Curves

**基本概念 / Basic Concept**  
征税会影响市场价格与交易数量，改变供需曲线的位置。  
Taxation affects market prices and quantity traded, shifting the supply or demand curve.

## 1. 对供给方征税 / Tax on Suppliers

- 征税使得每售出一单位商品，生产者需上缴一定税额。  
- Tax on suppliers means producers must pay a certain amount per unit sold.
- 供给曲线整体向上（或左）平移，幅度等于单位税额。  
- The supply curve shifts upward (or left) by the amount of the tax.
- 市场均衡价格上升，均衡数量下降。  
- Equilibrium price rises, equilibrium quantity falls.
- 部分税负由消费者承担（价格上涨），部分由生产者承担（净收入减少）。  
- The tax burden is shared: consumers pay higher prices, producers receive less net revenue.

## 2. 对需求方征税 / Tax on Consumers

- 征税使得每购买一单位商品，消费者需上缴一定税额。  
- Tax on consumers means buyers must pay a certain amount per unit purchased.
- 需求曲线整体向下（或左）平移，幅度等于单位税额。  
- The demand curve shifts downward (or left) by the amount of the tax.
- 市场均衡价格下降（对卖方），均衡数量下降。  
- Equilibrium price received by sellers falls, equilibrium quantity falls.
- 部分税负由生产者承担（收入减少），部分由消费者承担（实际支付总价上升）。  
- The tax burden is shared: producers receive less, consumers pay more in total.

## 3. 税收归宿 / Tax Incidence

- 税收归宿指税负在买卖双方之间的实际分担。  
- Tax incidence refers to how the tax burden is divided between buyers and sellers.
- 取决于供需弹性：  
- Depends on elasticity:
  - 需求较不弹性，消费者承担更多税负。  
    If demand is inelastic, consumers bear more of the tax.
  - 供给较不弹性，生产者承担更多税负。  
    If supply is inelastic, producers bear more of the tax.

## 4. 税收对总剩余的影响 / Tax Effect on Total Surplus

- 税收导致总剩余减少，产生无谓损失（deadweight loss）。  
- Taxes reduce total surplus, creating deadweight loss.
- 无谓损失等于因税收导致的交易减少部分的净福利损失。  
- Deadweight loss equals the net welfare loss from reduced transactions due to the tax.
