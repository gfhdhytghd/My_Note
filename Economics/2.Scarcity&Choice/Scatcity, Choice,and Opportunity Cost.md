# 稀缺性、选择与机会成本 / Scarcity, Choice, and Opportunity Cost

## 2.1 稀缺性的本质 / Nature of Scarcity

### 定义 / Definition

稀缺性是指人们的欲望是无限的，但用来满足这些欲望的资源是有限的。  
Scarcity means that human wants are unlimited while the resources to satisfy these wants are limited.

### 稀缺性的普遍存在 / Universal Existence of Scarcity

- **个人层面** / Individual Level：时间、金钱、精力有限  
  Time, money, and energy are limited
- **企业层面** / Corporate Level：劳动力、资本、原材料有限  
  Labor, capital, and raw materials are limited  
- **社会层面** / Social Level：土地、自然资源、技术能力有限  
  Land, natural resources, and technological capabilities are limited

---

## 2.2 选择的必然性 / Necessity of Choice

### 为什么必须选择 / Why Choice is Necessary

由于稀缺性的存在，我们无法同时拥有所有想要的东西，因此必须在不同选项之间做出选择。  
Due to scarcity, we cannot have everything we want simultaneously, so we must make choices among different options.

### 选择的特点 / Characteristics of Choice

1. **排他性** / Exclusivity：选择一个就意味着放弃其他  
   Choosing one means giving up others
2. **比较性** / Comparative：需要比较不同选项的价值  
   Need to compare the value of different options
3. **个人性** / Personal：每个人的偏好和价值判断不同  
   Each person has different preferences and value judgments
