# 比较优势 / Comparative Advantage

## 2.1 比较优势的定义 / Definition of Comparative Advantage

比较优势是指一个经济体在生产某种商品时，相对于其他经济体具有更低机会成本的能力。  
Comparative advantage refers to the ability of an economic entity to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost than other entities.

### 核心要点 / Key Points

- 比较优势强调的是机会成本而非绝对成本  
  Comparative advantage emphasizes opportunity cost rather than absolute cost
- 即使一个国家在所有商品上都没有绝对优势，仍可能拥有比较优势  
  Even if a country has no absolute advantage in any good, it may still have comparative advantage

---

## 2.2 数学表达 / Mathematical Expression

若国家A生产商品X的机会成本低于国家B，则A在X上具有比较优势。  
If country A has a lower opportunity cost of producing good X than country B, then A has a comparative advantage in X.

$$
\text{比较优势条件：} \quad OC_A^X < OC_B^X
$$

其中$OC$表示机会成本。  
Where $OC$ denotes opportunity cost.

### 机会成本计算 / Opportunity Cost Calculation

$$
OC_A^X = \frac{\text{A国生产X时放弃的Y数量}}{\text{A国生产的X数量}}
$$

$$
OC_A^X = \frac{\text{Quantity of Y given up by country A}}{\text{Quantity of X produced by country A}}
$$

---

## 2.3 实例分析 / Example Analysis

### 基本例子 / Basic Example

- 国家A生产1单位X需放弃2单位Y，国家B需放弃3单位Y，则A在X上有比较优势  
  If country A gives up 2 units of Y to produce 1 unit of X, and country B gives up 3 units of Y, then A has a comparative advantage in X

### 数值示例 / Numerical Example

| 国家/Country | 生产1单位小麦的机会成本/OC for 1 unit wheat | 生产1单位布料的机会成本/OC for 1 unit cloth |
|-------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 国家A/Country A | 2单位布料/2 units cloth | 0.5单位小麦/0.5 units wheat |
| 国家B/Country B | 3单位布料/3 units cloth | 0.33单位小麦/0.33 units wheat |

**结论/Conclusion：**
- A在小麦上有比较优势 / A has comparative advantage in wheat
- B在布料上有比较优势 / B has comparative advantage in cloth

---

## 2.4 比较优势与贸易 / Comparative Advantage and Trade

### 贸易的基础 / Basis for Trade

比较优势理论解释了为什么即使一个国家在所有商品上都具有绝对优势，国际贸易仍然有利可图。  
The theory of comparative advantage explains why international trade can be profitable even when one country has an absolute advantage in all goods.

### 贸易收益 / Gains from Trade

$$
\text{贸易收益} = \text{专业化生产} + \text{交换剩余}
$$

$$
\text{Gains from Trade} = \text{Specialization} + \text{Exchange Surplus}
$$
