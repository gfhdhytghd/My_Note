# 总剩余 / Total Surplus

**定义 / Definition**  
总剩余是消费者剩余与生产者剩余之和，反映市场资源配置的总福利。  
Total surplus is the sum of consumer surplus and producer surplus, reflecting the total welfare from market resource allocation.

**公式 / Formula**  
$$
TS = CS + PS
$$

**图示 / Graph**  
- 总剩余是需求曲线与供给曲线之间、从$0$到$Q^*$区域的面积。  
- Total surplus is the area between the demand and supply curves, from $0$ to $Q^*$.

**经济意义 / Economic Meaning**  
- 衡量市场效率，最大化时表示资源配置最优。  
- Measures market efficiency; maximized when resource allocation is optimal.
