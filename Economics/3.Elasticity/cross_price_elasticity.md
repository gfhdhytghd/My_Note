# 交叉价格弹性 / Cross-Price Elasticity

## 1. 定义 / Definition

**交叉价格弹性**衡量一种商品价格变化对另一种商品需求量的影响程度。  
**Cross-price elasticity** measures how the quantity demanded of one good responds to changes in the price of another good.

$$E_{x,y} = \frac{\%\Delta Q_x}{\%\Delta P_y}$$

其中：
- $E_{x,y}$ = 商品x对商品y的交叉价格弹性 / Cross-price elasticity of x with respect to y
- $\%\Delta Q_x$ = 商品x需求量的百分比变化 / Percentage change in quantity demanded of x
- $\%\Delta P_y$ = 商品y价格的百分比变化 / Percentage change in price of y

---

## 2. 中点弹性计算公式 / Midpoint Elasticity Formula

使用中点法可以避免起点偏差，计算更准确的弹性值。  
The midpoint method avoids start-point bias and provides more accurate elasticity calculations.

$$E_{x,y} = \frac{\frac{Q_{x2} - Q_{x1}}{(Q_{x1} + Q_{x2})/2}}{\frac{P_{y2} - P_{y1}}{(P_{y1} + P_{y2})/2}}$$

---

## 3. 弹性值解读 / Interpretation of Elasticity Values

### 3.1 替代品 / Substitutes
- **弹性值**：$E_{x,y} > 0$ / **Elasticity value**: positive
- **经济含义**：商品y价格上升 → 商品x需求增加 / **Economic meaning**: Price of y rises → demand for x increases
- **实例 / Examples**：
  - 咖啡与茶 / Coffee and tea
  - 可口可乐与百事可乐 / Coca-Cola and Pepsi
  - 汽油与柴油 / Gasoline and diesel

### 3.2 互补品 / Complements  
- **弹性值**：$E_{x,y} < 0$ / **Elasticity value**: negative
- **经济含义**：商品y价格上升 → 商品x需求减少 / **Economic meaning**: Price of y rises → demand for x decreases
- **实例 / Examples**：
  - 汽车与汽油 / Cars and gasoline
  - 打印机与墨盒 / Printers and ink cartridges
  - 面包与黄油 / Bread and butter

### 3.3 独立商品 / Independent Goods
- **弹性值**：$E_{x,y} = 0$ / **Elasticity value**: zero
- **经济含义**：商品y价格变化对商品x需求无影响 / **Economic meaning**: Price changes of y have no effect on demand for x
- **实例 / Examples**：
  - 鞋子与苹果 / Shoes and apples
  - 电脑与牛奶 / Computers and milk

---

## 4. 弹性强度分析 / Elasticity Strength Analysis

### 4.1 弹性强度等级 / Strength Levels
- $|E_{x,y}| > 1$：**强相关** / **Strong relationship**
- $0 < |E_{x,y}| < 1$：**弱相关** / **Weak relationship**  
- $|E_{x,y}| = 0$：**无相关** / **No relationship**

### 4.2 影响因素 / Influencing Factors
- 商品相似程度 / Degree of similarity between goods
- 消费者偏好 / Consumer preferences
- 收入水平 / Income levels
- 市场结构 / Market structure

---

## 5. 实际应用 / Practical Applications

### 5.1 定价策略 / Pricing Strategy
企业可利用交叉价格弹性制定竞争性定价策略。  
Companies can use cross-price elasticity to develop competitive pricing strategies.

### 5.2 市场分析 / Market Analysis
- 识别竞争对手产品 / Identify competitor products
- 评估市场竞争强度 / Assess market competition intensity
- 预测需求变化 / Predict demand changes

### 5.3 政策制定 / Policy Making
政府可利用交叉价格弹性评估税收政策对相关商品的影响。  
Governments can use cross-price elasticity to assess the impact of tax policies on related goods.
