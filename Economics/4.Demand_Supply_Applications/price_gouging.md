# 价格欺诈 / Price Gouging

**定义 / Definition**  
价格欺诈指在突发事件或紧急状态下，卖方因需求激增而显著提高价格的行为。  
Price gouging refers to sellers significantly raising prices during emergencies or sudden demand shocks.

**背景与情境 / Contexts**  
常见于自然灾害、公共卫生事件、突发供给中断等场景（如飓风、地震、疫情），核心特征是短期内需求大幅上升、供给一时难以快速扩大。  
Occurs during natural disasters, public health crises, or sudden supply disruptions; demand surges while supply cannot expand quickly in the short run.

## 1. 经济学视角 / Economic Perspectives

**支持观点 / In favor**  
- 高价格抑制过度抢购，促使稀缺物资更有效配置。  
- Higher prices deter hoarding and allocate scarce goods more efficiently.  
- 为供应商提供利润激励，吸引更多资源快速运入灾区。  
- Creates profit incentives for suppliers to move resources to affected areas.

**反对观点 / Against**  
- 在脆弱时期转移消费者剩余，被视为对弱势群体不公。  
- Shifts surplus from vulnerable consumers at critical times, viewed as unfair.  
- 可能引发社会不稳定与信任受损。  
- May trigger social unrest and erode trust.

**与弹性相关 / Role of Elasticity**  
- 需求弹性越小，价格上升引发的数量下降越有限，消费者负担更重。  
- With inelastic demand, quantity falls little as price rises; burden on consumers increases.  
- 供给在短期内往往缺乏弹性，导致价格对冲击更敏感。  
- Short-run supply is often inelastic, making prices more sensitive to shocks.

## 2. 法规与政策 / Laws and Policies

**紧急状态法规 / Emergency laws**  
许多法域在宣布紧急状态后禁止“过度”涨价（通常以一定百分比阈值界定），执法侧重基本生活物资（如水、食品、燃料、医疗用品）。  
Many jurisdictions ban “excessive” price increases once a state of emergency is declared, often using percentage thresholds and focusing on essentials (water, food, fuel, medical supplies).

**与价格管制的关系 / Relation to Price Controls**  
价格欺诈限制可视作临时性价格上限；若上限低于均衡价，可能导致短缺、排队和黑市。  
Anti-gouging rules operate like temporary price ceilings; if set below equilibrium, shortages, queues, and black markets can emerge.

## 3. 现实案例 / Real-world Cases

- 2005 年卡特里娜飓风：瓶装水与汽油价格飙升，引发公众愤怒与执法行动。  
- Hurricane Katrina (2005): Spikes in bottled water and gasoline prices prompted public anger and enforcement actions.  
- 新冠疫情初期：口罩与防护用品价格争议频发，线上线下均有监管介入。  
- Early COVID-19: Mask and PPE price controversies saw intervention both online and offline.
