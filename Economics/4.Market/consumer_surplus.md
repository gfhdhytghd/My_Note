# 消费者剩余 / Consumer Surplus

**定义 / Definition**  
消费者剩余是指消费者愿意为某商品支付的最高价格与实际支付价格之间的差额总和。  
Consumer surplus is the total difference between what consumers are willing to pay for a good and what they actually pay.

**公式 / Formula**  
$$
CS = \int_0^{Q^*} D(q)\,dq - P^* \cdot Q^*
$$
其中$D(q)$为需求曲线，$Q^*$为均衡数量，$P^*$为均衡价格。  
Where $D(q)$ is the demand curve, $Q^*$ is the equilibrium quantity, and $P^*$ is the equilibrium price.

**图示 / Graph**  
- 消费者剩余是需求曲线与市场价格线之间、从$0$到$Q^*$区域的面积。  
- Consumer surplus is the area between the demand curve and the market price line, from $0$ to $Q^*$.

**经济意义 / Economic Meaning**  
- 衡量市场中消费者因价格低于其愿付价格而获得的净收益。  
- Measures the net benefit consumers receive when the market price is lower than their willingness to pay.
