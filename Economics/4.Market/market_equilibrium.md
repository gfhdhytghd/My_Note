# 市场均衡 / Market Equilibrium

**定义 / Definition**  
市场均衡是指供给量等于需求量时的市场状态，此时市场价格和数量达到稳定。  
Market equilibrium is the state where supply equals demand, resulting in stable market price and quantity.

**均衡条件 / Equilibrium Conditions**  
- 供给量 = 需求量：$Q_s = Q_d$  
  Supply equals demand: $Q_s = Q_d$
- 市场出清：无过剩供给或过剩需求  
  Market clearing: no excess supply or excess demand

**均衡价格与数量 / Equilibrium Price and Quantity**  
- 均衡价格$P^*$：使供给量等于需求量的价格  
  Equilibrium price $P^*$: the price at which supply equals demand
- 均衡数量$Q^*$：在均衡价格下的交易数量  
  Equilibrium quantity $Q^*$: the quantity traded at the equilibrium price

**市场调整机制 / Market Adjustment Mechanism**  
- 当价格高于均衡价格时，供给过剩，价格下降  
  When price is above equilibrium, excess supply causes price to fall
- 当价格低于均衡价格时，需求过剩，价格上升  
  When price is below equilibrium, excess demand causes price to rise
