# 垄断特征 / Monopoly Characteristics

## 垄断定义 / Monopoly Definition

### 基本概念 / Basic Concepts

**垄断 / Monopoly：** 市场上只有一个卖者的市场结构 / A market structure with only one seller

**特点 / Characteristics：**
- 单一卖者 / Single seller
- 无替代品 / No close substitutes
- 价格制定者 / Price maker
- 进入壁垒 / Barriers to entry

## 垄断成因 / Causes of Monopoly

### 1. 自然垄断 / Natural Monopoly

**定义 / Definition：** 由于规模经济，单个企业能以最低成本满足整个市场需求 / Due to economies of scale, a single firm can satisfy the entire market demand at the lowest cost

**特点 / Characteristics：**
- 平均成本持续下降 / Average cost continuously decreases
- 固定成本高，边际成本低 / High fixed costs, low marginal costs
- 网络效应 / Network effects

**例证 / Illustration：** 受规模经济影响，一个企业统一铺设与运营网络比多家重复建设更省成本 / Impacted by economies of scale: one firm operating a single network is cheaper than many duplicate networks

**例子 / Examples：**
- 电力公司 / Electric utilities
- 自来水公司 / Water companies
- 电信网络 / Telecommunications networks
- 灌溉供水网络、输电线路 / Water irrigation networks, power lines

### 2. 政府垄断 / Government Monopoly

**定义 / Definition：** 政府通过法律或政策创造的垄断 / Monopoly created by government through laws or policies

**例子 / Examples：**
- 邮政服务 / Postal services
- 邮政一类邮件专营 / First-class mail exclusivity (post office)
- 某些公共服务 / Certain public services
- 专利保护 / Patent protection
- 版权与著作权保护 / Copyright law

### 3. 资源垄断 / Resource Monopoly

**定义 / Definition：** 控制关键资源或生产要素的垄断 / Monopoly that controls key resources or factors of production

**例子 / Examples：**
- 钻石公司（历史上）/ Diamond companies (historically)
- 稀有金属控制 / Rare metal control
- 早期 20 世纪 ALCOA 控制全球约 90% 铝土矿 / Early 1900s, ALCOA owned ~90% of world bauxite
- DeBeers 在 2000 年代初前控制全球约 70% 钻石矿 / DeBeers owned ~70% of diamond mines until early 2000s

### 4. 技术垄断 / Technological Monopoly

**定义 / Definition：** 通过技术创新或专利保护获得的垄断 / Monopoly obtained through technological innovation or patent protection

**例子 / Examples：**
- 制药公司 / Pharmaceutical companies
- 软件公司 / Software companies
- 高科技企业 / High-tech companies

## 垄断需求曲线 / Monopoly Demand Curve

### 市场需求曲线 / Market Demand Curve

**特点 / Characteristics：**
- 垄断企业面临整个市场的需求曲线 / Monopoly faces the entire market demand curve
- 向右下方倾斜 / Downward sloping
- 价格与需求量反向变动 / Price and quantity demanded move inversely

### 边际收益曲线 / Marginal Revenue Curve

**关系 / Relationship：**
$$MR = P + Q \cdot \frac{\Delta P}{\Delta Q}$$

**特点 / Characteristics：**
- 边际收益低于价格 / Marginal revenue is below price
- $MR < P$ / $MR < P$
- 边际收益曲线位于需求曲线下方 / MR curve is below demand curve

## 垄断利润最大化 / Monopoly Profit Maximization

### 利润最大化条件 / Profit Maximization Condition

**条件 / Condition：**
$$MR = MC$$

**结果 / Result：**
- 选择使边际收益等于边际成本的产量 / Choose output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost
- 根据需求曲线确定价格 / Determine price based on demand curve

### 垄断定价 / Monopoly Pricing

**定价策略 / Pricing Strategy：**
- 价格高于边际成本 / Price above marginal cost
- $P > MC$ / $P > MC$
- 获得垄断利润 / Earn monopoly profit

## 垄断缺陷 / Monopoly Defects

### 1. 配置无效率 / Allocative Inefficiency

**问题 / Problem：**
- 产量低于社会最优水平 / Output below socially optimal level
- 价格高于边际成本 / Price above marginal cost
- 消费者剩余减少 / Consumer surplus decreases

### 2. 生产效率损失 / Productive Inefficiency

**问题 / Problem：**
- 缺乏竞争压力 / Lack of competitive pressure
- 可能以高于最低成本的价格生产 / May produce at higher than minimum cost
- 创新动力不足 / Insufficient innovation incentives

### 3. 收入分配不均 / Income Distribution Inequality

**问题 / Problem：**
- 垄断利润转移给生产者 / Monopoly profits transferred to producers
- 消费者福利损失 / Consumer welfare loss
- 社会总剩余减少 / Social total surplus decreases

## 垄断管制 / Monopoly Regulation

### 1. 价格管制 / Price Regulation

**平均成本定价 / Average Cost Pricing：**
$$P = ATC$$

**边际成本定价 / Marginal Cost Pricing：**
$$P = MC$$

### 2. 反垄断法 / Antitrust Laws

**目标 / Objectives：**
- 防止垄断形成 / Prevent monopoly formation
- 促进市场竞争 / Promote market competition
- 保护消费者利益 / Protect consumer interests

### 3. 自然垄断管制 / Natural Monopoly Regulation

**方法 / Methods：**
- 价格上限 / Price caps
- 收益率管制 / Rate of return regulation
- 激励性管制 / Incentive regulation
