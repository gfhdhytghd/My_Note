# 价格歧视 / Price Discrimination

## 基本概念 / Basic Concepts

### 价格歧视定义 / Price Discrimination Definition

**价格歧视 / Price Discrimination：** 对相同产品或服务向不同消费者收取不同价格的行为 / The practice of charging different prices to different consumers for the same product or service

**条件 / Conditions：**
- 企业具有市场力量 / Firm has market power
- 能够区分不同消费者群体 / Can distinguish between different consumer groups
- 防止套利 / Prevent arbitrage

## 价格歧视类型 / Types of Price Discrimination

### 一级价格歧视 / First-Degree Price Discrimination

#### 定义 / Definition
**完全价格歧视 / Perfect Price Discrimination：** 对每个消费者收取其愿意支付的最高价格 / Charge each consumer the maximum price they are willing to pay

#### 特点 / Characteristics
- 每个消费者支付不同的价格 / Each consumer pays a different price
- 企业获得所有消费者剩余 / Firm captures all consumer surplus
- 产量等于完全竞争水平 / Output equals perfect competition level

#### 例子 / Examples
- 拍卖 / Auctions
- 个性化定价 / Personalized pricing
- 谈判定价 / Negotiated pricing

### 二级价格歧视 / Second-Degree Price Discrimination

#### 定义 / Definition
**数量歧视 / Quantity Discrimination：** 根据购买数量收取不同价格 / Charge different prices based on quantity purchased

#### 特点 / Characteristics
- 消费者自我选择 / Self-selection by consumers
- 通过价格菜单区分消费者 / Distinguish consumers through price menus
- 非线性定价 / Non-linear pricing

#### 例子 / Examples
- 批量折扣 / Bulk discounts
- 两部定价 / Two-part tariffs
- 阶梯定价 / Tiered pricing

### 三级价格歧视 / Third-Degree Price Discrimination

#### 定义 / Definition
**群体歧视 / Group Discrimination：** 对不同消费者群体收取不同价格 / Charge different prices to different consumer groups

#### 特点 / Characteristics
- 基于可观察特征分组 / Group based on observable characteristics
- 不同群体有不同的需求弹性 / Different groups have different demand elasticities
- 最常见的价格歧视形式 / Most common form of price discrimination

#### 例子 / Examples
- 学生折扣 / Student discounts
- 老年人优惠 / Senior citizen discounts
- 地区定价 / Regional pricing

## 价格歧视条件 / Conditions for Price Discrimination

### 1. 市场力量 / Market Power

**要求 / Requirement：**
- 企业必须具有定价能力 / Firm must have pricing power
- 不是价格接受者 / Not a price taker
- 能够影响市场价格 / Can influence market price

### 2. 需求弹性差异 / Different Demand Elasticities

**要求 / Requirement：**
- 不同消费者群体有不同的需求弹性 / Different consumer groups have different demand elasticities
- 高弹性群体收取低价格 / Charge low prices to high elasticity groups
- 低弹性群体收取高价格 / Charge high prices to low elasticity groups

### 3. 防止套利 / Prevent Arbitrage

**要求 / Requirement：**
- 防止低价购买者向高价购买者转售 / Prevent low-price buyers from reselling to high-price buyers
- 产品不能轻易转移 / Products cannot be easily transferred
- 服务具有不可转移性 / Services are non-transferable

## 价格歧视效果 / Effects of Price Discrimination

### 对企业的效果 / Effects on Firms

#### 积极效果 / Positive Effects
- 增加总收益 / Increase total revenue
- 提高利润 / Increase profits
- 更好地利用生产能力 / Better utilize production capacity

#### 消极效果 / Negative Effects
- 增加管理成本 / Increase management costs
- 可能面临法律风险 / May face legal risks
- 消费者可能产生负面反应 / Consumers may react negatively

### 对消费者的效果 / Effects on Consumers

#### 积极效果 / Positive Effects
- 某些群体获得更低价格 / Some groups get lower prices
- 增加产品可获得性 / Increase product availability
- 可能促进创新 / May promote innovation

#### 消极效果 / Negative Effects
- 某些群体支付更高价格 / Some groups pay higher prices
- 消费者剩余减少 / Consumer surplus decreases
- 可能加剧不平等 / May exacerbate inequality

### 对社会的效果 / Effects on Society

#### 配置效率 / Allocative Efficiency
- 可能提高或降低效率 / May increase or decrease efficiency
- 取决于具体实施方式 / Depends on specific implementation
- 需要权衡不同目标 / Need to balance different objectives
