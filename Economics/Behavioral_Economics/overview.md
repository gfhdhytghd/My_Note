# 行为经济学概览 / Overview of Behavioral Economics

## 什么是行为经济学？ / What Is Behavioral Economics?

- 基本定义 / Basic definition: 行为经济学研究心理因素、认知偏差及社会影响如何塑造人们的经济决策，与传统“完全理性、效用最大化”的假设形成对比。  
  Behavioral economics examines how psychological factors, cognitive biases, and social influences shape economic decision making, contrasting with the standard model of fully rational, utility-maximizing agents.
- 研究目标 / Goal: 通过引入更真实的人类行为假设，解释传统模型难以解释的现象，并为公共政策和市场设计提供更有效的干预工具。  
  Its goal is to explain phenomena that standard models struggle with and to inform better policy and market design by incorporating more realistic assumptions about human behavior.

## 核心要素 / Core Elements

- 限制理性 / Bounded rationality: 决策者信息有限、计算能力有限、时间有限，因此往往依赖启发式而非精确优化。  
  People face limits in information, computation, and time, so they rely on heuristics rather than exact optimization.
- 系统性偏差 / Systematic biases: 损失厌恶、现状偏好、框架效应等会系统性地影响选择，而不仅是随机噪声。  
  Loss aversion, status quo bias, framing effects, and other regular biases systematically affect choices rather than just adding random noise.
- 社会偏好 / Social preferences: 人们关心公平、互惠和他人看法，这会影响交易、谈判与合作行为。  
  Social preferences such as fairness, reciprocity, and concern for others’ opinions shape trading, bargaining, and cooperation.
