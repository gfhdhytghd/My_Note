# 自控与后悔规避 / Self-Control and Regret Avoidance

## 跨期自控与时间不一致 / Intertemporal Self-Control & Time Inconsistency
- 长期-短期冲突：人们为长期目标制定计划，但短期自我常改选即时满足；Long-run plans often conflict with short-run choices when self-control fails.
- 延迟奖励难点：垃圾食品过量或斯坦福棉花糖实验（等待15分钟可得第二颗）展示延迟奖励的困难；Overeating junk food and the Stanford marshmallow test illustrate difficulty delaying gratification.
- 行为含义：自控问题会导致偏离原计划的消费/储蓄决策；Self-control problems cause deviations from planned consumption/saving.

## 后悔规避 / Regret Avoidance
- 人们会为避免潜在后悔而拒绝看似更优的交易，如拒绝高价转卖彩票；People reject seemingly better deals to avoid anticipated regret, e.g., refusing to sell a lottery ticket at a higher price.
- 机制：预期的“要是当初……”带来心理成本，驱动保守选择；Anticipated “what if” regret acts as a psychological cost that pushes toward conservative choices.
