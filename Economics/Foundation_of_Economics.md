## 经济学基本原理 / Basic Principles of Economics

### F2. 激励很重要 / Incentives matter

激励是影响人们行为的重要因素。当激励机制改变时，人们的行为也会相应地发生变化。
Incentives are important factors that influence people's behavior. When incentives change, people's behavior changes accordingly.

### F3. 生活就是权衡取舍 / Life is about trade-offs

生活中充满了选择，每个选择都意味着权衡取舍。资源有限，我们必须在不同的选项中做出抉择。
Life is full of choices, and each choice involves trade-offs. Resources are limited, and we must make decisions among different options.

**例子 / Example：**
一个重型轰炸机的成本可能意味着：  
The cost of a heavy duty bomber could be:

- 30 个城市的一所学校  
  A school in 30 cities
- 2 家医院  
  2 hospitals
- 50 英里公路  
  50 miles of highway

### F4. 机会成本 / Opportunity Cost

机会成本是指为了进行某项活动而必须放弃的价值最高的其他选择。
Opportunity cost is the highest-valued alternative that must be given up to engage in an activity.

#### 例子 / Example

如果你用 2 小时去看电影，那么你放弃的可能是用这段时间学习或打工的机会，这些被放弃的就是你的机会成本。
If you spend 2 hours watching a movie, the opportunity cost may be the time you could have spent studying or working. What you give up is your opportunity cost.

### F5. 边际思维 / Marginal Thinking

边际思维是指评估再获得一单位某物时，其带来的收益是否大于所付出的成本。
Marginal thinking is evaluating whether the benefit of acquiring one more unit of something is greater than the cost.

**例子 / Example：**  
购买一本教材。  
Purchase a textbook.

公司操纵用户心智  
Companies manipulate consumer's marginal thinking.

### F6. 贸易 / Trade

贸易与专业化是经济的基石。
Trade and specialization are the cornerstones of the economy.

所有参与方通过贸易与专业化都能获得更大的利益。
All parties involved are better off through trade and specialization.

## 经济学研究方法 / Economic Research Methods

经济学家使用科学方法来检验模型。
Economists use the scientific method to test models.

### 四个步骤 / 4 Steps:

1. **观察现象**  
   Observe a phenomenon

2. **基于观察，研究并提出假设，即对该现象的解释**  
   Based on observations, research and develop a hypothesis, which is an explanation for that phenomenon.

3. **构建模型以检验假设**  
   Construct a model to test the hypothesis.

4. **设计实验来测试模型的有效性。收集数据后，可以验证、修正或否定假设。**  
   Design experiments to test how well the model works. After collecting data, one can verify, revise, or refute the hypothesis.

### 经济学家如何做到这一点？ / How do economists do this?

**问：经济学家是如何做到这一点的？**  
Q: How do economists do this?

**答：收集现实世界的数据对于检验经济理论非常重要。**  
A: Data gathering and real world events are important for testing economic theory.

实验经济学家通过向受试者提供金钱激励，直接检验经济模型。
Experimental economists test models directly by providing monetary incentives to human subjects.

理想情况下，经济学家通过"其他条件不变"（ceteris paribus，保持其他变量不变）的方法，每次只改变一个变量来检验模型。
Economists (ideally) test models by changing one variable at a time, ceteris paribus (holding all else constant).

## 经济学术语 / Economic Terms

### 变量 / Variables

**变量（Variable）**：指在模型中可能出现或不出现的影响因素。
Variable: a factor that may or may not be included in a model.

**内生变量（Endogenous variable）**：在模型中被控制或由模型内部决定的变量。
A variable is endogenous if it is controlled for or determined within the model.

**外生变量（Exogenous variable）**：在模型之外，由外部决定的变量。
A variable is exogenous if it is determined outside the model.

## 重要提醒 / Important Notes

警惕错误的假设。（2008年金融危机）
Beware of faulty assumptions. (2008 financial crisis)
