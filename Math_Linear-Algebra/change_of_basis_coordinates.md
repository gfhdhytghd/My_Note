# 换基与坐标变换 / Change of Basis and Coordinate Conversion

## 核心问题 / Core Question
同一个向量本身不会因为换基而改变，改变的是它在不同基下的坐标表示。若$B=\{b_1,\dots,b_n\}$与$C=\{c_1,\dots,c_n\}$是同一向量空间的两组基，那么我们关心的是如何在$[v]_B$与$[v]_C$之间转换。  
The vector itself does not change when we change basis; only its coordinate representation changes. If $B=\{b_1,\dots,b_n\}$ and $C=\{c_1,\dots,c_n\}$ are two bases of the same vector space, the goal is to convert between $[v]_B$ and $[v]_C$.

## 矩阵视角 / Matrix View
把基向量按列排成矩阵
$$
P_B=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_n],\qquad P_C=[c_1\ \cdots\ c_n].
$$
若$x$是向量$v$在标准坐标下的列向量，则有
$$
x=P_B[v]_B=P_C[v]_C.
$$
Arrange the basis vectors as columns:
$$
P_B=[b_1\ \cdots\ b_n],\qquad P_C=[c_1\ \cdots\ c_n].
$$
If $x$ is the standard-coordinate column vector of $v$, then
$$
x=P_B[v]_B=P_C[v]_C.
$$

因此
$$
[v]_B=P_B^{-1}x,\qquad [v]_C=P_C^{-1}x.
$$
若要直接从$B$基坐标换到$C$基坐标，可得
$$
[v]_C=P_C^{-1}P_B[v]_B.
$$
Therefore
$$
[v]_B=P_B^{-1}x,\qquad [v]_C=P_C^{-1}x.
$$
To convert directly from $B$-coordinates to $C$-coordinates, we get
$$
[v]_C=P_C^{-1}P_B[v]_B.
$$

## 两种常用做法 / Two Standard Methods
### 1. 先回到标准坐标 / Go Through Standard Coordinates
若已知$[v]_B$，先算$x=P_B[v]_B$，再算$[v]_C=P_C^{-1}x$。这个方法最直观，也最容易看清“先还原向量，再重新编码”的过程。  
If $[v]_B$ is known, first compute $x=P_B[v]_B$, then compute $[v]_C=P_C^{-1}x$. This is the most intuitive method because it makes the process “decode the vector, then encode it again” explicit.

### 2. 用增广矩阵或逆矩阵直接求 / Solve Directly by Augmented Matrix or Inverse
若想直接求$[v]_C$，可以解线性方程
$$
P_C[v]_C=x.
$$
若$x$还不知道，也可以先把$P_B[v]_B$代入右边，得到
$$
P_C[v]_C=P_B[v]_B.
$$
然后把它写成增广矩阵做行化简，或直接左乘$P_C^{-1}$。  
If you want $[v]_C$ directly, solve the linear system
$$
P_C[v]_C=x.
$$
If $x$ is not known explicitly, substitute $P_B[v]_B$ on the right:
$$
P_C[v]_C=P_B[v]_B.
$$
Then row-reduce the augmented matrix, or multiply by $P_C^{-1}$ directly.

## 小例子 / Quick Example
在$\mathbb{R}^2$中，设
$$
B=\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
\right\},
\qquad
v=\begin{bmatrix}2\\0\end{bmatrix}.
$$
若要求$v$在基$B$下的坐标，写成
$$
P_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
[v]_B=P_B^{-1}v.
$$
计算可得
$$
[v]_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
1
\end{bmatrix},
$$
因为
$$
\begin{bmatrix}2\\0\end{bmatrix}
=
1\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}
+1\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$
In $\mathbb{R}^2$, let
$$
B=\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},
\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
\right\},
\qquad
v=\begin{bmatrix}2\\0\end{bmatrix}.
$$
To find the coordinates of $v$ in basis $B$, write
$$
P_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
[v]_B=P_B^{-1}v.
$$
The computation gives
$$
[v]_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
1
\end{bmatrix},
$$
because
$$
\begin{bmatrix}2\\0\end{bmatrix}
=
1\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}
+1\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## 做题提醒 / Problem-Solving Reminders
- 先分清“向量本身”与“向量在某组基下的坐标”。  
  Always distinguish the vector itself from its coordinates in a chosen basis.
- 把基向量按列排成矩阵，不要按行排。  
  Put basis vectors in columns, not rows.
- 若题目写“换 basis”，本质通常是解一个线性方程组或做一次矩阵求逆。  
  A “change of basis” problem is usually just solving a linear system or applying an inverse matrix.
- 若两组基都不是标准基，最稳妥公式是$[v]_C=P_C^{-1}P_B[v]_B$。  
  If neither basis is standard, the safest formula is $[v]_C=P_C^{-1}P_B[v]_B$.
