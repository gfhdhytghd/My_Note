# 标量与矢量：基本概念 / Scalars and Vectors: Basics

## 标量 / Scalar
标量（scalar）只有大小（magnitude），没有方向；可用一个实数描述。  
A scalar has magnitude only (no direction) and can be described by a single real number.

例子：质量$m$、时间$t$、温度$T$、能量$E$。  
Examples: mass $m$, time $t$, temperature $T$, energy $E$.

## 矢量 / Vector
矢量/向量（vector）既有大小也有方向；常写作$\vec{v}$或粗体$\mathbf{v}$。  
A vector has both magnitude and direction; it’s often written as $\vec{v}$ or in bold $\mathbf{v}$.

例子：位移$\vec{r}$、速度$\vec{v}$、加速度$\vec{a}$、力$\vec{F}$。  
Examples: displacement $\vec{r}$, velocity $\vec{v}$, acceleration $\vec{a}$, force $\vec{F}$.

常见运算：向量加法、数乘；点积$\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}$给出标量，叉积$\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$（三维）给出矢量。  
Common operations: vector addition and scalar multiplication; dot product $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}$ yields a scalar, cross product $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$ (in 3D) yields a vector.

## 向量的乘法 / Vector Multiplication
- 标量乘法 / Scalar multiplication：$k\vec{a}$ 只改变长度（乘以 $|k|$）与方向（若 $k<0$ 则反向）。  
  $k\vec{a}$ scales magnitude by $|k|$ and flips direction if $k<0$.
- 点乘（内积）/ Dot product：$\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta=a_xb_x+a_yb_y+a_zb_z$，结果是标量。  
  $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta=a_xb_x+a_yb_y+a_zb_z$, returns a scalar.
  - 几何意义 / Geometric meaning：$\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=|\vec{a}|\cdot(\text{b 在 a 方向的分量})$。  
    $|\vec{a}|$ times the component of $\vec{b}$ along $\vec{a}$.
  - 物理例子 / Physics example：功 $W=\vec{F}\cdot\Delta\vec{r}$。  
    Work $W=\vec{F}\cdot\Delta\vec{r}$.
- 叉乘（外积）/ Cross product：$\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta\,\hat{n}$，方向由右手定则确定。  
  $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta\,\hat{n}$, direction by the right-hand rule.
  - 分量公式 / Component formula：$\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=(a_yb_z-a_zb_y,\;a_zb_x-a_xb_z,\;a_xb_y-a_yb_x)$。  
    $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=(a_yb_z-a_zb_y,\;a_zb_x-a_xb_z,\;a_xb_y-a_yb_x)$.
  - 物理例子 / Physics examples：力矩 $\vec{\tau}=\vec{r}\times\vec{F}$，角动量 $\vec{L}=\vec{r}\times\vec{p}$。  
    Torque $\vec{\tau}=\vec{r}\times\vec{F}$, angular momentum $\vec{L}=\vec{r}\times\vec{p}$.
- 混合积（三重积）/ Scalar triple product：$\vec{a}\cdot(\vec{b}\times\vec{c})$ 等于三向量张成平行六面体的有向体积；也可写成行列式。  
  $\vec{a}\cdot(\vec{b}\times\vec{c})$ is the signed volume; it can also be written as a determinant.

## 矢量的作图（二维） / Graphing a Vector (2D)
![Vector graphing (2D example)](../../Daily%20Notes/attachments/vector_graphing_2d.svg)

在二维中，把$\vec{v}=\langle v_x,v_y\rangle$画成“有向线段”：从原点$O(0,0)$出发，沿$x$方向走$v_x$，沿$y$方向走$v_y$，终点是向量的“头”（head）。  
In 2D, graph $\vec{v}=\langle v_x,v_y\rangle$ as a directed segment: start at the origin $O(0,0)$, move $v_x$ along $x$ and $v_y$ along $y$; the endpoint is the vector’s head.

同一矢量可平移到任意位置（只要大小与方向不变）；向量加法常用“首尾相接”（head-to-tail）来画$\vec{a}+\vec{b}$。  
The same vector can be translated anywhere (as long as magnitude and direction stay the same); for addition, use the head-to-tail method to sketch $\vec{a}+\vec{b}$.

## 数量三重积的行列式表示 / Determinant Form of the Scalar Triple Product

数量三重积$\mathbf{u}\cdot(\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{w})$可以写成以三个向量为行的行列式： / The scalar triple product $\mathbf{u}\cdot(\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{w})$ can be written as a determinant whose rows are the three vectors:

$$
\mathbf{u}\cdot(\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{w})=
\begin{vmatrix}
u_1 & u_2 & u_3\\
v_1 & v_2 & v_3\\
w_1 & w_2 & w_3
\end{vmatrix}
$$
