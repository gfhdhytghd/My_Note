## 问题与直觉 / Problem and Intuition

材料力学教材里同一章会同时出现 SI 单位和美制单位。很多错误不是公式错，而是把力和应力、功和功率、英尺和英寸混了。先认清每个单位“量的是什么”，再做换算。

Mechanics-of-materials texts mix SI and U.S. customary units. Many errors come not from formulas but from confusing force with stress, work with power, or feet with inches. First identify what each unit measures, then convert.

## 常用单位 / Common Units

| 单位 / Unit | 全称 / Full name | 物理量 / Quantity | 换算 / Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lbf | pound-force | 力 / Force | $1\ \text{lbf}\approx4.448\ \text{N}$ |
| kip | kilopound | 力 / Force | $1\ \text{kip}=1000\ \text{lbf}$ |
| psi | pounds per square inch | 应力、压强 / Stress, pressure | $1\ \text{psi}\approx6895\ \text{Pa}$ |
| ksi | kilopounds per square inch | 应力、压强 / Stress, pressure | $1\ \text{ksi}=1000\ \text{psi}\approx6.895\ \text{MPa}$ |
| kip·ft, lb·in | — | 力矩、扭矩 / Moment, torque | $1\ \text{kip}\cdot\text{ft}=12\ \text{kip}\cdot\text{in}$ |
| hp | horsepower | 功率 / Power | $1\ \text{hp}=550\ \text{ft}\cdot\text{lbf/s}\approx745.7\ \text{W}$ |

- kip 是力，ksi 是应力：$\frac{1\ \text{kip}}{1\ \text{in}^2}=1\ \text{ksi}$。  
  kip is a force and ksi is a stress: one kip over one square inch is one ksi.
- 功率必须带时间：$1\ \text{hp}=550\times12=6600\ \text{in}\cdot\text{lbf/s}=396000\ \text{in}\cdot\text{lbf/min}$。单独的 in·lbf 是功或能量，也是力矩的单位。  
  Power must include time. A bare in·lbf is work, energy, or moment.
- 美制机械马力约 $745.7\ \text{W}$，公制马力（PS）约 $735.5\ \text{W}$，两者不同。  
  The U.S. mechanical horsepower is about $745.7\ \text{W}$; the metric horsepower is about $735.5\ \text{W}$.

## 功率与扭矩 / Power and Torque

直线运动 $P=Fv$；转轴 $P=T\omega$，其中 $\omega$ 以 rad/s 计。若 $T$ 用 in·lbf，$P$ 用 hp：

For linear motion $P=Fv$; for a rotating shaft $P=T\omega$ with $\omega$ in rad/s. With $T$ in in·lbf and $P$ in hp:

$$P(\text{hp})=\frac{T(\text{in}\cdot\text{lbf})\,\omega(\text{rad/s})}{6600},\qquad\omega=\frac{2\pi n}{60}\ \ (n\text{ in rpm}).$$

**例（自拟数据）**：电机以 $1800\ \text{rpm}$ 传递 $10\ \text{hp}$。$\omega=\frac{2\pi(1800)}{60}\approx188.5\ \text{rad/s}$，$P=66000\ \text{in}\cdot\text{lbf/s}$，所以 $T=\frac{66000}{188.5}\approx350\ \text{in}\cdot\text{lbf}$。这个扭矩可以接着代入 [[Physics/3.Mechanics_of_Materials/torsion_circular_shafts]] 求剪应力。

**Example (assumed data):** A motor delivers $10\ \text{hp}$ at $1800\ \text{rpm}$. Then $\omega\approx188.5\ \text{rad/s}$ and $T\approx350\ \text{in}\cdot\text{lbf}$, which can be used in the torsion note to find shear stress.

## 易错点 / Common Mistakes

- 弯矩用 kip·ft，截面尺寸用 in：代入应力公式前必须统一成 kip·in。  
  Moments in kip·ft with section sizes in inches must be converted to kip·in before computing stress.
- 用 rpm 直接代入 $P=T\omega$：必须先换成 rad/s。  
  Using rpm directly in $P=T\omega$; convert to rad/s first.
- 把 hp 当成 in·lbf：hp 是每秒的功。  
  Treating hp as in·lbf; hp is work per second.
