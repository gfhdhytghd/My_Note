# Economy
### 完全竞争市场中的企业 / The Firm in a Competitive Market

- 价格接受者与需求 / Price taker and demand：企业面对水平需求曲线，$D=P=MR$，无法通过自身产量改变价格。/ The firm faces a horizontal demand, $D=P=MR$, and cannot influence price. 参见 [[perfect_competition_characteristics]].
- 利润最大化规则 / Profit maximization rule：选择满足$MR=MC$的产量$Q^*$；在短期内仅当$P\ge AVC$时生产，否则停产。/ Choose $Q^*$ where $MR=MC$; in the short run produce only if $P\ge AVC$, otherwise shut down. 相关：[[firm_profit_maximization]].
- 短期供给曲线 / Short-run supply curve：企业的短期供给是其$MC$曲线在$AVC$最小点之上的部分。/ The firm’s short-run supply is the portion of its $MC$ above the minimum $AVC$. 见 [[perfect_competition_characteristics]].
- 长期进入与退出 / Long-run entry and exit：自由进入退出推动价格趋于$min\,ATC$，长期经济利润为零。/ Free entry and exit drive $P\to min\,ATC$ with zero economic profit in the long run. 可对照 [[long_run_costs]].
- 利润与停产判别 / Profit and shutdown tests：利润$=\,(P-ATC(Q))\times Q$；若$P\ge AVC$可在短期继续生产以覆盖可变成本；若$P<AVC$应停产以避免扩大亏损。/ Profit equals $(P-ATC(Q))\times Q$; continue in short run if $P\ge AVC$, shut down if $P<AVC$.
- 行业与个体供给 / Market vs. firm supply：行业短期供给为各企业供给的水平加总；长期供给更有弹性，受进入退出影响更大。/ Market short-run supply horizontally sums firm supplies; long-run supply is more elastic due to entry/exit.
- 常见比较静态 / Common comparative statics：成本下降使$MC$与$ATC$下移、$Q^*$上升、长期$P$下降；从量税使$MC$上移并使供给左移。/ Cost decreases shift $MC$ and $ATC$ down (higher $Q^*$, lower long-run $P$); per-unit tax shifts $MC$ up and supply left. 参考 [[perfect_competition_taxation]].
