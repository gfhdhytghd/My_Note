# Chemistry
### 顺反异构如何判别 / Distinguishing cis and trans

- 先看是否具备几何异构条件 / Check preconditions: 需有受限旋转（双键 C=C 或环系）；若无受限旋转则不存在顺反关系。/ Needs restricted rotation (C=C or rings); without it, no cis/trans.
- 烯烃（C=C）/ Alkenes: 若每个双键碳上连接的两个取代基都不同，则用 E/Z 系统；比较两端“高优先级”（CIP 规则，原子序数越大优先级越高）的相对方位：同侧为 Z，异侧为 E。仅当两端各有一对“相同取代基”时，可以用传统 cis/trans：相同取代基同侧为 cis、异侧为 trans。/ If both alkene carbons bear two different substituents, use E/Z: rank priorities (CIP), same side of the higher priorities = Z, opposite = E. Only when each side has an identical group pair can you also say cis (same side) or trans (opposite) for those identical groups.
- 环烷烃 / Cycloalkanes: 以环平面为参照，“同侧/同面”为 cis，“异侧/异面”为 trans。立体画法速判：两个取代基若都为楔线（向上）或都为虚线（向下）→ cis；一个楔一个虚→ trans。椅式翻转不改变取代基的“向上/向下”指派。/ Relative to the ring plane: same face = cis, opposite faces = trans. Quick wedge/dash test: both wedges (up) or both dashes (down) → cis; one wedge and one dash → trans. Chair flips do not change up/down designations.
- 肽键（酰胺键）/ Peptide (amide) bond: 肽键因共振而具部分双键特征、近乎共平面，存在顺反两种几何构型。判据以 ω 二面角或 Cα 的相对位置为准：ω ≈ 180° 为 trans（两侧 Cα 处于对侧），ω ≈ 0° 为 cis（两侧 Cα 同侧）。实际蛋白中绝大多数肽键为 trans，唯独 X–Pro 位点常见一定比例的 cis。/ The peptide bond is resonance-stabilized, planar, and can be cis/trans. Determine by the ω dihedral or Cα positions: ω ≈ 180° = trans (Cα on opposite sides), ω ≈ 0° = cis (Cα on the same side). In proteins, nearly all peptide bonds are trans; cis occurs primarily at X–Pro sites.
- 不适用与易错点 / Not applicable and pitfalls: 若某一双键碳上连有两个完全相同的基团，则既不能用 cis/trans，也不能用 E/Z（因优先级并列）。苯环多取代位用 o/m/p（邻/间/对）而非常说的 cis/trans。/ If either alkene carbon has two identical groups, neither cis/trans nor E/Z applies (priority ties). On benzene, use o/m/p rather than cis/trans for common naming.
- 速记流程 / Quick decision flow:（1）有无 C=C 或环？（2）若是烯烃：能否按 CIP 排出每侧最高优先级？不能→无几何异构；能→判断高优先级同侧为 Z（若同时存在一对相同取代基也可称 cis），异侧为 E（亦可称 trans）。（3）若是环：看是否同面（cis）或异面（trans）。/ (1) Is there a C=C or a ring? (2) For alkenes: can you assign highest priorities by CIP on both carbons? If not, no geometric isomerism; if yes, same side = Z (and cis when identical pair exists), opposite = E (and trans). (3) For rings: check same face (cis) vs opposite faces (trans).
- 例子 / Examples: 2-丁烯：高优先级为 CH3 与 H，若同侧→ Z（亦称 cis-2-丁烯），异侧→ E（亦称 trans-2-丁烯）。1,2-二氯乙烯：Cl 与 H 比较，same side→ Z/cis，opposite→ E/trans。1,1-二氯乙烯：一侧有两个 Cl → 无 E/Z 或 cis/trans。/ 2-butene: priorities CH3 vs H; same side → Z (cis-2-butene), opposite → E (trans-2-butene). 1,2-dichloroethene: Cl vs H, same side → Z/cis, opposite → E/trans. 1,1-dichloroethene: two Cl on one carbon → neither E/Z nor cis/trans.

### 生物大分子与聚合物 / Biological Macromolecules and Polymers

- 大分子定义 / Definition: 相比以往小分子（<~900 g/mol），生物大分子包含大量原子、分子量可达数十万；其共同特征是由重复结构单元构成。  
  Versus small molecules (<~900 g/mol), biological macromolecules contain many atoms and can reach hundreds of thousands g/mol; they are defined by repeating structural elements.
- 聚合物本质 / Polymer nature: 大分子通常是聚合物，由“单体”通过共价键反复连接成长链；连接过程是重复的化学反应序列。  
  Macromolecules are typically polymers formed by repetitive covalent linkages of monomers into long chains; the coupling reactions repeat along the chain.
- 蛋白质 / Proteins: 单体为氨基酸；线性不分支；任意序列皆可，序列与侧链化学性质决定结构与功能。  
  Monomers are amino acids; polymers are linear and unbranched; sequence and side‑chain chemistry determine structure and function.
- 核酸 / Nucleic acids: 单体为核苷酸；单体化学性质相近，信息由顺序编码；通常为线性不分支。  
  Monomers are nucleotides; monomer chemistries are similar, information is encoded by order; generally linear and unbranched.
- 示意要点 / Schematic takeaways: 单体不必完全相同；聚合物可视作“彩色珠串”由不同单体组成；重复单元与连接方式决定宏观性质。  
  Monomers need not be identical; think “colored bead strings”; repeating unit and linkage chemistry set macroscopic properties.

# Economic
## Firm behaviour
In Short run, Firms only make decisions based on variable cost since fixed cost are fixed.

短期内企业只看可变成本作决策；固定成本已固定。/ In the short run, decisions depend on variable costs; fixed costs are fixed.

### 图示 / Diagram

- 坐标轴 / Axes: 纵轴价格 $P$，横轴数量 $Q$。  
  Vertical axis is price $P$; horizontal axis is quantity $Q$.
- 成本曲线 / Cost curves: 边际成本 $MC$（上升）；平均总成本 $ATC$（U 形）；平均可变成本 $AVC$（U 形且在 $ATC$ 下方）。  
  Marginal cost $MC$ upward sloping; average total cost $ATC$ is U‑shaped; average variable cost $AVC$ is U‑shaped below $ATC$.
- 价格与边际收益 / Price and MR: 三条水平线 $D=MR$ 分别对应价格 $P_1,P_2,P_3$。  
  Three horizontal $D=MR$ lines at prices $P_1,P_2,P_3$.
- 产量选择 / Output choice: 在 $MR=MC$ 处取最优产量 $Q$。  
  Choose output $Q$ where $MR=MC$.

### 三种价格情形 / Three Cases

1. $P_1: \; P>ATC \Rightarrow \pi>0$（企业盈利，继续生产）。  
   Firms operate with positive profit.
2. $P_2: \; AVC\le P<ATC \Rightarrow \pi<0$（亏损但继续生产以最小化亏损）。  
   Firms operate to minimize loss.
3. $P_3: \; P<AVC \Rightarrow TR<TVC$（停产）。  
   Firm shuts down.

### 利润公式 / Profit

$$
\pi=(P-ATC)\times Q
$$

### 短期决策要点 / Short‑run Rule

- 短期内，只基于可变成本作边际决策；固定成本视作既定，不影响 $MR=MC$ 的产量选择。  
  In the short run, marginal decisions depend on variable costs; fixed costs do not affect the $MR=MC$ output choice.

### 沉没成本 / Sunk Cost

- 定义 / Definition: 已因过去决策而支付、无法收回的成本；短期内固定成本通常为沉没成本。  
  Costs already incurred and unrecoverable; in the short run, fixed costs are typically sunk.
- 例子 / Example: 买了电影票去看 Star Wars Christmas Special（很烂）——票钱已付属沉没成本，是否继续看不应受其影响。  
  After buying a ticket to a terrible movie, the ticket price is sunk and should not affect the decision to continue watching.

### 沉没成本与谬误 / Sunk Cost & Fallacy

- 定义与谬误 / Definition & fallacy：沉没成本是已发生且不可回收的支出；“沉没成本谬误”是因为已沉没的支出而改变当前选择，而不是按现在的最佳备选作选择。  
  Sunk costs are expenditures already incurred and unrecoverable; the fallacy is letting them change a current choice instead of choosing the best alternative now.
- 经验法则 / Rule of thumb：若当前时间/资源的其他用途更好，就应当离开或更换方案——忽略沉没成本。  
  If an alternative use of time/resources is better, switch—ignore sunk costs.

### 长期 vs 短期 / Long Run vs Short Run

- 成本可变性 / Cost variability：短期有固定成本，长期所有成本可变。  
  In SR some costs are fixed; in LR all costs are variable.
- 进入退出 / Entry and exit：长期利润为进入信号、亏损为退出信号；进入↑→竞争↑→价格↓→利润回落；退出↑→竞争↓→价格↑→亏损收敛。  
  Profits trigger entry; losses trigger exit. Entry raises competition, lowers price, erodes profit; exit does the reverse.

### 长期均衡（完全竞争）/ LR Equilibrium (Price-Taking)

- 价格与需求 / Price and demand：企业面对水平的需求线 $D=MR=p^*$。  
  Firm faces a horizontal demand $D=MR=p^*$.
- 最优产量 / Optimal output：选择满足 $MR=MC$ 的产量。  
  Choose output where $MR=MC$.
- 零经济利润 / Zero economic profit：长期均衡有 $p^*=MC=ATC$ 且在 $ATC$ 最小点，经济利润 $\pi=0$，无进入或退出激励。  
  In LR equilibrium, $p^*=MC=ATC$ at the minimum of $ATC$, so $\pi=0$ and no incentive to enter or exit.

### 效率基准 / Efficiency Benchmark

- 价格接受者的效率基准 / Price-taking as benchmark：完全竞争被视为经济效率的基准。  
  Price-taking markets are the benchmark for economic efficiency.
- 生产效率 / Productive efficiency：企业在最低 $ATC$ 生产。  
  Firms produce at the lowest $ATC$.
- 配置效率 / Allocative efficiency：市场价格等于边际成本 $P=MC$。  
  Price equals marginal cost, $P=MC$.

### 图形要点 / Graph Cues

- $MC$ 上升并与 $ATC$ 相交于 $ATC$ 的最低点；$SRAVC$ 为 U 形且在 $ATC$ 之下。  
  Upward-sloping $MC$ crosses $ATC$ at its minimum; $SRAVC$ is U-shaped below $ATC$.
- 水平的 $p^*$ 线与 $MR=MC$ 处相交，长期还与 $ATC$ 相交（零利润）。  
  A horizontal $p^*$ intersects at $MR=MC$ and, in LR, also meets $ATC$ (zero profit).

## 总结（右页新增） / Summary (Right Page)

### 例题：哪些行为可能“经济有效率”？ / Which behaviors may be economically efficient?

1. 拖延 / Procrastinating：可能高效——若把时间转去更高价值的事或等待更好时机。  
   Possibly efficient if it reallocates time to higher‑value tasks or a better timing window.
2. 使用 1 美分硬币流通 / Using pennies as currency：不高效——铸造一枚 1 美分约需 3.6 美分（成本高于面值）。  
   Likely inefficient: minting a penny costs about 3.6 cents, exceeding its face value.
3. 对着奶壶直接喝 / Drinking milk directly from a jug：可能高效——省杯子、少清洗、节省时间。  
   Potentially efficient: saves a cup, reduces washing, and saves time.
