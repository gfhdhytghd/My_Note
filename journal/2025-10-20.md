### 核心要点 / Key Points
- 气体性质：充满容器、可压缩、密度低 / Gas properties: fill container, compressible, low density.
- 压强 P 源自气体分子与容器壁的碰撞 / Pressure P arises from molecular collisions with walls.
- 与固液相比，理想气体中分子彼此相距较远、相互作用可忽略 / In ideal gases, molecules are far apart with negligible interactions versus solids/liquids.
- 气体动理论五条假设：粒子体积忽略；持续无规则运动；无相互作用；弹性碰撞；平均动能 ∝ 温度 / KMT postulates: negligible volume; constant random motion; no interactions; elastic collisions; average KE ∝ temperature.
- 同温度下不同气体的平均动能相同；温度越高平均动能越大 / At the same T, different gases have equal average KE; higher T → higher average KE.

### 关键公式 / Key Equations
- 理想气体方程 / Ideal Gas Law: $PV=nRT$；亦可写为 $PV=Nk_BT$（$N$ 为分子数）/ Also $PV=Nk_BT$ where $N$ is molecule count.
- 平均动能（分子/每摩尔）/ Average kinetic energy (molecule/mol): $\langle E_k\rangle=\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT$；$\bar{E}_k=\tfrac{3}{2}RT$。
- 均方根速率 / RMS speed: $u_{rms}=\sqrt{\tfrac{3RT}{M}}$（$M$ 为摩尔质量，单位 kg·mol$^{-1}$）/ where $M$ is molar mass in kg·mol$^{-1}$.
- 气体密度 / Gas density: $\rho=\tfrac{PM}{RT}$（由理想气体方程与物质的量定义推出）/ follows from ideal gas law and amount definitions.
- 道尔顿分压定律 / Dalton’s law: $P_{tot}=\sum_i P_i$，且 $P_i=x_iP_{tot}$（$x_i$ 为摩尔分数）/ and $P_i=x_iP_{tot}$ where $x_i$ is mole fraction.
- 格雷厄姆定律 / Graham’s law: $\frac{r_1}{r_2}=\sqrt{\frac{M_2}{M_1}}$（同温同压；$r$ 为逸散/扩散速率，$M$ 为摩尔质量）/ at same T and P; $r$ is effusion/diffusion rate, $M$ is molar mass. 亦可写为 $\frac{r_1}{r_2}=\sqrt{\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}}$。
