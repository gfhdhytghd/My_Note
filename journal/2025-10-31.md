## 化学整理 / Chemistry Notes

### 表面张力要点 / Surface Tension Highlights
- 表面张力$\gamma$理解为界面自由能密度，推导$\gamma=F/L=\Delta E/\Delta A$，并用肥皂膜实验体会保持最小面积趋势。  
  Understood surface tension $\gamma$ as interfacial free-energy density via $\gamma=F/L=\Delta E/\Delta A$, using the soap-film experiment to see its area-minimizing drive.
- 将分子间作用力角度联系回 [[Chemistry/6.Intermolecular_Forces/intermolecular_forces]]，强调强氢键液体如何抬高$\gamma$并解释水滴近球形。  
  Reconnected with [[Chemistry/6.Intermolecular_Forces/intermolecular_forces]] to stress how strong hydrogen-bonded liquids elevate $\gamma$, explaining the nearly spherical water droplets.
- 记录温度依赖关系$d\gamma/dT<0$且在临界点附近趋近于0，提醒实验时需掌控温度并考虑表面活性剂影响。  
  Logged the dependence $d\gamma/dT<0$ with $\gamma\to0$ near the critical point, noting the need to control temperature and account for surfactants in experiments.

### 粘度与温度 / Viscosity vs Temperature
- 粘度定义为液体对流动的阻力；分子间作用力越强或分子越大时粘度越高，而温度升高会降低粘度并加速流动。  
  Defined viscosity as resistance to flow; stronger intermolecular forces or larger molecules raise viscosity, while higher temperature lowers viscosity and speeds flow.

### 溶液基本概念 / Solution Basics
- 溶液是均相混合物，溶质为少量组分，溶剂为主要组分；水作溶剂时称为水溶液。  
  Noted that solutions are homogeneous mixtures; the minor component is the solute, the major component the solvent, and water-based mixtures are aqueous solutions.

### 溶解过程能量剖分 / Dissolution Enthalpy Breakdown
- 将溶解焓拆分为$\Delta H_1$（破坏溶质内相互作用）、$\Delta H_2$（破坏溶剂间作用力）与$\Delta H_3$（形成溶质-溶剂作用），并有$\Delta H_\mathrm{soln}=\Delta H_1+\Delta H_2+\Delta H_3$。  
  Decomposed solution enthalpy into $\Delta H_1$ (disrupt solute interactions), $\Delta H_2$ (disrupt solvent interactions), and $\Delta H_3$ (form solute–solvent interactions), giving $\Delta H_\mathrm{soln}=\Delta H_1+\Delta H_2+\Delta H_3$.

### 水合与溶剂化壳层 / Hydration & Solvation Shells
- 以NaCl溶于水为例：先破坏晶格的离子相互作用，再松动水分子氢键，最终靠离子-偶极作用形成水合壳层推动溶解。  
  Illustrated NaCl dissolution: lattice ionic forces break, water hydrogen bonds loosen, and ion–dipole interactions build hydration shells that drive dissolution.

### 溶解焓符号与示例 / Solution Enthalpy Signposts
- $\Delta H_\mathrm{soln}$可正可负，CuSO4溶解放热而$\ce{NH4NO3}$吸热，提示实验需关注温度变化及能量对比。  
  Emphasized that $\Delta H_\mathrm{soln}$ spans exothermic (e.g., CuSO4) to endothermic (e.g., $\ce{NH4NO3}$) dissolutions, so experiments should monitor temperature shifts and energy balance.

### 自发性与熵推动 / Spontaneity & Entropy Drivers
- 即便溶解吸热，只要混合带来足够熵增（无序度提升），过程依然可能自发进行；放热或熵增任一具备即可驱动。  
  Highlighted that endothermic dissolutions can still be spontaneous when entropy gain from mixing is large; either energy release or sufficient disorder increase can propel a process.

### 疏水效应与蛋白折叠 / Hydrophobic Effect & Protein Folding
- 疏水效应来源于水分子熵增：疏水基团聚集减少水分子定向束缚，使$\Delta S_\mathrm{water}>0$，尽管蛋白自身熵降低。  
  Described hydrophobic effect as arising from increased water entropy: clustering hydrophobes frees water orientation, yielding $\Delta S_\mathrm{water}>0$ even as protein entropy drops.
- 蛋白折叠的能量收支：内部新IMFs形成带来有利$\Delta H<0$，而破坏水-蛋白氢键和链构象自由度则不利，需综合考量熵焓权衡。  
  Noted folding energetics: forming intraprotein IMFs makes $\Delta H$ favorable, but breaking water–protein H-bonds and constraining the chain are unfavorable, so folding depends on net $\Delta H/\Delta S$ balance.

### 溶液导电性 / Solution Conductivity
- 溶液能点亮小灯泡依赖可自由迁移的离子，通电时阴阳离子分别向正负电极运动形成电流。  
  Reviewed conductivity’s essence: a solution lights the bulb only when mobile ions migrate under potential, with anions toward the positive electrode and cations toward the negative, establishing current.

### 电解质分类 / Electrolyte Classification
- 整理电解质分类：强电解质（完全离解）、弱电解质（部分离解）与非电解质（不离解），并强调只有生成大量离子的溶液才是良导体。  
  Summarized electrolyte classes: strong (complete dissociation), weak (partial), and nonelectrolytes (no ions), highlighting that abundant ions are required for good conductivity.

### 强酸记忆清单 / Strong Acid Roster
- 识记七种强酸：HCl、HBr、HI、HClO3、HClO4、H2SO4、HNO3，提醒其在水中完全生成$\ce{H3O+}$与相应阴离子。  
  Memorized the seven strong acids—HCl, HBr, HI, HClO3, HClO4, H2SO4, HNO3—and noted their full conversion to $\ce{H3O+}$ plus counter anions in water.

### 强碱记忆清单 / Strong Base Roster
- 识记八种强碱：LiOH、NaOH、KOH、RbOH、CsOH、Ca(OH)2、Sr(OH)2、Ba(OH)2，并对比弱碱如$\ce{NH3}$、Mg(OH)2、胺类仅部分产生$\ce{OH-}$。  
  Memorized the eight strong bases—LiOH, NaOH, KOH, RbOH, CsOH, Ca(OH)2, Sr(OH)2, Ba(OH)2—contrasting them with weak bases like $\ce{NH3}$, Mg(OH)2, and amines that yield little $\ce{OH-}$.
