## 亨利定律与气体溶解度 / Henry's Law & Gas Solubility
- 仅气体溶解度随外部压力显著变化，而液体与固体在常见范围内几乎不受影响。  Only dissolved gases respond strongly to pressure changes; liquids and solids are nearly pressure-independent.
- 亨利定律表述为 $C_g=kP_g$，其中 $C_g$ 为气体溶解度、$P_g$ 为气体分压、$k$ 为与溶剂间分子间作用力相关的常数。  Henry's law uses $C_g=kP_g$ where $k$ captures IMF strength between the gas and solvent.
- 强氢键或偶极作用的气体拥有更大的 $k$ 值，因此在相同分压下溶解更多；图像上体现为更陡的斜率。  Gases forming stronger IMFs with water have larger $k$ and steeper solubility–pressure slopes, so more dissolves at the same partial pressure.
- 碳酸饮料装瓶时在 1.5–2.0 atm 的 $\text{CO}_2$ 分压下达到高溶解度；开盖后分压骤降至近 0 atm，溶解度降低导致气泡逸出。  Soda stays saturated under 1.5–2.0 atm $\text{CO}_2$ headspace, but once opened the drop to ~0 atm drives supersaturation and bubbling.

# Economics
## Core assumptions of utility theory

(1) Consumers always choose the highest valued alternative
(and assign that the highest utility)
Ex: which would you choose to eat?
A) Domino’s breakfast pizza
B) Grasshoppers and noodles
C) Premium steakhouse
C being preferred to A & Bmeans that the utility of C is higher than the utility of A or B.
If A is preferred to B, it means that the utility of A is higher than the utility of B.
Ex of a valid utility assignment to match the above preferences:
Utility of A = 6
Utility of B = 1
Utility of C = 30

(2) One good can be substituted for another (utility assigned for each good).
Ex: Would you sell, at some price, a ring your grandfather gave you before he passed?
Answering no violates this assumption.
(3) Decisions can be made without perfect information.
– People choose their best option given the information they have.
(4) More of a good thing is not always better.
