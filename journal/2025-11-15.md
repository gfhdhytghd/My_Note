# Chemistry

## 理想产率 / Theoretical Yield

- 定义 / Definition: 理想产率（理论产率）是在反应完全、无副反应、无损失且按化学计量进行时，所能得到的目标产物的最大量。  
  Theoretical yield is the maximum amount of product predicted by stoichiometry assuming complete reaction with no side reactions or losses.
- 计算步骤 / Steps:（1）配平化学方程；（2）将给定反应物转换为摩尔数；（3）用反应系数求出每个反应物“可生成”的产物摩尔数；（4）取最小者作为理论产率对应的产物摩尔数；（5）如需质量，乘以产物摩尔质量。  
  Steps: (1) balance the equation; (2) convert given reactants to moles; (3) use coefficients to compute moles of product from each reactant; (4) the smallest is the theoretical yield in moles; (5) multiply by product molar mass for mass.
- 百分产率 / Percent yield: $\%\,\text{yield}=\dfrac{\text{actual}}{\text{theoretical}}\times100\%$。  
  Percent yield: $\%\,\text{yield}=\dfrac{\text{actual}}{\text{theoretical}}\times100\%$.
- 相关笔记 / See also: [[Chemistry/1.Atom_and_Mole/mole_concept]]（化学计量基础）  
  See also: [[Chemistry/1.Atom_and_Mole/mole_concept]] (stoichiometry basics)

## 受限反应物 / Limiting Reactant

- 定义 / Definition: 受限反应物（限量试剂）是首先被消耗完、限制产物生成量的反应物；其决定理论产率。  
  The limiting reactant is consumed first and limits product formation; it determines the theoretical yield.
- 判定方法 / Tests: 将各反应物各自“可生成”的产物量（或与目标产物的计量比）进行比较，给出最小值者对应的反应物即为受限反应物。亦可比较“所需/可用”的计量比，缺口更大的为受限。  
  Determine by computing product from each reactant via coefficients and picking the smallest; equivalently compare required/available mole ratios, the larger deficit indicates the limiting one.
- 实例 / Example: 合成氨 $\ce{N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3}$。若 $\,m(\ce{N2})=14.0\,\text{g}$ 与 $\,m(\ce{H2})=2.0\,\text{g}$：  
  Example: Haber synthesis $\ce{N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3}$. Given $\,m(\ce{N2})=14.0\,\text{g}$ and $\,m(\ce{H2})=2.0\,\text{g}$:
  - 转摩尔 / To moles: $n(\ce{N2})=14.0/28.0=0.50\,\text{mol}$；$n(\ce{H2})=2.0/2.0=1.0\,\text{mol}$。  
    $n(\ce{N2})=0.50\,\text{mol}$; $n(\ce{H2})=1.0\,\text{mol}$.
  - 需求比 / Required: 1 mol $\ce{N2}$ 需 3 mol $\ce{H2}$；对 $0.50$ mol $\ce{N2}$ 需 $1.50$ mol $\ce{H2}$，但仅有 $1.0$ mol，故 $\ce{H2}$ 为受限反应物。  
    Stoichiometry: 1 mol $\ce{N2}$ needs 3 mol $\ce{H2}$; for $0.50$ mol $\ce{N2}$ it needs $1.50$ mol $\ce{H2}$ but only $1.0$ mol available, so $\ce{H2}$ is limiting.
  - 理想产率 / Theoretical: 由 $\ce{H2}$ 出发，$1.0\,\text{mol}\,\ce{H2} \to \tfrac{2}{3}\,\text{mol}\,\ce{NH3}=0.667\,\text{mol}$；质量 $m=0.667\times17.03\approx11.35\,\text{g}$。  
    From $\ce{H2}$: $1.0\,\text{mol}\,\ce{H2} \to \tfrac{2}{3}\,\text{mol}\,\ce{NH3}=0.667\,\text{mol}$; mass $m=0.667\times17.03\approx11.35\,\text{g}$.
  - 若实得 $10.0\,\text{g}$，百分产率 $\approx 10.0/11.35\times100\%\approx88\%$。  
    If actual is $10.0\,\text{g}$, percent yield $\approx 88\%$.

# Economics
## 医疗中的报酬递减 / Diminishing Returns in Healthcare

- 关键现象 / Key idea: 医疗服务具有边际报酬递减特征——接受的医疗越多，每增加一单位医疗带来的额外健康收益越小。  
  Healthcare exhibits diminishing returns: the more care you receive, the smaller the additional health benefit from each extra unit of care.
- 医疗支出分布高度不均 / Skewed spending:  
  Healthcare spending is highly skewed across people:
  - 底部 50% 支出者仅占全部医疗支出的 3%。  
    The bottom 50% of spenders consume only 3% of all healthcare.
- 提炼为 / Extracted to：card![[Economics/17.Uncertainty_Asymmetric_Information/health_insurance_information_problems]]
- 社会问题 / Social question: 医疗的社会收益是否值得政府为之付出的财政成本？  
  Core question: is the social benefit of healthcare worth its cost to the government?
- 财政比重 / Fiscal weight: 约 25% 的联邦政府支出用于医疗相关项目（如 Medicare、Medicaid 等）。  
  Around 25% of federal spending goes to healthcare programs (e.g., Medicare, Medicaid).

   Consumers
   - 患者 / Patients  
     Patients
   - 政府（公共支付方）/ Government as payer in Medicare/Medicaid  
     Government when it pays for care via programs such as Medicare and Medicaid.
2. 生产者 / Producers

     They mitigate individual risk by pooling and smoothing large medical expenses.
4. 制药企业 / Pharmaceutical companies  
   Pharmaceutical companies that develop and sell drugs and treatments.

## 医疗保险基本术语 / Insurance Terms in Healthcare

- 保费 / Premium: 每月为获得保险保障而支付的金额。  
  Premium: the monthly payment for insurance coverage.
- 共付额 / Copay: 每次就诊或治疗时由消费者支付的固定金额。  
  Copay: a fixed dollar amount paid by the consumer per visit or treatment.
- 免赔额 / Deductible: 在保险开始报销之前，投保人必须先自行支付到的总金额。  
  Deductible: the total amount that must be paid out of pocket before policy benefits apply.
- 共同保险 / Co-insurance: 超过免赔额之后，由投保人按一定比例承担的医疗费用。  
  Co-insurance: the percentage of costs paid by the consumer after the deductible is reached, up to a limit.

## 医疗费用为何高企？ / Why Are Healthcare Costs So High?

### 信息不对称 / Asymmetric Information

- 定义 / Definition: 当交易或决策发生时，一方掌握的信息明显多于另一方，这种信息不均衡会扭曲市场结果。  
  Asymmetric information occurs when one party knows more than the other at the time of decision-making, distorting market outcomes.

### 例子 / Examples

- 二手车市场 / Used car markets: 只有卖家真正了解车况好坏，买家很难分辨“好车/坏车”。  
  Only the seller truly knows the quality of the car; buyers cannot easily distinguish good cars from bad ones.
- 劳动合同 / Firm-employee relationship: 员工真实的努力程度和工作强度对雇主而言是部分不可观察的。  
  The true amount of effort an employee exerts is partly private information to the worker.
- 保险市场 / Insurance: 投保人的风险类型（高风险/低风险）对保险公司来说往往不可见。  
  In insurance, the risk level of the buyer is often unknown to the insurer.

## 逆向选择：医疗保险市场 / Adverse Selection in Health Insurance

- 定义 / Definition: 信息更充分的一方只在对自己有利时才愿意交易，导致高质量一方被挤出市场。  
  Adverse selection occurs when the informed side trades only when it benefits them, driving high-quality types or goods out of the market.

### 医疗支出类型 / Healthcare spending types

- 低支出人群 / Low spenders: 每月医疗支出 \$50。  
  Low spenders spend \$50 per month on healthcare.
- 高支出人群 / High spenders: 每月医疗支出 \$150。  
  High spenders spend \$150 per month on healthcare.
- 保险公司不知道是谁高支出、谁低支出，且假设两类人出现的概率各为 $1/2$。  
  Insurance companies do not know which type a customer is; assume each type is equally likely with probability $1/2$.

### 公平定价（所有人都参保）/ Fair Price if Everyone Buys Insurance

- 期望成本 / Expected value:  
  Expected cost per customer is  
  $EV=\tfrac{1}{2}(50)+\tfrac{1}{2}(150)=100$。
  - 若将保费定为 \$100，保险公司期望利润为零（公平保费）。  
    A premium of \$100 gives the insurer zero expected profit.

### 谁会愿意参保？ / Who actually buys?

- 对低支出者 / Low spenders: 若缴 \$100 保费但预期支出只有 \$50，则净损失 \$50，不会参保。  
  Low spenders paying \$100 for expected costs of \$50 lose \$50 and will not buy insurance.
- 结果 / Outcome: 只剩高支出者购买保险，保险公司为避免亏损会将保费提高到 \$150，只服务高支出人群。  
  As low spenders exit, only high spenders remain; insurers then set the premium to \$150 and effectively insure only high spenders.
- 这就是逆向选择：高质量/低风险类型被挤出市场，市场风险结构恶化。  
  This is adverse selection: low-risk types leave the pool and the market ends up serving only high-risk types.

## 柠檬市场模型 / Used Car Market (Lemons Model)

### 情景设定 / Scenario

- 一位销售员出售三种车 / A salesman sells three types of cars:  
  A salesman sells three kinds of cars:
  - 柠檬车（低质量）/ Lemon: 价值 \$4{,}000。  
    Lemon car worth \$4,000.
  - 中等车 / Middle: 价值 \$6{,}000。  
    Middle-quality car worth \$6,000.
  - 好车（桃子）/ Peach: 价值 \$8{,}000。  
    Peach (high-quality) car worth \$8,000.
- 买家不知道车的真实质量，三种车出现的概率都为 $1/3$。  
  The buyer does not know quality; each type occurs with probability $1/3$.

### 情形一：所有车都在市场上 / Case 1: All cars are sold

- 期望价值 / Expected value:  
  $EV=\tfrac{1}{3}(4000)+\tfrac{1}{3}(6000)+\tfrac{1}{3}(8000)=6000$。  
  The expected value to the buyer is \$6,000.
- 买家愿意支付约 \$6{,}000。  
  The buyer is willing to pay \$6,000.
- 但对于车主 / But for owners:  
  - 对于好车（价值 \$8{,}000），以 \$6{,}000 出售太便宜，因此好车车主退出市场。  
    Peach owners will not sell at \$6,000 because it is too low for an \$8,000 car.

### 情形二：只剩柠檬车和中等车 / Case 2: Only lemon and middle cars remain

- 新的期望价值 / New expected value:  
  $EV=\tfrac{1}{2}(4000)+\tfrac{1}{2}(6000)=5000$。  
  The expected value now is \$5,000.
- 买家最多愿意支付约 \$5{,}000。  
  The buyer is willing to pay \$5,000.
- 然而 / However: 对于价值 \$6{,}000 的中等车，这个价格仍然太低，因此中等车车主也退出。  
  For owners of \$6,000 middle cars, \$5,000 is too low, so they exit the market as well.

### 最终结果 / Final outcome

- 市场上只剩柠檬车 / Only lemons remain in the market。  
  Only lemon cars are traded in equilibrium.
- 由于逆向选择，高质量车和中等车被挤出，市场平均质量不断下降。  
  Because of adverse selection, higher-quality cars are driven out and average market quality collapses.
