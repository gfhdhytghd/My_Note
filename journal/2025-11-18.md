### 1. Enzymes as Biological Catalysts / 酶作为生物催化剂
- Enzymes are large biomolecules (thousands of amu).  
  酶是由上千原子质量单位组成的大型生物大分子。
- Reactants (substrates) bind at the active site, a cavity whose shape and chemical groups complement the substrate.  
  反应物（底物）在酶的活性位点结合，该位点是形状和官能团都与底物互补的“口袋”。
- High specificity → only certain substrates bind → reaction control.  
  高专一性意味着只有特定底物能结合，从而精确控制反应。∂
- Enzymes allow the cell to regulate when/where reactions occur.  
  酶使细胞能够调控反应发生的时间和空间位置。

**Enzyme Catalysis Cycle / 酶催化循环**
1. Free enzyme (E) + substrate (S).  
   游离酶 E 与底物 S 结合。
2. Formation of E–S complex.  
   形成酶–底物复合物 E–S。
3. Reaction occurs at active site.  
   反应在活性位点发生。
4. Product released → E–P complex → free enzyme.  
   生成物释放，经 E–P 复合物后恢复为游离酶。
5. Enzyme reused repeatedly (catalyst).  
   酶不被消耗，可多次循环使用。

Enzymes accelerate reactions by:  
酶通过以下方式加速反应：
- Holding reactants close and oriented correctly.  
  把反应物拉近并以合适取向排列。
- Correctly positioning amino acids for chemistry.  
  准确定位参与反应的氨基酸残基。
- Stabilizing high-energy transition states.  
  稳定高能的过渡态。

### 2. How Drugs Work / 药物如何起作用

Most drugs are small molecules that:  
大多数药物是小分子，它们会：
- Bind to a target protein (often an enzyme).  
  与靶蛋白（通常是酶）结合。
- Modify its activity — typically inhibition (turning the enzyme off).  
  改变其活性，最常见是抑制，使酶“关机”。

Drug-bound enzyme → no substrate conversion → biological effect.  
药物与酶结合后，底物不能被转化，从而产生生理效应。

---

### 3. Types of Inhibitors / 抑制剂的类型

**1. Competitive Inhibitors / 竞争性抑制剂**
- Bind directly to the active site.  
  直接占据酶的活性位点。
- Compete with substrate; substrate cannot bind.  
  与底物竞争，使底物无法结合。

**2. Noncompetitive Inhibitors / 非竞争性抑制剂**
- Bind outside the active site.  
  结合在活性位点之外的其他部位。
- Cause structural changes → active site no longer works.  
  引起酶构象改变，使活性位点失效。

**3. Covalent Inhibitors / 共价抑制剂**
- Form irreversible covalent bonds.  
  形成不可逆的共价键。
- Permanently deactivate the enzyme.  
  永久性地使酶失活。

---

### 4. Specificity of Drug Binding / 药物结合的特异性

Effective drugs must:  
有效药物需要：
- Bind specifically to their intended protein.  
  对目标蛋白具有高特异性结合。
- Avoid off-target binding → reduce side effects.  
  尽量减少“脱靶”结合，从而降低副作用。

Drug must fit physically in the active site:  
药物在空间上必须适配活性位点：
- Too large or wrong shape → cannot enter.  
  过大或形状不匹配时进不去口袋。
- Too small → fewer IMFs formed → weak binding.  
  太小则可形成的分子间作用力减少，结合变弱。

Example shown: Gefitinib (anticancer drug).  
课堂示例：抗癌药物吉非替尼（Gefitinib）。

---

### 5. Thermodynamics of Drug Binding / 药物结合的热力学

Drug binding is favorable when:  
药物结合在热力学上有利通常满足：

ΔH < 0 (enthalpy is exothermic).  
焓变 ΔH < 0，即放热，有利于结合。

ΔS > 0 (entropy increases).  
熵变 ΔS > 0，体系无序度增加，也有利于结合。

---

### 6. Enthalpy (ΔH) Contributions / 焓变 ΔH 的贡献

| Process                    | Favorable? | Explanation                                   |
|----------------------------|-----------:|-----------------------------------------------|
| Breaking drug–water IMFs   | ❌         | Requires energy                               |
| Breaking protein–water IMFs| ❌         | Also requires energy                          |
| Forming drug–protein IMFs  | ✔️         | Strong IMFs lower enthalpy                    |
| Forming water–water IMFs   | ✔️         | Released water molecules re-form H-bond network |

Overall ΔH can still be negative if favorable interactions outweigh losses.  
如果新形成的有利相互作用足够强，总体 ΔH 依然可以为负。

对应中文解释：  
- 断裂药物–水分子间作用力是吸热过程，不利于结合。  
- 断裂蛋白–水分子作用同样吸热。  
- 形成药物–蛋白之间的新作用力会放热，降低焓。  
- 被排出的水恢复水–水氢键网络，也会释放能量。

---

### 7. Entropy (ΔS) Contributions / 熵变 ΔS 的贡献

**Unfavorable (ΔS < 0) / 不利贡献（ΔS < 0）**
1. Loss of freedom of motion – Enzyme + drug → enzyme–drug complex.  
   自由度降低：酶与药物形成复合物后整体自由运动减少。
- Fewer rotational/vibrational states.  
  可用的转动、振动状态变少。
- Molecules become more constrained.  
  分子被限制在更固定的构象。

2. Loss of conformational flexibility.  
  构象灵活性损失：
- Drug must adopt a specific shape inside binding pocket.  
  药物在口袋中需要固定为特定构象。

**Favorable (ΔS > 0) / 有利贡献（ΔS > 0）**
3. Release of ordered water molecules (hydrophobic effect).  
  释放有序水分子（疏水效应）。
- Hydrophobic surfaces → water forms structured shells.  
  疏水表面会迫使水分子排成有序“壳层”。
- Binding buries hydrophobic regions → water released.  
  结合掩埋疏水区域，这些有序水分子被释放回体相。
- Major positive entropy contribution.  
  这是产生正熵变的主要来源。

This is often the dominant contributor that makes ΔS > 0.  
通常正是这一项使得总熵变 ΔS 变为正值。

---

### 8. The Hydrophobic Effect / 疏水效应

When hydrophobic parts of protein and drug come together:  
当蛋白和药物的疏水部分靠在一起时：
- Ordered water molecules are released.  
  原本排列有序的水分子被释放。
- System entropy increases.  
  体系整体熵增加。
- Binding becomes more favorable.  
  结合过程在热力学上更有利。

This is a central idea in biochemistry and drug design.  
疏水效应是生物化学与药物设计中的核心概念之一。

---

### 9. Structural Fit and Drug Optimization / 结构适配与药物优化

Changing a drug’s structure affects:  
改变药物结构会影响：
- Shape complementarity.  
  与蛋白结合口袋的形状互补性。
- Conformational freedom.  
  分子的构象自由度与柔性。
- Number and type of IMFs.  
  可形成的分子间作用力的数量与类型。
- Accessibility to active site.  
  是否能顺利进入并到达活性位点。

Good drug design balances:  
优秀药物设计需要平衡：
- Fit (enthalpy).  
  形状与作用力匹配（偏向焓的贡献）。
- Flexibility (entropy).  
  适度的柔性与熵的损失。
- Hydrophobic/hydrophilic interactions.  
  疏水与亲水相互作用的分布。

---

### Summary / 小结

Drug binding is governed by:  
药物与蛋白结合主要由以下因素决定：
- Specificity of fit.  
  结合的特异性与结构匹配。
- Inhibition mechanism.  
  抑制机制类型。
- Enthalpy (IMFs formed vs broken).  
  焓变：新旧分子间作用力的得失。
- Entropy (loss of freedom vs hydrophobic effect).  
  熵变：自由度损失与疏水效应之间的权衡。

A drug binds well when:  
药物“结合得好”通常意味着：
- It fits the active site.  
  形状上适配活性位点。
- Forms strong interactions.  
  能形成足够强的相互作用力。
- Releases ordered water.  
  结合过程中释放有序水分子。
- Achieves ΔG = ΔH − TΔS < 0.  
  最终自由能变 ΔG = ΔH − TΔS 为负，结合自发。

# Economics

## Solutions to Adverse Selection / 逆向选择的解决方案

- 信息收集 / More information: 保险公司或市场方通过更多信息收集来识别风险类型（例如了解投保人是否吸烟、既往病史、车辆质量等），从而更准确地定价。  
  More information gathering about people buying insurance or the quality of the car (e.g., asking if someone smokes) allows insurers to classify risk and price policies more accurately.
- 风险池 / Pooling: 通过雇主提供统一的团体保险计划，把高风险和低风险人群打包在一个池子里，减少“只有高风险者留下来”的逆向选择问题。  
  Pooling in employer-sponsored health plans forces all employees to join the same pool, mixing high-risk and low-risk individuals and mitigating adverse selection.

## Other Asymmetric Information Issues / 其他信息不对称问题

### Principal–Agent Problem / 委托–代理问题

- 定义 / Definition: 委托人（如患者或保险公司）雇佣代理人（如医生）代表自己做决策，但无法完全观察代理人的努力和行为。  
  The principal (patient or insurer) hires an agent (doctor) to act on their behalf but cannot perfectly monitor the agent’s effort or actions.
- 医疗情景 / Healthcare example: 患者让医生安排检查和治疗，但看不到医生到底付出了多少努力，也难以判断治疗失败是“运气不好”还是“努力不足”。  
  In healthcare, a patient asks a doctor to order tests or treatments, but cannot tell whether a bad outcome is due to bad luck or lack of effort from the doctor.

**Solutions / 可能的解决方案**
- 加强监督 / Monitoring: 通过同行评议、医疗记录审查、质量考核等方式更密切地监督医生行为。  
  Monitor doctors more closely via peer review, chart audits, and quality metrics.
- 设计激励合约 / Incentive contracts: 通过绩效工资、按结果付费等机制，让医生在得到好结果时有额外激励，从而更愿意努力。  
  Design contracts that reward good outcomes (pay-for-performance, bonuses) so that agents have incentives aligned with principals.

### Moral Hazard / 道德风险

- 定义 / Definition: 一旦人们通过保险等方式被风险保护，可能会从事更冒险的行为，因为坏结果的成本部分由他人承担。  
  Moral hazard occurs when people take riskier actions because insurance or protection means they do not bear the full cost of bad outcomes.
- 例子 / Example: 有了汽车保险后可能更大胆驾驶；在健康保险下可能更倾向于多用医疗服务。  
  Example: driving more aggressively after buying car insurance or consuming more healthcare because it is insured.

**Mitigation / 缓解方式**
- 经验费率 / Experience rating: 根据历史行为和理赔记录提高或降低保费，以奖励好行为、惩罚坏行为（在健康保险上实施有难度）。  
  Use higher or lower premiums based on past claims and behavior (experience rating), though this is harder to implement in health insurance.
- 共付机制 / Cost sharing: 通过自付额、共同支付（copay）和共保比例（coinsurance）让投保人承担部分边际成本，从而抑制过度消费。  
  Use deductibles, copays, and coinsurance so that insured individuals still face part of the marginal cost and are less likely to overuse services.

## Single Payer vs Private Healthcare / 单一支付方与私营医疗体系（加拿大 vs 美国）

- 美国情况 / United States: 医疗总成本很高，2022 年约有 8.4% 的人口没有医疗保险。  
  In the US, healthcare is expensive and about 8.4% of the population lacked health insurance in 2022.

- 加拿大情况 / Canada:
  - 普及性 / Coverage: 所有人都能获得基本医疗服务（全民覆盖）。  
    Everyone has access to basic healthcare under a single-payer system.
  - 配给 / Rationing: 由于预算有限，医疗服务的数量和排队等待时间必须被“配给”，非危及生命治疗可能等待较久。  
    Because the budget is limited, quantities of care are rationed and wait times for non-life-threatening treatments can be long.
  - 管制 / Regulation: 私人按服务收费的行医模式受到严格限制或非法；政府设定支付给提供者的价格。  
    Private fee-for-service practice is heavily restricted or illegal, and the government sets rates paid to providers.
  - 医院财政 / Hospital funding: 医院主要通过政府拨款获得资金。  
    Hospitals receive funding largely through government grants.
  - 支付结构 / Payer mix: 政府大约支付全部医疗支出的 70%；牙科、处方药和长期护理更多由私人保险或自付承担。  
    The government covers roughly 70% of total healthcare costs, while dental care, prescriptions, and long-term care are often covered by private insurance or out-of-pocket.
  - 跨国就医 / Cross-border care: 因等待时间较长，一些加拿大人会选择去其他国家寻求非急症治疗。  
    Due to long wait times, some Canadians travel abroad for non-life-threatening procedures.
