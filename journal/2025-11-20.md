### 1. Introduction / 1. 引言

本节课主要介绍 RNA 的结构与化学性质，并重点讲解化学反应中电子移动的表示方法——也就是常说的箭推法（arrow pushing）。  
This lecture introduces RNA structure and chemistry, and focuses on how to represent electron movement in reactions, i.e., arrow pushing.

化学反应本质上是价电子的重新排列，导致键的断裂与形成。  
Chemical reactions are essentially rearrangements of valence electrons, leading to bond breaking and formation.

反应机理（reaction mechanism）描述这些电子从何而来、到何处去。  
Reaction mechanisms describe where the electrons come from and where they go.

### 2. Arrow Pushing 的基本规则 / 2. Basic rules of arrow pushing

1. 每一条箭代表一对价电子的移动。  
   Each curved arrow represents the movement of one pair of valence electrons.

2. 箭从电子对的来源处出发（孤对电子或键），指向电子最终到达的位置（一个原子或一个键）。  
   The arrow starts from the source of the electrons (a lone pair or a bond) and points to where the electrons end up (an atom or a bond).

3. 从原子开始的箭表示移动的是该原子的孤电子对。  
   An arrow starting at an atom indicates movement of that atom’s lone pair.

4. 从键中心开始的箭表示该键正在断裂。  
   An arrow starting from the middle of a bond indicates that bond is breaking.

5. 箭头指向一个原子表示形成新的共价键或新的孤电子对。  
   An arrow pointing to an atom shows the formation of a new covalent bond or a new lone pair on that atom.

6. 箭头指向一个键表示形成双键或三键（增加键级）。  
   An arrow pointing to a bond indicates formation of a double or triple bond (increase in bond order).

### 4. Nucleophile、Electrophile 与 Leaving Group / 4. Nucleophile, electrophile, and leaving group

**Nucleophile (Nu:) / 亲核试剂**  
• 意为“亲核试剂”，电子丰富（带部分负电或全负电），是电子对提供者，会攻击电子缺乏的原子。  
• A nucleophile is electron-rich (partially or fully negatively charged), donates electron pairs, and attacks electron-poor atoms.

**Electrophile (E) / 亲电子试剂**  
• “亲电子试剂”，带部分正电或全正电，是电子对接受者。  
• An electrophile is (partially or fully) positively charged and accepts electron pairs.

一般反应模式：电子丰富的原子攻击电子缺乏的原子，形成新键。  
In general, electron-rich atoms attack electron-poor atoms to form new bonds.

**Leaving Group (LG) / 离去基团**  
• 在反应中离去、带走键中电子的原子或基团。  
• A leaving group is the atom or group that departs in a reaction, taking the bonding electron pair with it.

• 离去时总是带走该键的电子对，单取代（single displacement）反应一定包含离去基团。  
• When it leaves, it always takes the bond’s electron pair; single-displacement reactions always involve a leaving group.

注意：反应前后电荷总和必须守恒。  
Note: the total charge must be conserved before and after a reaction.

### 5. 例子：碳酸（H₂CO₃）与水的反应机理 / 5. Example: mechanism of carbonic acid with water

反应：`H₂CO₃ + H₂O → HCO₃⁻ + H₃O⁺`  
Reaction: `H₂CO₃ + H₂O → HCO₃⁻ + H₃O⁺`

机理包含两条关键箭：  
The mechanism involves two key arrows:

1. 水分子氧上的孤对电子攻击碳酸中的氢：  
   The lone pair on the oxygen of water attacks a hydrogen on carbonic acid:  
   • 箭从水的 O 上的孤对电子指向碳酸的 H，表示形成新的 O–H 键。  
     The arrow goes from the lone pair on water’s oxygen to the hydrogen of carbonic acid, forming a new O–H bond.  
   • 在此反应中，水的氧是 nucleophile，氢是 electrophile。  
     Here, the oxygen of water is the nucleophile and the hydrogen is the electrophile.

2. 碳酸原本的 O–H 键断裂：  
   The original O–H bond in carbonic acid breaks:  
   • 箭从 H₂CO₃ 原来的 O–H 键指向该 O，该氧得到该电子对并形成新的孤电子对。  
     The arrow goes from the original O–H bond to the oxygen, which gains the electron pair and forms a new lone pair.  
   • 断裂产物 HCO₃⁻ 可视作带负电的离去基团。  
     The product HCO₃⁻ can be viewed as a negatively charged leaving group.

最终电荷保持平衡：反应物总电荷为 0，生成物为 (−1) + (+1) = 0。  
Overall charge is conserved: reactants have total charge 0; products have (−1) + (+1) = 0.

### 6. 总结 / 6. Summary

• Arrow pushing 是描述化学反应中电子转移的标准方法。  
  Arrow pushing is the standard way to describe electron transfer in chemical reactions.  
• Nucleophile = 电子提供者；Electrophile = 电子接受者。  
  Nucleophiles donate electrons; electrophiles accept electrons.  
• Leaving group = 离去并带走键中电子对的基团。  
  A leaving group departs with the bonding electron pair.  
• 所有反应机理必须遵守电子守恒与电荷守恒。  
  All mechanisms must obey conservation of electrons and total charge.

## Gambler's Fallacy

赌徒谬误指的是在一系列独立随机事件中，人们错误地认为过去的结果会“纠正”未来的结果，例如连续出现多次正面后就觉得反面“该来了”。  
The gambler's fallacy is the mistaken belief that past outcomes of independent random events will “correct” future outcomes, e.g., after many heads in a row one feels that tails is “due”.  

在公平抛硬币或轮盘赌中，每一次试验的概率都是独立且固定的（如抛硬币时正反面始终各为 50%），之前出现的长串同一结果不会改变下一次的概率。  
In a fair coin toss or roulette game, each trial is independent with fixed probabilities (e.g., heads and tails remain 50–50), and a long streak of one outcome does not change the probability of the next trial.  

赌徒谬误源于对“大数定律”的误解：大数定律只保证样本量足够大时长期频率接近期望概率，并不意味着短期内必须通过“补偿性结果”迅速回归平均。  
The fallacy arises from a misunderstanding of the law of large numbers: it guarantees that long‑run frequencies approach expected probabilities for large samples, not that short‑run results must be “compensated” to quickly revert to the mean.  

在行为经济学和金融市场中，赌徒谬误会导致投资者在股价连跌后过度自信地“抄底”，或在连涨后过度悲观地预期“必然下跌”，从而做出系统性错误决策。  
In behavioral economics and financial markets, the gambler's fallacy leads investors to buy too confidently after a price losing streak or to predict an inevitable crash after a rally, resulting in systematic decision errors.  

## 厌恶与框架效应 / Aversion and Framing Effect

厌恶（aversion）通常指人们对不公平或不平等结果的排斥，在分配或谈判情境中，人们会为了避免处于明显劣势而拒绝看似“理性”的提议。  
Aversion usually refers to people’s dislike of unfair or unequal outcomes; in allocation or bargaining settings, people may reject seemingly “rational” offers to avoid being placed at a clear disadvantage.  

内疚厌恶（guilt aversion）指人们厌恶因为自己造成他人受损而产生的负罪感，例如在最后通牒博弈中，提议者会为了不让对方感到被伤害或被羞辱，而主动给出更公平的分配方案。  
Guilt aversion captures people’s dislike of the guilt that comes from harming others; in the ultimatum game, for example, proposers often offer fairer splits to avoid making the responder feel hurt or insulted.  

框架效应（framing effect）指人们在面对逻辑等价的陈述或选择时，会因为问题或信息被“如何表述”而做出不同的决策。  
The framing effect means that when people face logically equivalent statements or choices, they may decide differently depending on how the question or information is framed.  

例：问“你更愿意选择 10% 的死亡率，还是 90% 的存活率？”时，两种表述在统计上完全等价，但前者强调损失、后者强调收益，常导致人们给出不同的偏好。  
Example: asking “Would you rather have a 10% mortality rate or a 90% survival rate?” uses statistically equivalent information, but the loss frame versus gain frame often leads to different preferences.  

例：在某些任务（如成为器官捐献者）中，如果需要主动“勾选同意”（opt in），参与者比例往往较低；若改为默认参加，只需勾选“退出”（opt out），参与者比例会显著上升，这体现了默认选项与框架对行为的强大影响。  
Example: for tasks such as becoming an organ donor, participation rates are typically low when people must actively “opt in”, but much higher when participation is the default and people must “opt out”, illustrating how default options and framing strongly shape behavior.  

这些厌恶与框架效应和赌徒谬误一样，都是行为经济学中的系统性偏差，可以进一步整理到 `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/behavioral_biases]]` 或 `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/framing_effect]]` 等条目中。  
Like the gambler’s fallacy, these aversion and framing effects are systematic biases studied in behavioral economics and can be organized into dedicated notes such as `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/behavioral_biases]]` or `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/framing_effect]]`.  

## 启动效应与现状偏好 / Priming Effect and Status-Quo Bias

启动效应（priming effect）指人们在不自觉的情况下，被某些词语、图像或动作“预先激活”相关联的概念，从而在随后的判断和行为中受到暗示性影响。  
The priming effect describes how people are unconsciously influenced by suggestive language, images, or actions that activate associated concepts, subtly shaping subsequent judgments and behavior.  

例：受试者在被采访关于某个角色之前，被不动声色地递上一杯热饮或冷饮（他们并未意识到这是实验的一部分）；结果显示，拿热饮的人更可能把该角色描述为“温暖友好”，而拿冷饮的人则更容易形容对方“冷淡疏离”。  
Example: in one study, participants were unconsciously given a hot or cold drink before being interviewed about a character; those holding a hot drink were more likely to describe the character as “warm and friendly”, whereas those holding a cold drink were more likely to see the character as “cold and distant”.  

现状偏好（status‑quo bias）指人们倾向于维持当前状态，哪怕改变本可以带来更好结果，只要改变需要额外思考或承担责任，人们往往会选择“保持原样”。  
Status‑quo bias is the tendency for people to stick with the current state of affairs, even when changing might yield better outcomes, because change requires extra thought or responsibility while “leaving things as they are” feels safer.  

这种对现状的依恋会影响退休金计划、保险、订阅服务等场景——一旦默认选项设定为“继续当前方案”，许多人即便知道有更优选择，也不会主动调整，从而产生显著的经济后果。  
This attachment to the status quo affects pension plans, insurance, subscription services, and more—once the default is to “keep the current plan”, many people refrain from switching even when they know there are better options, leading to sizable economic consequences.

Priming and status‑quo bias can likewise be grouped under `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/behavioral_biases]]`, or documented in separate notes such as `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/priming_effect]]` and `[[Economics/Behavioral_Economics/status_quo_bias]]`.
