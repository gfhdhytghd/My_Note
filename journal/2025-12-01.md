### Math
- 求和符号：用$\sum$表示连续加总，如$\sum_{i=1}^n a_i$或带权和$\sum_{i=1}^n w_i a_i$，注意上下限与求和索引对应；Summation notation uses $\sum$ for repeated addition, e.g., $\sum_{i=1}^n a_i$ or weighted $\sum_{i=1}^n w_i a_i$, with bounds matching the index.
- 左端点/右端点/中点近似：把区间$[a,b]$切成$n$份，$\Delta x=(b-a)/n$；左端点近似$L_n=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}f(a+i\Delta x)\Delta x$、右端点$R_n=\sum_{i=1}^n f(a+i\Delta x)\Delta x$、中点$M_n=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}f(a+(i+0.5)\Delta x)\Delta x$；Left/Right/Midpoint
- approximations split $[a,b]$ into $n$ parts with $\Delta x=(b-a)/n$; $L_n=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}f(a+i\Delta x)\Delta x$, $R_n=\sum_{i=1}^n f(a+i\Delta x)\Delta x$, $M_n=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}f(a+(i+0.5)\Delta x)\Delta x$.

## 抗生素讲义 / Antibiotic Lecture Notes
- 课程安排：未来三讲将先概览抗生素及其革新医疗与耐药威胁，再细看被多种抗生素抑制合成的细菌细胞壁，随后讨论阻断细胞壁构建的β-内酰胺类（青霉素、阿莫西林等）与耐药菌表达的β-内酰胺酶，最后介绍以不同机制阻断细胞壁合成的万古霉素；Over the next three lectures we will learn about antibiotic drugs, how they revolutionized medicine, the threat of resistance, the structure of the bacterial cell wall, β-lactam drugs (penicillin, amoxicillin) plus β-lactamase in resistant bacteria, and vancomycin’s distinct cell-wall inhibition.
- 概念定义：抗生素意为“反生命”，是抑制或杀灭生物功能的小分子（常见分子量<1000 g/mol），日常多指治疗细菌感染的药物；The term antibiotic literally means “against life,” referring to molecules that halt growth or kill, commonly used for bacterial infections and typically small (MW<1000 g/mol).
- 进化分野：细菌与真核（人类所属）在生命早期已分道，在分子层面差异巨大，使药物可以对细菌有毒但对宿主安全；A phylogenetic tree shows bacteria diverged early from eukaryotes, creating molecular differences that allow drugs to be toxic to bacteria yet safe for humans.
- 临床起点：1930年代磺胺类（如磺胺胺/sulfanilamide）开启抗生素临床使用，真正革命在1940年代早期青霉素问世，引发“黄金时代”，后续几十年发现数百种新抗生素；Clinical use began in the 1930s with sulfonamides such as sulfanilamide, then penicillin’s early-1940s debut sparked a golden age yielding hundreds of novel antibiotics.
- 医疗影响：抗生素为原本难治的疾病提供治愈手段，也让手术风险大降，促进诸如髋关节置换等非致命病症的手术普及，显著提升寿命与生活质量；Antibiotics cured formerly intractable diseases, transformed surgery by reducing deadly infections, enabled elective operations like hip replacements, and improved longevity and quality of life.
- 耐药定义：抗生素耐药是细菌因基因变异对药物暴露产生耐受，这些变异是抗生素选择压力下的进化反应；Antibiotic resistance arises when genetic changes let bacteria tolerate drug exposure—an evolutionary response to selective pressure.
- 耐药机制：常见机制包括膜通透性下降、表面转运蛋白突变、主动外排泵清除药物、药物结合的蛋白/RNA轻微结构改变以保持功能但不再结合，以及代谢或化学失活药物；Genetic changes can reduce membrane permeability, alter transporter proteins, upregulate efflux pumps, subtly modify drug-binding proteins/RNAs to preserve function while preventing binding, or metabolize/chemically deactivate drugs.
- 社会代价：美国每年约200万人感染耐药菌，致约2.3万人死亡，延长治疗并增加医疗成本约20-30亿美元；In the US about 2 million resistant infections occur annually, causing ~23,000 deaths and adding an estimated $2–3 billion in healthcare costs.
- 进化过程：细菌群体因复制错误天然存在基因多样性，药物杀死大多数但偶有具优势突变的个体存活，存活者扩增后耐药基因频率升高，再次用药时群体更难清除，展示了耐药性如何在选择压力下迅速富集；Within diverse bacterial populations, bactericidal drugs kill most cells but advantage-conferring mutants survive, expand, and make subsequent treatments less effective, illustrating resistance enrichment under selection.
- α/β 标号：β-内酰胺环中从羰基碳算起第二个碳（β位）连到酰胺氮，形成应力四元环；In a β-lactam the β carbon is the second carbon from the carbonyl and bonds to the amide nitrogen, creating a strained four-member ring.
- β-内酰胺简介：β-内酰胺是最重要也最常用的抗生素类群，含青霉素与阿莫西林等，通过抑制PBP催化的跨肽化反应阻断细胞壁交联；The β-lactams (including penicillin and amoxicillin) block cell-wall crosslinking by inhibiting PBP-catalyzed transpeptidation.
- 结构家族：β-内酰胺环是核心，周围结构决定不同类别如青霉素类、头孢菌素类、碳青霉烯类及其R基差异，结构差异带来作用谱差别；These drugs share the β-lactam ring but differ via surrounding frameworks and R groups across classes like penicillins, cephalosporins, and carbapenems, leading to varying spectra.
- 作用机制：β-内酰胺在性质和形状上模仿细胞壁前体，结合PBP活性位并开环形成基本不可逆的加合物，占据活性位阻止跨肽化；在结构中常见的丝氨酸残基是关键攻击点；The β-lactam ring reacts with PBPs by mimicking cell-wall precursors, opening to form a largely irreversible adduct that blocks the active site and transpeptidation, with a key serine residue in the PBP covalently linked in the product state.

### Economics

## 1. 跨期自控与时间不一致 / Intertemporal Self-Control & Time Inconsistency
- 长期-短期冲突：人们为长期目标制定计划，但短期自我常改选即时满足；Long-run plans often conflict with short-run choices when self-control fails.
- 案例：垃圾食品过量或斯坦福棉花糖实验（等待15分钟可得第二颗）展示延迟奖励的困难；Examples include overeating junk food and the Stanford marshmallow test showing the challenge of delaying gratification.

## 2. 后悔规避 / Regret Avoidance
- 人们会为避免潜在后悔而拒绝看似更优的交易，如拒绝高价转卖彩票；People often reject seemingly better deals to avoid anticipated regret, e.g., refusing to sell a lottery ticket at a higher price.

## 3. 收入分配与同酬假设 / Income Distribution & Pay Equalization Thought Experiment
- 要让所有岗位同酬，需满足“所有人相同、所有岗位同等吸引、所有劳动者完全流动”，现实中难以成立；Equal pay across all jobs would require identical people, equally attractive jobs, and perfect worker mobility—conditions not met in reality.

## 4. 收入差异的主要驱动 / Drivers of Income Differences
- 补偿性差异：不受欢迎岗位支付更高、受欢迎岗位薪酬偏低（屋顶工、伐木 vs 艺术家、音乐人、游戏设计师）；Compensating differentials pay more for undesirable jobs and less for attractive ones (roofing/logging vs artist/musician/game designer).
- 教育与人力资本：高技能/高教育劳动力供给少、替代难→工资更高；Scarce skilled or educated workers are harder to replace, so they earn more.
- 地域与生活方式：高生活成本或低生活质量地区通常支付更高，吸引型地区薪酬可能更低；Wages adjust for cost of living and lifestyle desirability.
- 工会：提升议价力以争取更高薪酬；Unions increase bargaining power and pay.
- 效率工资：企业用更高工资激励、减少流失；Efficiency wages motivate workers and reduce turnover via above-market pay.

## 5. 工资歧视与职业挤占 / Wage Discrimination & Occupational Crowding
- 薪酬差距粗略统计未控制多种因素；剔除可解释因素后仍有约3%-5%差距被视为歧视；Headline gaps (e.g., $0.77 vs $1) omit controls; unexplained 3%–5% after adjustments is labeled discrimination.
- 商业理性：歧视损害利润，并非理性策略；Discrimination reduces profits and is not rational business practice.
- 职业挤占：女性集中岗位往往薪酬低，男性集中岗位薪酬高；Occupational crowding channels women into lower-paying roles and men into higher-paying ones.
- 例子：秘书、托育、幼教、口腔卫生员 vs 技术员、水管工、机修工；Examples include secretaries/childcare/preschool teachers/dental hygienists vs technicians/plumbers/mechanics.
- 收入不平等：自由市场环境下仍会存在收入不均；Income inequality persists even in free-market systems.
