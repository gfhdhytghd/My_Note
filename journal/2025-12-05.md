## Chemistry

### 反应熵变与焓变 / Reaction Entropy & Enthalpy Changes
- 状态函数定义：$\Delta S^\circ=\sum \nu S^\circ_\text{prod}-\sum \nu S^\circ_\text{react}$，$\Delta H^\circ=\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{react})$，均与路径无关；<br>State functions: $\Delta S^\circ=\sum \nu S^\circ_\text{prod}-\sum \nu S^\circ_\text{react}$ and $\Delta H^\circ=\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{react})$, both path-independent.
- 物理意义：$\Delta S>0$ 表示能量/微观状态分布更分散（常见于气体摩尔数增加或升温）；$\Delta H<0$ 为放热，体系在恒压下向环境释放热量；$\Delta H>0$ 吸热需由热源供能；<br>$\Delta S>0$ means more dispersal of energy/microstates (often when gas moles rise or temperature increases); $\Delta H<0$ is exothermic heat release at constant $p$, whereas $\Delta H>0$ is endothermic and needs heat input.
- 自发性关联：$\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$，焓驱动（$\Delta H\ll 0$）可弥补负熵变，熵驱动（$\Delta S\gg 0$）可在高温时推动吸热反应自发；<br>Spontaneity via $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$: enthalpy-driven ($\Delta H\ll 0$) can offset negative $\Delta S$, while entropy-driven ($\Delta S\gg 0$) can render endothermic reactions spontaneous at higher $T$.
- 测量与估算：$\Delta H$ 常由量热实验或赫斯定律叠加求得；$\Delta S$ 可用标准摩尔熵求和或由可逆过程热量 $\Delta S=\int q_\text{rev}/T$ 推得；<br>Standard $\Delta H$ from calorimetry or Hess’s law; $\Delta S$ from tabulated $S^\circ$ sums or $\Delta S=\int q_\text{rev}/T$ for reversible steps.
- 相变与温度效应：相变中 $\Delta S$ 与熔/汽化潜热相关（$\Delta S_\text{phase}=\Delta H_\text{phase}/T_\text{trans}$），高温下 $T\Delta S$ 权重变大，决定 $\Delta G$ 符号；<br>During phase changes $\Delta S$ ties to latent heat via $\Delta S_\text{phase}=\Delta H_\text{phase}/T_\text{trans}$, and rising $T$ amplifies $T\Delta S$ to set the sign of $\Delta G$.
