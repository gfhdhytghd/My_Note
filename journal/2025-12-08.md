#### 比热容与q_{sys}/q_{surr}关系 / Specific Heat Capacity & q_{sys}/q_{surr}
- 比热容定义：单位质量升温1 K所需热量 $c$，单位 J g$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$；摩尔热容 $C_m$ 以 J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$；<br>Specific heat capacity $c$ is heat to raise 1 g by 1 K (J g$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$); molar heat capacity $C_m$ uses J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$.
- 计算公式：样品吸/放热 $q=c m \Delta T$ 或 $q=n C_m \Delta T$，其中 $\Delta T=T_\text{final}-T_\text{initial}$ 决定符号；<br>Compute heat via $q=c m \Delta T$ or $q=n C_m \Delta T$, where $\Delta T=T_\text{final}-T_\text{initial}$ sets the sign.
- 符号规定：定义体系为正方向，放热 $q_\text{sys}<0$、吸热 $q_\text{sys}>0$，而环境/量热计 $q_\text{surr}=-q_\text{sys}$；<br>Sign convention: system-positive; exothermic gives $q_\text{sys}<0$, endothermic $q_\text{sys}>0$, with surroundings/calorimeter $q_\text{surr}=-q_\text{sys}$.
- 能量守恒：孤立量热中 $q_\text{sys}+q_\text{surr}=0$，常用环境温变求体系热效应；<br>Energy conservation: in isolated calorimetry $q_\text{sys}+q_\text{surr}=0$, so surroundings' temperature change reveals system heat.
- 恒压/恒容：恒压量热得 $q_p=\Delta H$，恒容弹式量热计得 $q_v=\Delta U$，均满足 $q_\text{surr}=-q_\text{sys}$；<br>Constant-pressure calorimetry yields $q_p=\Delta H$; bomb calorimetry gives $q_v=\Delta U$; both obey $q_\text{surr}=-q_\text{sys}$.
