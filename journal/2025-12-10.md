## Chemistry

### 反应焓 ΔH_{\mathrm{rxn}} 与生成焓 ΔH_f / Reaction Enthalpy & Enthalpy of Formation
- 定义与测量：恒压条件下反应焓变$\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}=q_p$，放热$\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}<0$、吸热$\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}>0$，可用咖啡杯量热计直接测 $q_p$；<br>Definition and measurement: at constant pressure $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}=q_p$, exothermic when $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}<0$ and endothermic when $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}>0$, obtainable from coffee-cup calorimetry as $q_p$.
- 标准生成焓：$\Delta H_f^\circ$ 定义为元素标准态生成 1 mol 化合物的焓变，元素标准态 $\Delta H_f^\circ=0$；<br>Standard enthalpy of formation $\Delta H_f^\circ$ is the enthalpy change to form 1 mol of a compound from elements in their standard states, where elements have $\Delta H_f^\circ=0$.
- 由生成焓求反应焓：$\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}^\circ=\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{react})$，$\nu$ 为化学计量系数（生成物取正、反应物取负）；<br>Compute reaction enthalpy via $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}^\circ=\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum \nu \Delta H_f^\circ(\text{react})$, using stoichiometric coefficients with products positive and reactants negative.
- 方向与倍数关系：反应式整体乘 $n$ 时 $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}$ 乘 $n$；反应逆转时符号翻转，体现焓为状态函数；<br>Scaling and direction: multiply the balanced reaction by $n$ then $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}$ scales by $n$; reversing the reaction flips the sign, reflecting enthalpy as a state function.
- 赫斯定律：若缺少直接的 $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}$，可用多步已知 $\Delta H_f^\circ$ 或已知反应焓叠加求得，总焓变为各子反应之和；<br>Hess’s law: when direct $\Delta H_\mathrm{rxn}$ is unavailable, sum tabulated $\Delta H_f^\circ$ or known stepwise enthalpies—the total enthalpy change equals the sum of subreactions.
