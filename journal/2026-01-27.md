## Math / 数学

### Integration by Parts (from Product Rule) / 分部积分（由乘法求导推出）

**Idea / 思路：** Start from the product rule $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$ and rewrite it in differential form $d(uv)=u\,dv+v\,du$. Integrate both sides to get the integration-by-parts formula. / 从乘法求导 $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$ 出发，写成微分形式 $d(uv)=u\,dv+v\,du$，两边积分即可得到分部积分公式。

$$
\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du
$$

**How to use / 使用步骤：**
- Choose $u$ and $dv$ from the integrand $\int f(x)g(x)\,dx$ / 在 $\int f(x)g(x)\,dx$ 中选 $u$ 与 $dv$
- Compute $du$ and $v$ / 计算 $du$ 与 $v$
- Substitute into $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$ and simplify / 代入公式并化简
- (Heuristic / 经验法则) Pick $u$ so it becomes simpler after differentiating (often $\ln x$, algebraic terms, inverse trig) / 一般选导数会“变简单”的部分做 $u$（常见：$\ln x$、多项式、反三角）

**Example 1 / 例1：** $\int x e^x\,dx$
- Let $u=x,\;dv=e^x\,dx$ so $du=dx,\;v=e^x$ / 取 $u=x,\;dv=e^x\,dx$，则 $du=dx,\;v=e^x$
$$
\int x e^x\,dx=xe^x-\int e^x\,dx=xe^x-e^x+C=e^x(x-1)+C
$$

**Example 2 / 例2：** $\int \ln x\,dx$
- Let $u=\ln x,\;dv=dx$ so $du=\frac1x\,dx,\;v=x$ / 取 $u=\ln x,\;dv=dx$，则 $du=\frac1x\,dx,\;v=x$
$$
\int \ln x\,dx=x\ln x-\int 1\,dx=x\ln x-x+C
$$
