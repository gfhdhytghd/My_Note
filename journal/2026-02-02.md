# Math Calculus

## 三角代换 / Trigonometric Substitution

### 什么时候用 / When to use

- 当被积式里出现$\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$、$\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$、$\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$这类根式时，用三角恒等式把根号“消掉”。  
  When an integrand contains radicals like($\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$), ($\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$), or ($\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$), use trig identities to eliminate the square root.

### 三种标准代换 / Three standard substitutions

- $\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$：令$x=a\sin\theta$（或$x=a\cos\theta$）。  
  For($\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$): set($x=a\sin\theta$) (or($x=a\cos\theta$)).
  - $dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$，且$\sqrt{a^2-x^2}=a\sqrt{1-\sin^2\theta}=a\cos\theta$。  
    Then($dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$) and($\sqrt{a^2-x^2}=a\cos\theta$).

- $\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$：令$x=a\tan\theta$。  
  For($\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$): set($x=a\tan\theta$).
  - $dx=a\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$，且$\sqrt{a^2+x^2}=a\sqrt{1+\tan^2\theta}=a\sec\theta$。  
    Then($dx=a\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$) and($\sqrt{a^2+x^2}=a\sec\theta$).

- $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$：令$x=a\sec\theta$（也常见$x=a\cosh u$，但那是双曲代换）。  
  For($\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$): set($x=a\sec\theta$) (hyperbolic substitution($x=a\cosh u$) is another option).
  - $dx=a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta$，且$\sqrt{x^2-a^2}=a\sqrt{\sec^2\theta-1}=a\tan\theta$。  
    Then($dx=a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta$) and($\sqrt{x^2-a^2}=a\tan\theta$).

### 回代技巧 / Back-substitution tips

- 回代时用直角三角形：把$\sin\theta,\cos\theta,\tan\theta$用$x$与$a$表示。  
  Use a right triangle to express($\sin\theta,\cos\theta,\tan\theta$) back in terms of($x$) and($a$).
  - 若$x=a\sin\theta$，则$\sin\theta=x/a$，$\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}/a$，$\tan\theta=x/\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$。  
    If($x=a\sin\theta$), then($\sin\theta=x/a$), ($\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}/a$), and($\tan\theta=x/\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$).
  - 若$x=a\tan\theta$，则$\tan\theta=x/a$，$\sec\theta=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}/a$，$\sin\theta=x/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$，$\cos\theta=a/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$。  
    If($x=a\tan\theta$), then($\tan\theta=x/a$), ($\sec\theta=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}/a$), ($\sin\theta=x/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$), and($\cos\theta=a/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$).
  - 若$x=a\sec\theta$，则$\sec\theta=x/a$，$\tan\theta=\sqrt{x^2-a^2}/a$，$\sin\theta=\sqrt{x^2-a^2}/x$，$\cos\theta=a/x$。  
    If($x=a\sec\theta$), then($\sec\theta=x/a$), ($\tan\theta=\sqrt{x^2-a^2}/a$), ($\sin\theta=\sqrt{x^2-a^2}/x$), and($\cos\theta=a/x$).

- 例：$\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}$，令$x=a\sin\theta$：  
  Example: for$\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}$, let($x=a\sin\theta$):

## 线性组合与组分 / Linear Combination & Components

card![[Math_Linear-Algebra/linear_combination_span_components]]

- 速记 / Quick notes：线性组合=$a_1v_1+\cdots+a_kv_k$；张成是所有线性组合的集合；“组分”常指(1)基下坐标(2)沿方向投影分量。  
  Quick notes: linear combination $a_1v_1+\cdots+a_kv_k$; span is the set of all such combinations; “components” often means (1) coordinates in a basis (2) projection along a direction.
- 关联 / Related：用$A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_k]$把“线性组合”写成$Ax$，从而把求系数问题转成线性方程组。  
  Related: with $A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_k]$, linear combinations become $Ax$, turning coefficient-finding into a linear system.
- 格式 / Notation：把$v_1,\dots,v_k$拼成矩阵$A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_k]$，把系数拼成列向量$c$，则$Ac=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$（矩阵-向量乘积=线性组合）。  
  Notation: stack $v_1,\dots,v_k$ as columns of $A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_k]$ and stack coefficients into a column vector $c$, then $Ac=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$ (matrix–vector product equals a linear combination).

## 比尔-朗伯定律 / Beer–Lambert Law

- 核心结论 / Core idea：溶液对某一波长光的吸光度与浓度和光程成正比（在一定条件下线性）。  
  Absorbance at a given wavelength is proportional to concentration and path length (linear under certain conditions).

### 公式与符号 / Equation & symbols

- $A=\varepsilon\ell c$。  
  $A=\varepsilon\ell c$.
  - $A$：吸光度（无量纲）/ absorbance (unitless).  
  - $\varepsilon$：摩尔吸光系数 / molar absorptivity (typical units $L/(mol\cdot cm)$).  
  - $\ell$：光程（通常比色皿$1cm$）/ path length (often $1cm$ cuvette).  
  - $c$：浓度（常用$mol/L$）/ concentration (often $mol/L$).

- 与透过率关系 / Relation to transmittance：$A=\log_{10}(I_0/I)=-\log_{10}(T)$，其中$T=I/I_0$。  
  Relation: $A=\log_{10}(I_0/I)=-\log_{10}(T)$ with $T=I/I_0$.

### 实验怎么用 / How it’s used in lab

- 做标准曲线 / Calibration curve：配一系列已知$c$的标准溶液，测$A$并作图$A$ vs.$c$，斜率=$\varepsilon\ell$（固定波长与比色皿）。  
  Prepare standards with known $c$, measure $A$, plot $A$ vs. $c$; slope equals $\varepsilon\ell$ (fixed wavelength and cuvette).
- 求未知浓度 / Unknown concentration：测未知样品$A$后用直线方程反推$c$。  
  Measure $A$ for the unknown and back-calculate $c$ from the line.

### 适用条件与偏离 / When it works & deviations

- 条件 / Assumptions：单色光、溶液足够稀、无明显散射/浑浊、化学形态不随浓度变化（不聚集/不解离导致比例改变）。  
  Assumptions: monochromatic light, sufficiently dilute solution, minimal scattering/turbidity, and no concentration-dependent speciation (no aggregation/dissociation changing proportionality).
- 偏离 / Deviations：高浓度、杂散光、仪器非线性、颗粒散射会让$A$-$c$不再严格线性。  
  Deviations: high concentration, stray light, instrument nonlinearity, and scattering can break linearity between $A$ and $c$.
