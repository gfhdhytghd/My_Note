What does the determinant really tell us:

det tells how much the "size" changed during the transformation of std matrix(需要解释下size)

sign of det teled the orintation changes

| Transformation | Determinant Magnitude | Determinant Sign |
|---|---|---|
| Scaling | Changes by the scaling factor (e.g., \(|k|^n\) in \(n\) dimensions) | May change (flips if the scale factor is negative) |
| Rotation | Unchanged (magnitude = 1) | Unchanged |
| Reflection | Unchanged (magnitude = 1) | Flips sign |
| Shearing | Unchanged (magnitude = 1) | Unchanged |
| Projection | Becomes 0 | Not applicable (det = 0) |.
