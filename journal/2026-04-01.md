## Math 220 / 矩阵对角化 / Matrix Diagonalization

这部分建立在[[journal/2026-03-27]]里关于特征值与特征向量的内容之上：对角化本质上是“换到特征向量组成的坐标系里看矩阵”。  
This section builds on [[journal/2026-03-27]] on eigenvalues and eigenvectors: diagonalization is essentially viewing a matrix in the coordinate system formed by eigenvectors.

### 定义与判定 / Definition and Criterion
如果矩阵$A$可以写成$A=PDP^{-1}$，其中$D$是对角矩阵，$P$的列向量是$A$的特征向量，那么就说$A$可对角化。  
If a matrix $A$ can be written as $A=PDP^{-1}$, where $D$ is diagonal and the columns of $P$ are eigenvectors of $A$, then $A$ is diagonalizable.

对角矩阵$D$把原来“难算的线性变换”变成了沿坐标轴分别缩放，所以它比原矩阵更容易理解与计算。  
The diagonal matrix $D$ turns a more complicated linear transformation into separate scalings along coordinate axes, so it is much easier to interpret and compute with.

一个$n\times n$矩阵可对角化，当且仅当它有$n$个线性无关的特征向量。特征值重复并不自动意味着失败，关键在于能不能凑够足够多的独立特征向量。  
An $n\times n$ matrix is diagonalizable if and only if it has $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors. Repeated eigenvalues do not automatically cause failure; the real question is whether there are enough independent eigenvectors.

### 操作步骤 / Procedure
1. 先解$\det(A-\lambda I)=0$找到特征值。 / First solve $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$ to find the eigenvalues.
2. 对每个特征值解$(A-\lambda I)\vec{v}=0$找到特征向量。 / For each eigenvalue, solve $(A-\lambda I)\vec{v}=0$ to find the eigenvectors.
3. 选出$n$个线性无关的特征向量作为$P$的列。 / Choose $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors as the columns of $P$.
4. 按相同顺序把对应特征值放到$D$的对角线上。 / Put the corresponding eigenvalues on the diagonal of $D$ in the same order.
5. 若找不到$n$个线性无关特征向量，就不能对角化。 / If you cannot find $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors, then the matrix is not diagonalizable.

### 一个$2\times2$例子 / A $2\times2$ Example
考虑
Consider
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
5&2\\
2&5
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

特征方程为
The characteristic equation is
$$
\det(A-\lambda I)=
\begin{vmatrix}
5-\lambda&2\\
2&5-\lambda
\end{vmatrix}
=(5-\lambda)^2-4
=(\lambda-7)(\lambda-3).
$$
所以特征值是$\lambda=7$与$\lambda=3$。  
So the eigenvalues are $\lambda=7$ and $\lambda=3$.

当$\lambda=7$时，$(A-7I)\vec{v}=0$给出$x=y$，所以可取特征向量$\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$；当$\lambda=3$时，$(A-3I)\vec{v}=0$给出$x=-y$，所以可取特征向量$\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}$。  
When $\lambda=7$, the equation $(A-7I)\vec{v}=0$ gives $x=y$, so we may take the eigenvector $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$; when $\lambda=3$, the equation $(A-3I)\vec{v}=0$ gives $x=-y$, so we may take $\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}$.

于是可以取
So we may choose
$$
P=\begin{bmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
D=\begin{bmatrix}
7&0\\
0&3
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
P^{-1}=\frac12\begin{bmatrix}
1&1\\
1&-1
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
因此
Therefore,
$$
A=PDP^{-1}.
$$

这里的意思是：在标准坐标下看，$A$会把向量“混合”起来；但在由特征向量组成的新基底下，它只是在第一条特征方向上乘$7$，在第二条特征方向上乘$3$。  
This means that in the standard coordinates, $A$ mixes the components of a vector; but in the new basis formed by eigenvectors, it simply multiplies one eigendirection by $7$ and the other by $3$.

### 对角化为什么能帮助乘方 / Why Diagonalization Helps with Powers
一旦$A=PDP^{-1}$，那么
Once $A=PDP^{-1}$, we get
$$
A^n=(PDP^{-1})^n=PD^nP^{-1},
$$
因为中间的$P^{-1}P$会不断相消。  
because the middle factors $P^{-1}P$ keep canceling.

而$D^n$非常容易算：如果
And $D^n$ is very easy to compute: if
$$
D=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\dots,\lambda_n),
$$
那么
then
$$
D^n=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1^n,\lambda_2^n,\dots,\lambda_n^n).
$$

对前面的$2\times2$矩阵来说，
For the earlier $2\times2$ matrix,
$$
A^n
=
P\begin{bmatrix}
7^n&0\\
0&3^n
\end{bmatrix}P^{-1}
=
\frac12\begin{bmatrix}
7^n+3^n&7^n-3^n\\
7^n-3^n&7^n+3^n
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
这比把$\begin{bmatrix}5&2\\2&5\end{bmatrix}$连续乘$n$次要轻松得多。  
This is much easier than multiplying $\begin{bmatrix}5&2\\2&5\end{bmatrix}$ by itself $n$ times.

对前面的$3\times3$矩阵来说，
For the earlier $3\times3$ matrix,
$$
B^n
=
P\begin{bmatrix}
3^n&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&3^n
\end{bmatrix}P^{-1}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{3^n+1}{2}&\frac{3^n-1}{2}&0\\
\frac{3^n-1}{2}&\frac{3^n+1}{2}&0\\
0&0&3^n
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
也就是说，对角化把“矩阵连乘”转成了“标量乘方”。  
In other words, diagonalization turns “repeated matrix multiplication” into “raising scalars to powers.”

### 小结 / Summary
对角化的核心不是把式子写成$PDP^{-1}$本身，而是找到一个更适合这个线性变换的基底。在这个基底里，矩阵作用被拆成了几个互不干扰的缩放方向。  
The key of diagonalization is not merely rewriting a matrix as $PDP^{-1}$, but finding a basis better suited to the linear transformation. In that basis, the action of the matrix splits into several independent scaling directions.

这也是为什么它在线性递推、差分方程、马尔可夫链、微分方程与长期行为分析里都很有用，因为一旦进入对角形式，很多高次幂和长期趋势都会立刻变清楚。  
That is why diagonalization is useful in linear recurrences, difference equations, Markov chains, differential equations, and long-term behavior analysis: once the matrix is diagonal, many high powers and asymptotic trends become much clearer.
