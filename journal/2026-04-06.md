## Math 220 / 幂迭代与主特征向量 / Power Iteration and the Dominant Eigenvector

这部分可以看成[[journal/2026-04-01]]里对角化观点的一个直接应用：当我们不断计算$A\vec{x}$、$A^2\vec{x}$、$A^3\vec{x}$时，长期方向往往会被“绝对值最大的特征值”对应的特征向量控制。  
This can be viewed as a direct application of the diagonalization viewpoint in [[journal/2026-04-01]]: when we keep computing $A\vec{x}$, $A^2\vec{x}$, $A^3\vec{x}$, the long-term direction is usually controlled by the eigenvector associated with the eigenvalue of largest magnitude.

### 基本迭代 / Basic Iteration
给定非零初始向量$\vec{x}_0$，power iteration定义为
Given a nonzero initial vector $\vec{x}_0$, power iteration is defined by
$$
\vec{x}_{k+1}=\frac{A\vec{x}_k}{\|A\vec{x}_k\|}.
$$
每一步都先乘上$A$，再把长度归一化，所以我们主要观察的是方向如何变化，而不是长度本身。  
At each step we first multiply by $A$ and then normalize the length, so the main thing we track is how the direction changes, not the size itself.

### 为什么会趋向特征向量 / Why It Tends to an Eigenvector
先假设$A$可对角化，并且有一组特征向量基底$\vec{v}_1,\dots,\vec{v}_n$，对应特征值满足
First assume that $A$ is diagonalizable and has an eigenvector basis $\vec{v}_1,\dots,\vec{v}_n$, with eigenvalues satisfying
$$
|\lambda_1|>|\lambda_2|\geq\cdots\geq|\lambda_n|.
$$
如果初始向量写成
If the initial vector can be written as
$$
\vec{x}_0=c_1\vec{v}_1+c_2\vec{v}_2+\cdots+c_n\vec{v}_n,
\qquad
c_1\neq0,
$$
那么
then
$$
A^k\vec{x}_0
=
c_1\lambda_1^k\vec{v}_1+c_2\lambda_2^k\vec{v}_2+\cdots+c_n\lambda_n^k\vec{v}_n
=
\lambda_1^k\left(
c_1\vec{v}_1
+c_2\left(\frac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_1}\right)^k\vec{v}_2
+\cdots
+c_n\left(\frac{\lambda_n}{\lambda_1}\right)^k\vec{v}_n
\right).
$$
因为对$i\geq2$都有$\left|\frac{\lambda_i}{\lambda_1}\right|<1$，所以当$k$越来越大时，后面的项都会相对变小，留下来的主导方向就是$\vec{v}_1$。归一化以后，$\vec{x}_k$就会越来越接近$\vec{v}_1$或$-\vec{v}_1$。  
Because $\left|\frac{\lambda_i}{\lambda_1}\right|<1$ for every $i\geq2$, the later terms become relatively smaller as $k$ grows, so the dominant remaining direction is $\vec{v}_1$. After normalization, $\vec{x}_k$ gets closer and closer to $\vec{v}_1$ or $-\vec{v}_1$.

### 直观理解 / Intuition
从[[journal/2026-03-27]]的语言看，矩阵会沿不同特征方向分别做“乘$\lambda$”的动作；反复作用很多次以后，增长最快的那条方向就会压过其他方向。  
Using the language of [[journal/2026-03-27]], the matrix acts as “multiply by $\lambda$” along each eigendirection; after many repeated applications, the fastest-growing direction overwhelms the others.

所以power iteration并不是“凭空猜到”特征向量，而是通过不断放大主特征方向、不断压低其他方向，最后把向量推到主特征向量附近。  
So power iteration does not “guess” an eigenvector out of nowhere; it keeps amplifying the dominant eigendirection while suppressing the others, and this pushes the vector toward the dominant eigenvector.

### 一个二维例子 / A Two-Dimensional Example
沿用[[journal/2026-04-01]]里的矩阵
Using the matrix from [[journal/2026-04-01]],
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
5&2\\
2&5
\end{bmatrix},
$$
它的特征值是$7$和$3$，对应特征向量可以取
its eigenvalues are $7$ and $3$, with eigenvectors
$$
\vec{v}_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\vec{v}_2=\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

如果从$\vec{x}_0=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}$开始，那么
If we start from $\vec{x}_0=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}$, then
$$
\vec{x}_0
=
\frac12\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}
+\frac12\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
=
\frac12\vec{v}_1+\frac12\vec{v}_2.
$$
于是
Therefore,
$$
A^k\vec{x}_0
=
\frac12 7^k\vec{v}_1+\frac12 3^k\vec{v}_2
=
\frac12\begin{bmatrix}
7^k+3^k\\
7^k-3^k
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
这里第二个坐标与第一个坐标的比值是
Here the ratio of the second coordinate to the first is
$$
\frac{7^k-3^k}{7^k+3^k}\to1.
$$
所以向量方向越来越接近直线$y=x$，也就是越来越接近特征向量$\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$的方向。  
So the vector direction gets closer and closer to the line $y=x$, which means it approaches the direction of the eigenvector $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$.

例如
For example,
$$
A\vec{x}_0=\begin{bmatrix}5\\2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A^2\vec{x}_0=\begin{bmatrix}29\\20\end{bmatrix},\qquad
A^3\vec{x}_0=\begin{bmatrix}185\\158\end{bmatrix}.
$$
这些向量一开始并不是特征向量，但它们的方向明显越来越靠近$\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$。  
These vectors are not eigenvectors at first, but their directions clearly move closer and closer to $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$.

### 需要注意的情况 / Important Caveats
如果初始向量刚好满足$c_1=0$，也就是它完全没有主特征方向的分量，那么迭代就看不到$\vec{v}_1$，而会被别的方向主导。  
If the initial vector happens to satisfy $c_1=0$, meaning it has no component in the dominant eigendirection, then the iteration never sees $\vec{v}_1$ and will be driven by other directions instead.

如果最大的两个特征值满足$|\lambda_1|=|\lambda_2|$，那么就没有唯一的“最快增长方向”，这时power iteration可能不收敛到单一特征向量。  
If the two largest eigenvalues satisfy $|\lambda_1|=|\lambda_2|$, then there is no unique “fastest-growing direction,” and power iteration may fail to converge to a single eigenvector.

如果主特征值是负的，那么归一化后的向量可能在$\vec{v}_1$与$-\vec{v}_1$之间来回翻转；严格地说，此时收敛的是特征方向，而不一定是带符号的单一向量。  
If the dominant eigenvalue is negative, then the normalized vectors may flip back and forth between $\vec{v}_1$ and $-\vec{v}_1$; strictly speaking, the eigendirection converges even if a single signed vector does not.

### 小结 / Summary
power iteration之所以有效，本质上是因为$A^k\vec{x}_0$里的各个特征方向会按$\lambda_i^k$的速度增长或衰减，而绝对值最大的特征值最终占优势。  
Power iteration works because the eigendirections inside $A^k\vec{x}_0$ grow or decay at rates determined by $\lambda_i^k$, and the eigenvalue of largest magnitude eventually dominates.

所以只要主特征值在绝对值上严格最大，并且初始向量在对应特征方向上有非零分量，反复乘矩阵并归一化就会把向量推向那个主特征向量的方向。  
So as long as the dominant eigenvalue is strictly largest in magnitude and the initial vector has a nonzero component in that eigendirection, repeated multiplication and normalization push the vector toward that dominant eigenvector direction.
