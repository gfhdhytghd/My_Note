## 正交与投影 / Orthogonality & Projections

这一整块是线性代数里非常核心的一章，也是考试高频内容。/ This is a core chapter in linear algebra and a common exam topic.

### 1. 向量长度与点积 / Vector Length and Dot Product

- 向量长度 / Vector norm:

$$
\|\vec{v}\|=\sqrt{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{v}}
$$

- 点积 / Dot product:

$$
\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}=\sum_i v_iw_i
$$

- 几何意义 / Geometric meaning:

$$
\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}=\|\vec{v}\|\|\vec{w}\|\cos\theta
$$

- 用点积判断夹角 / Use the dot product to determine angles:
  - $\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}=0$：垂直 / perpendicular
  - $\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}>0$：锐角 / acute angle
  - $\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}<0$：钝角 / obtuse angle

### 2. 正交与正交归一 / Orthogonal and Orthonormal

- 正交 / Orthogonal:

$$
\vec{v}_i\cdot\vec{v}_j=0\quad(i\ne j)
$$

这表示向量两两垂直。/ This means the vectors are pairwise perpendicular.

- 正交归一 / Orthonormal:
  - 正交 / orthogonal
  - 每个向量长度为 $1$ / each vector has norm $1$

$$
\|\vec{v}_i\|=1
$$

- 重要结论 / Key facts:
  - 正交向量组一定线性无关。/ Any orthogonal set of nonzero vectors is linearly independent.
  - Orthonormal basis 是最方便的基底。/ An orthonormal basis is the most convenient basis to compute with.

### 3. 正交矩阵 / Orthogonal Matrix

- 定义 / Definition: 矩阵 $Q$ 是正交矩阵，当且仅当它是方阵且列向量构成 orthonormal set. / A matrix $Q$ is orthogonal iff it is square and its columns form an orthonormal set.

- 核心性质 / Core properties:

1. 最重要性质 / Fundamental identity:

$$
Q^TQ=I
$$

2. 逆矩阵 / Inverse:

$$
Q^{-1}=Q^T
$$

3. 长度不变 / Length is preserved:

$$
\|Q\vec{v}\|=\|\vec{v}\|
$$

4. 角度与点积不变 / Angles and dot products are preserved:

$$
(Q\vec{v})\cdot(Q\vec{w})=\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}
$$

- 几何意义 / Geometric meaning: 正交矩阵表示刚体变换，如旋转与反射，但不包含拉伸。/ Orthogonal matrices represent rigid motions such as rotations and reflections, but not scaling.

### 4. 正交归一基 / Orthonormal Basis

- 定义 / Definition: 一组向量既 orthonormal，又能张成整个空间。/ A set of vectors is an orthonormal basis if it is orthonormal and spans the whole space.

- 超级重要性质 / Crucial expansion formula: 若 $\{\vec{u}_1,\vec{u}_2,\dots,\vec{u}_n\}$ 是 orthonormal basis，则任意向量 $\vec{v}$ 都可以写成

$$
\vec{v}=\sum_i(\vec{v}\cdot\vec{u}_i)\vec{u}_i
$$

- 重点 / Main takeaway: 展开系数直接由点积给出，不需要再解方程组。/ The coordinates are obtained directly by dot products, so no linear system is needed.

### 5. 向量投影 / Projection onto a Vector

- 投影到向量 $\vec{w}$ 上 / Project $\vec{v}$ onto $\vec{w}$:

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{w}}\vec{v}=\frac{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}}{\vec{w}\cdot\vec{w}}\vec{w}
$$

- 若 $\vec{w}$ 是单位向量 / If $\vec{w}$ is a unit vector:

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{w}}\vec{v}=(\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w})\vec{w}
$$

- 几何意义 / Geometric meaning: 把 $\vec{v}$ 压到 $\vec{w}$ 的方向上。/ It is the component of $\vec{v}$ in the direction of $\vec{w}$.

### 6. 正交分解 / Orthogonal Decomposition

- 定理 / Theorem: 任意向量都可以分解为

$$
\vec{v}=\vec{v}_{\parallel W}+\vec{v}_{\perp W}
$$

- 含义 / Meaning:
  - $\vec{v}_{\parallel W}$ 在子空间 $W$ 内，即投影部分。/ $\vec{v}_{\parallel W}$ lies in $W$, the projected part.
  - $\vec{v}_{\perp W}$ 与子空间 $W$ 垂直。/ $\vec{v}_{\perp W}$ is perpendicular to $W$.

- 关键性质 / Key property:

$$
\vec{v}_{\parallel W}\cdot\vec{v}_{\perp W}=0
$$

- 误差向量 / Error vector:

$$
\vec{e}=\vec{v}-\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}
$$

这个误差对应从 $\vec{v}$ 到子空间 $W$ 的最短距离。/ This error gives the shortest distance from $\vec{v}$ to the subspace $W$.

### 7. 投影到子空间 / Projection onto a Subspace

- 若子空间 $W$ 有 orthonormal basis $\{\vec{u}_1,\dots,\vec{u}_k\}$，则 / If $W$ has orthonormal basis $\{\vec{u}_1,\dots,\vec{u}_k\}$, then

$$
\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}=\sum_{i=1}^k(\vec{v}\cdot\vec{u}_i)\vec{u}_i
$$

- 这和正交归一基展开公式本质相同。/ This is the same idea as expansion in an orthonormal basis.

### 8. 矩阵形式 / Matrix Form

- 若矩阵 $Q$ 的列向量构成 orthonormal basis，则 / If the columns of $Q$ are orthonormal, then

$$
P=QQ^T
$$

是投影到对应列空间的投影矩阵。/ This is the projection matrix onto the column space of $Q$.

- 投影结果 / Projection result:

$$
\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}=QQ^T\vec{v}
$$

### 9. 物理直觉 / Physical Intuition

- 斜坡上的力可以分解为平行斜面与垂直斜面的两个分量。/ A force on an inclined plane can be split into components parallel and perpendicular to the slope.
- 平行分量推动物体滑动，垂直分量对应支持力。/ The parallel component drives motion; the perpendicular component is balanced by the normal force.
- 本质上，向量分解就是投影。/ At its core, vector decomposition is projection.

### 10. 常见题型 / Common Problem Types

1. 判断是否正交 / Check orthogonality: 验证点积是否为 $0$. / Compute the dot product and see whether it is $0$.
2. 单位化向量 / Normalize a vector:

$$
\vec{u}=\frac{\vec{v}}{\|\vec{v}\|}
$$

3. 计算投影 / Compute a projection:

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{w}}\vec{v}=\frac{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}}{\vec{w}\cdot\vec{w}}\vec{w}
$$

4. 做正交分解 / Decompose into projection plus error:

$$
\vec{v}=\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}+\vec{e}
$$

5. 判断正交矩阵 / Check whether a matrix is orthogonal:

$$
Q^TQ=I
$$

6. 用正交归一基快速展开 / Expand quickly in an orthonormal basis: 系数就是点积。/ The coefficients are just dot products.
