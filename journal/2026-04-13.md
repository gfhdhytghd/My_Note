## 正交投影与最小二乘 / Orthogonal Projections and Least Squares

这一节的核心是：投影是最佳逼近，误差向量一定与目标子空间正交。/ The core idea of this lesson is that projection gives the best approximation, and the error vector is always orthogonal to the target subspace.

相关前置内容可回看 [[journal/2026-04-11]]。/ For prerequisite ideas, review [[journal/2026-04-11]].

### 1. 投影到一条直线 / Projection onto a Line

设 $W=\operatorname{span}(\vec{w})$，其中 $\vec{w}\ne\vec{0}$，而 $\vec{v}$ 是任意向量。/ Let $W=\operatorname{span}(\vec{w})$, where $\vec{w}\ne\vec{0}$, and let $\vec{v}$ be any vector.

投影公式是 / The projection formula is

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{w}}\vec{v}
=
\frac{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}}{\vec{w}\cdot\vec{w}}\vec{w}.
$$

几何上，这是把 $\vec{v}$ 压到 $\vec{w}$ 的方向上，得到直线 $W$ 上离 $\vec{v}$ 最近的点。/ Geometrically, this pushes $\vec{v}$ onto the direction of $\vec{w}$ and gives the point on the line $W$ closest to $\vec{v}$.

### 2. 正交分解与误差 / Orthogonal Decomposition and Error

把 $\vec{v}$ 分解成投影部分和误差部分：/ Decompose $\vec{v}$ into its projected part and error part:

$$
\vec{v}=\widehat{\vec{v}}+\vec{e}.
$$

其中 / where

$$
\widehat{\vec{v}}=\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v},
\qquad
\vec{e}=\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}.
$$

这里 $\widehat{\vec{v}}$ 平行于 $W$，而 $\vec{e}$ 垂直于 $W$。/ Here $\widehat{\vec{v}}$ is parallel to $W$, while $\vec{e}$ is perpendicular to $W$.

关键性质是 / The key property is

$$
\vec{e}\cdot\vec{w}=0.
$$

也就是说，误差不是随便剩下的部分，而是从 $\vec{v}$ 到 $W$ 的垂直距离方向。/ In other words, the error is not arbitrary; it points in the perpendicular direction from $\vec{v}$ to $W$.

### 3. 投影到子空间 / Projection onto a Subspace

设 $W\subset\mathbb{R}^n$，并且 $W$ 有一组正交基 $\{\vec{w}_1,\vec{w}_2,\dots,\vec{w}_k\}$。/ Let $W\subset\mathbb{R}^n$, and suppose $W$ has an orthogonal basis $\{\vec{w}_1,\vec{w}_2,\dots,\vec{w}_k\}$.

投影到子空间的公式是 / The projection formula onto the subspace is

$$
\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}
=
\sum_{i=1}^k
\frac{\vec{v}\cdot\vec{w}_i}{\vec{w}_i\cdot\vec{w}_i}\vec{w}_i.
$$

直观上，就是把 $\vec{v}$ 分别投影到每个正交方向上，再把这些分量加起来。/ Intuitively, project $\vec{v}$ onto each orthogonal direction separately, then add those components together.

这个公式的关键条件是：基底必须正交。/ The crucial condition is that the basis must be orthogonal.

如果基底不是正交的，不能直接套用这个分项投影公式，否则会重复计算重叠部分。/ If the basis is not orthogonal, this component-by-component formula cannot be used directly, because overlapping parts would be counted incorrectly.

### 4. 为什么投影是最佳近似 / Why Projection Is the Best Approximation

定义 / Define

$$
\widehat{\vec{v}}=\operatorname{proj}_W\vec{v}.
$$

那么 $\widehat{\vec{v}}$ 满足 / Then $\widehat{\vec{v}}$ satisfies

$$
\|\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}\|
=
\min_{\vec{w}\in W}\|\vec{v}-\vec{w}\|.
$$

这表示投影就是子空间 $W$ 中最接近 $\vec{v}$ 的向量。/ This means the projection is the vector in $W$ closest to $\vec{v}$.

几何原因是 $\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}$ 垂直于整个子空间 $W$，所以它给出从点到子空间的最短路径。/ Geometrically, $\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}$ is perpendicular to the whole subspace $W$, so it gives the shortest path from the point to the subspace.

### 5. 基不是正交时的方法 / What to Do When the Basis Is Not Orthogonal

如果给出的基底不是正交基，常用两种方法：先做 Gram-Schmidt，或者直接用最小二乘。/ If the given basis is not orthogonal, there are two common methods: first apply Gram-Schmidt, or directly use least squares.

#### 方法 1：Gram-Schmidt / Method 1: Gram-Schmidt

Gram-Schmidt 的目标是把原来的基底变成正交基，同时保持张成的子空间不变。/ The goal of Gram-Schmidt is to turn the original basis into an orthogonal basis while preserving the same span.

基本步骤是逐步去掉与前面方向重叠的部分：/ The basic procedure is to remove the parts overlapping with previous directions:

$$
\vec{u}_1=\vec{w}_1.
$$

$$
\vec{u}_2
=
\vec{w}_2-\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{u}_1}\vec{w}_2.
$$

$$
\vec{u}_3
=
\vec{w}_3
-\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{u}_1}\vec{w}_3
-\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{u}_2}\vec{w}_3.
$$

一般地 / In general,

$$
\vec{u}_j
=
\vec{w}_j-\sum_{i=1}^{j-1}\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{u}_i}\vec{w}_j.
$$

得到正交基 $\{\vec{u}_1,\vec{u}_2,\dots\}$ 后，就可以使用子空间投影公式。/ After obtaining the orthogonal basis $\{\vec{u}_1,\vec{u}_2,\dots\}$, we can use the subspace projection formula.

#### 方法 2：最小二乘 / Method 2: Least Squares

最小二乘适合处理方程 $A\vec{x}=\vec{b}$ 没有精确解的情况。/ Least squares handles the case where the equation $A\vec{x}=\vec{b}$ has no exact solution.

换句话说，$\vec{b}$ 不在 $\operatorname{Col}(A)$ 里面，所以无法找到某个 $\vec{x}$ 让 $A\vec{x}$ 完全等于 $\vec{b}$。/ In other words, $\vec{b}$ is not in $\operatorname{Col}(A)$, so there is no vector $\vec{x}$ such that $A\vec{x}$ equals $\vec{b}$ exactly.

目标是找到让误差最小的 $\widehat{\vec{x}}$：/ The goal is to find the vector $\widehat{\vec{x}}$ that makes the error as small as possible:

$$
\widehat{\vec{x}}
=
\arg\min_{\vec{x}}\|A\vec{x}-\vec{b}\|.
$$

几何上，$A\vec{x}$ 一定位于 $A$ 的列空间中，所以我们是在找 $\vec{b}$ 到 $\operatorname{Col}(A)$ 的投影。/ Geometrically, $A\vec{x}$ always lies in the column space of $A$, so we are finding the projection of $\vec{b}$ onto $\operatorname{Col}(A)$.

也就是说 / That is,

$$
A\widehat{\vec{x}}=\operatorname{proj}_{\operatorname{Col}(A)}\vec{b}.
$$

这里的误差向量是 / The error vector is

$$
\vec{e}=\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}.
$$

因为 $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ 是投影，所以误差必须垂直于整个列空间。/ Since $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ is the projection, the error must be perpendicular to the entire column space.

由于 $A$ 的列向量张成 $\operatorname{Col}(A)$，误差与每一列都正交，等价于 / Since the columns of $A$ span $\operatorname{Col}(A)$, the error is orthogonal to every column, which is equivalent to

$$
A^T\vec{e}=\vec{0}.
$$

代入 $\vec{e}=\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}$，得到 / Substituting $\vec{e}=\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ gives

$$
A^T(\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}})=\vec{0}.
$$

最小二乘的核心结论是法方程：/ The central result of least squares is the normal equation:

$$
A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}.
$$

如果 $A$ 的列向量线性无关，那么 $A^TA$ 可逆，此时 / If the columns of $A$ are linearly independent, then $A^TA$ is invertible, and

$$
\widehat{\vec{x}}=(A^TA)^{-1}A^T\vec{b}.
$$

解出 $\widehat{\vec{x}}$ 后，$A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ 就是 $\vec{b}$ 在列空间中的最佳近似。/ After solving for $\widehat{\vec{x}}$, the vector $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ is the best approximation to $\vec{b}$ inside the column space.

##### 标准解题步骤 / Standard Procedure

1. 判断 $A\vec{x}=\vec{b}$ 是否可能无解；若无解，就改做 least squares。/ Decide whether $A\vec{x}=\vec{b}$ may be inconsistent; if so, switch to least squares.
2. 写出法方程 $A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}$。/ Write the normal equation $A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}$.
3. 计算 $A^TA$ 和 $A^T\vec{b}$。/ Compute $A^TA$ and $A^T\vec{b}$.
4. 解出 $\widehat{\vec{x}}$。/ Solve for $\widehat{\vec{x}}$.
5. 若题目问最佳近似向量，计算 $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$。/ If the problem asks for the best approximation vector, compute $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$.
6. 若题目问误差，计算 $\vec{e}=\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}$，并检查 $A^T\vec{e}=\vec{0}$。/ If the problem asks for the error, compute $\vec{e}=\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ and check $A^T\vec{e}=\vec{0}$.

##### 例 1：无解系统的最佳近似 / Example 1: Best Approximation for an Inconsistent System

考虑 / Consider

$$
A=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0\\
0&1\\
1&1
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\vec{b}=
\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
2\\
2
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

这里 $A\vec{x}$ 一定形如 $\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_1+x_2\end{bmatrix}$，但 $\vec{b}$ 要求同时满足 $x_1=1$、$x_2=2$、$x_1+x_2=2$，矛盾，所以没有精确解。/ Here $A\vec{x}$ must have the form $\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_1+x_2\end{bmatrix}$, but $\vec{b}$ would require $x_1=1$, $x_2=2$, and $x_1+x_2=2$ at the same time, which is contradictory, so there is no exact solution.

写法方程 / Write the normal equation:

$$
A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}.
$$

计算 / Compute

$$
A^TA=
\begin{bmatrix}
2&1\\
1&2
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A^T\vec{b}=
\begin{bmatrix}
3\\
4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

所以 / Therefore

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
2&1\\
1&2
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
\widehat{x}_1\\
\widehat{x}_2
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
3\\
4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

解得 / Solving gives

$$
\widehat{\vec{x}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{2}{3}\\
\frac{5}{3}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

最佳近似向量是 / The best approximation vector is

$$
A\widehat{\vec{x}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{2}{3}\\
\frac{5}{3}\\
\frac{7}{3}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

误差向量是 / The error vector is

$$
\vec{e}
=
\vec{b}-A\widehat{\vec{x}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{1}{3}\\
\frac{1}{3}\\
-\frac{1}{3}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

检查正交性 / Check orthogonality:

$$
A^T\vec{e}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&1\\
0&1&1
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{1}{3}\\
\frac{1}{3}\\
-\frac{1}{3}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
0\\
0
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

这说明误差确实垂直于 $\operatorname{Col}(A)$，所以 $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ 是最接近 $\vec{b}$ 的列空间向量。/ This shows that the error is perpendicular to $\operatorname{Col}(A)$, so $A\widehat{\vec{x}}$ is the closest column-space vector to $\vec{b}$.

##### 例 2：直线拟合 / Example 2: Fitting a Line

用直线 $y=c+mt$ 拟合三个点 / Fit the three points with the line $y=c+mt$:

$$
(0,1),\qquad(1,2),\qquad(2,2).
$$

把未知量写成 $\vec{x}=\begin{bmatrix}c\\m\end{bmatrix}$，得到 / Write the unknowns as $\vec{x}=\begin{bmatrix}c\\m\end{bmatrix}$, giving

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0\\
1&1\\
1&2
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
c\\
m
\end{bmatrix}
\approx
\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
2\\
2
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

这里 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}$，$\vec{b}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\2\end{bmatrix}$。/ Here $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}$ and $\vec{b}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\\2\end{bmatrix}$.

计算 / Compute

$$
A^TA=
\begin{bmatrix}
3&3\\
3&5
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A^T\vec{b}=
\begin{bmatrix}
5\\
6
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

法方程为 / The normal equation is

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
3&3\\
3&5
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
c\\
m
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
5\\
6
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

解得 / Solving gives

$$
m=\frac{1}{2},
\qquad
c=\frac{7}{6}.
$$

所以最小二乘拟合直线是 / Therefore the least-squares fitting line is

$$
y=\frac{7}{6}+\frac{1}{2}t.
$$

这个例子说明：当数据点不能全部落在同一条直线上时，least squares 给出的是让总误差最小的那条线。/ This example shows that when the data points do not all lie on one line, least squares gives the line with the smallest total error.

### 6. 整节课的结构 / Structure of the Lesson

一条直线的投影靠点积公式完成。/ Projection onto one line is computed with the dot product formula.

正交子空间的投影是把每个正交方向上的分量加起来。/ Projection onto an orthogonal subspace is found by adding the components along each orthogonal direction.

非正交基底不能直接套公式，需要先用 Gram-Schmidt 变成正交基，或者用 least squares 建立法方程。/ A non-orthogonal basis cannot be used directly in the projection formula; first convert it with Gram-Schmidt or set up the normal equation using least squares.

### 7. 一句话理解 / One-Sentence Summary

Projection = Best Approximation：投影就是在目标子空间里找到离原向量最近的向量。/ Projection means finding the closest vector to the original vector inside the target subspace.

### 8. 常见考试坑 / Common Exam Traps

1. 忘记子空间投影公式要求基底正交。/ Forgetting that the subspace projection formula requires an orthogonal basis.
2. 直接对非正交基底套分项投影公式。/ Applying the component projection formula directly to a non-orthogonal basis.
3. Gram-Schmidt 少减一项，导致结果没有真正正交。/ Missing a subtraction term in Gram-Schmidt, so the result is not actually orthogonal.
4. 忘记误差向量 $\vec{e}=\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}$ 必须正交于目标子空间。/ Forgetting that the error vector $\vec{e}=\vec{v}-\widehat{\vec{v}}$ must be orthogonal to the target subspace.
5. Least squares 忘写法方程 $A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}$。/ Forgetting to write the normal equation $A^TA\widehat{\vec{x}}=A^T\vec{b}$ for least squares.

### 9. 直觉记忆 / Intuition to Remember

投影像影子，误差像垂直落下的那一段，最小二乘就是让这段误差尽可能短。/ A projection is like a shadow, the error is the perpendicular drop, and least squares makes that error as short as possible.
